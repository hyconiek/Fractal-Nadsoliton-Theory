Date: 2026-11-04. Conditional source-model analysis.

## Main result

The actual physical source terms `c^2 z g^2 f`, `g4 z^3 f^3` and
`g6 z^5 f^5` have fixed exponentially localized spatial profiles.
Using their actual modal amplitudes and the existing weighted propagator,
their zero-data response has local field/velocity size O(epsilon^2) and
integrated squared compact trace O(epsilon^2) through time S epsilon^-2.
No slow-envelope derivative bound or carrier separation is needed.

The physical-to-QP position correction also contributes only O(epsilon^2)
to the integrated squared trace. The unpaid VD trace is consequently
reduced to the explicitly retained source BT2: the quadratic shape-mode
product cz and nonlinear terms containing continuum fields.

This pays a source-specific part of the gap exposed by CT. CT's sharp
O(epsilon) obstruction concerns a general unweighted forcing class; its
broad comparison profile is not one of these fixed localized sources.
SP's earlier z^3 component estimate is reused, not rediscovered.

## A shortcut that fails, and why the estimate still works

The mixed cubic carrier `2sqrt(3)-sqrt(w)` reaches the even continuum
threshold at the allowed parameter `w=4/3`. Its spatial profile has
nonzero threshold overlap. A calculation retaining both quadratic
cascades gives a nonzero imaginary dressed coefficient `6sqrt(3)d_b`.
Thus a uniform nonresonance premise for this two-mode branch is false.

These are source-jet/reference-response results, not a critical-time
expansion of the actual prepared trajectory. The physical shape starts
at zero and its canonical slip is only O(epsilon^2). Its persistence at
that smaller scale remains unproved on critical time.

The component estimate survives the threshold resonance: a localized
source of size epsilon^3 may grow like sqrt(t) while still meeting the
O(epsilon^2) integrated trace budget. The proof uses this admissible
growth instead of assuming the carrier stays away from threshold.
