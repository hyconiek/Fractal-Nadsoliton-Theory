<h2>1. Executive verdict</h2>
<div class="verdict"><span class="label">Verdict</span><strong>The unfinished report is not a scientifically defensible realization of the mandate and cannot be completed without replacing its central theorem.</strong> Its slogan “one measure, four transforms, nine shadows” turns a useful analogy into equality, conflates spectral measures of different operators, and relies on a finite “real spectral triple” that fails the standard first-order condition.</div>
<p class="lead">There is nevertheless a precise unification. On the frozen carrier ℤ<sub>12</sub>, the strict and legacy kernels are two independently selected real graph filters in the same seven-dimensional commutative operator algebra</p>
<div class="formula">𝒞<sub>12</sub><sup>rad</sup> = ℝ[L] = C*(L)<sub>sa</sub> ≅ ℝ<sup>7</sup>, &nbsp; K<sub>strict</sub> = p<sub>s</sub>(L), &nbsp; K<sub>legacy</sub> = p<sub>ℓ</sub>(L).</div>
<p>This is exact and is the maximal exact static unification. The spectral theorem supplies a common Fourier basis and functional calculus <em>after</em> the transfer functions p<sub>s</sub>, p<sub>ℓ</sub> have been chosen. It does not choose those functions, a Green parameter, contact subtraction, potential, drive, averaging rule, bootstrap map, information semantics, unit, or empirical observable.</p>
<div class="abstract"><span class="label">Deepest surviving interpretation</span>The repository is a <strong>finite commutative graph-filter algebra equipped with independently chosen variational, adaptive, feedback, and semantic decorations</strong>: a <strong>decorated adaptive graph-filter system</strong>. The static sector is standard operator theory and graph signal processing; the adaptive sector is a finite matrix gradient/feedback system. The combination may be distinctive, but the corpus does not establish a new unavoidable object or universal construction.</div>
<p>The obstruction is structural underdetermination: the same spectral carrier admits uncountably many filters, potentials, flows, feedback maps, and semantic readings. Later strict-core reports correctly detect special forms of the same boundary—no strict selector, canonical unit source, Born/readout interface, or reverse SM/GR source closure.</p>
</section>

<h3>3.1 Finite-resolvent counterexample</h3>
<p>Let L be the cycle Laplacian and P<sub>j</sub> its seven distinct spectral projectors. For z∉spec(L),</p>
<div class="formula">(L−zI)<sup>−1</sup> = Σ<sub>j=0</sub><sup>6</sup> P<sub>j</sub> /(μ<sub>j</sub>−z), &nbsp; μ<sub>j</sub>=2−2cos(2πj/12).</div>
<p>In the distributional boundary limit,</p>
<div class="formula">lim<sub>ε↓0</sub> Im(L−(E+iε)I)<sup>−1</sup> = π Σ<sub>j</sub> P<sub>j</sub> δ(E−μ<sub>j</sub>).</div>
<p>The distinct eigenvalues are 0, 2−√3, 1, 2, 3, 2+√3, 4. The legacy frequency ω=π/4 corresponds to E=2−2cos(π/4)=2−√2, not in this list. The boundary imaginary part is zero, not the nonzero legacy profile. Finite width, noninteger-mode interpolation, phase, damping, or a window can create a shell-like filter, but each is extra data. The common Green family is therefore a chosen regularized model/analogy, not inevitable identity.</p>
<h3>3.2 Fit is not identity</h3>
<p>The repository-wide referee report records A≈2.053, m²≈0.749, a contact term, and nRMSE 0.025 for the strict resolvent fit; it records much poorer one-pole fits for legacy. Nonzero residual cannot become equality “within resolution” in an exact inevitability question. <h3>3.3 First-order condition counterexample</h3>
<div class="theorem"><span class="label">Proposition 3.1</span>Let 𝒜=ℂ¹² act diagonally on ℋ=ℂ¹², J be componentwise conjugation, and D a real symmetric matrix with a nonzero off-diagonal entry. Then the standard real spectral-triple first-order condition fails.</div>
<p><strong>Proof.</strong> For diagonal a,b∈𝒜,</p>
<div class="formula">([[D,a],Jb*J<sup>−1</sup>])<sub>ij</sub> = D<sub>ij</sub>(a<sub>j</sub>−a<sub>i</sub>)(b<sub>j</sub>−b<sub>i</sub>).</div>
<p>Choose a=b=e<sub>i</sub>. For j≠i with D<sub>ij</sub>≠0, this entry equals D<sub>ij</sub>. Both FIN kernels have such entries, so first order fails. <span class="proof-end">□</span></p>
<p>The finite triple (𝒜,ℋ,D) is a valid basic odd complex spectral triple: finite dimensionality makes its resolvent compact and commutators bounded. A standard <em>real</em> triple needs another bimodule/real structure or an explicitly weakened definition. P3104 is more cautious: it constructs an interface using a diagonal algebra and square-root-Laplacian Dirac-like proxy; it does not prove the older D=K real-triple assertion.</p>
<h3>3.4 Variational and adaptive incompatibility</h3>
<p>For V(x)=∏<sub>r=0</sub><sup>6</sup>(x−λ<sub>r</sub>)², functional calculus gives V′(K<sub>s</sub>)=0. But ∇[tr V(K)−tr(KC)]=V′(K)−C before projection. Thus K<sub>s</sub> is stationary in the driven system only if ΠC=0, or if V is rebuilt to satisfy the projected equation. Correlation 0.9996 or nonzero terminal drift is proximity, not an exact fixed point. Also tr V(K) is constant on unitary conjugacy orbits, so without C it has flat basis directions, not an isolated matrix attractor.</p>
<p>The correct affine-flow necessity result is narrower than claimed: an affine vector field cannot have two <em>isolated asymptotically stable</em> equilibria. A singular affine field can have infinitely many equilibria along an affine subspace.</p>

<h3>4.1 Frozen kernels and kernel split</h3>
<p>On X=ℤ<sub>12</sub>, with cyclic distance d and zero diagonal, the audited profiles are</p>

This report proves a common algebraic home only—not the missing completion map, role-transfer theorem, selector source, or unit source.</p>
<h3>4.2 Radial-circulant algebra theorem</h3>
<p>Let S be cyclic shift, L=2I−S−S* the cycle Laplacian, and 𝒞<sub>12</sub><sup>rad</sup> the real symmetric circulants.</p>
<div class="theorem"><span class="label">Theorem 4.1 — maximal exact static unification</span>The algebra 𝒞<sub>12</sub><sup>rad</sup> is seven-dimensional and equals ℝ[L]. Every K in it has a unique real polynomial p of degree at most six such that K=p(L). Hence unique p<sub>s</sub>,p<sub>ℓ</sub> satisfy K<sub>strict</sub>=p<sub>s</sub>(L), K<sub>legacy</sub>=p<sub>ℓ</sub>(L).</div>
<p><strong>Proof.</strong> The discrete Fourier transform diagonalizes every circulant. Reality and symmetry identify modes m and 12−m, leaving seven independent spectral values. L has seven distinct values μ<sub>0</sub>,…,μ<sub>6</sub>. Lagrange interpolation gives a unique polynomial p of degree ≤6 with p(μ<sub>j</sub>)=κ<sub>j</sub>, where κ<sub>j</sub> are eigenvalues of K. Fourier conjugation yields K=p(L), and dimension equality gives 𝒞<sub>12</sub><sup>rad</sup>=ℝ[L]≅ℝ⁷. <span class="proof-end">□</span></p>
<div class="two-col"><div class="card"><h4>It proves</h4><ul><li>common Fourier eigenbasis;</li><li>exact common commutative *-algebra;</li><li>exact functional-calculus representations;</li><li>exact polynomial interrelation when a kernel separates all seven modes;</li><li>canonical resolvent/exponential after choosing an operator.</li></ul></div><div class="card"><h4>It does not prove</h4><ul><li>selection of either transfer function;</li><li>causal completion of one kernel by the other;</li><li>one low-parameter Green parent;</li><li>physical role transfer;</li><li>units, orientation, action, learning, or information semantics.</li></ul></div></div>
<p>Interpolation is classificatory, not explanatory. Arbitrary data on seven nodes admit it; the reported large derivative of the strict-to-legacy interfunction further shows it is not a robust low-complexity bridge.</p>
<h3>4.3 Which spectral measure?</h3>
<p>The PVM of L determines L, but K<sub>s</sub> and K<sub>ℓ</sub> only after p<sub>s</sub>,p<sub>ℓ</sub> are supplied. The PVM of K<sub>s</sub> determines that operator, not the independent legacy profile, action, or feedback rule. The spectral theorem is a representation theorem</p>
<div class="formula">f(D)=∫f(λ)dE<sub>D</sub>(λ).</div>
<p>Its hypothesis includes f. It is not a selection principle for f.</p>

<h2>9. Inverse physics test</h2>
<p>The correct question is not whether FIN can be fitted to known constants, but what extra mathematics would make any output an experimental proposition.</p>
<div class="theorem"><span class="label">Proposition 9.1 — current non-testability</span>The present kernel algebra does not define an experimentally testable physical theory because it lacks a typed preparation-to-observation map and statistical comparison rule. This remains true even if every internal operator identity is accepted.</div>
<p><strong>Proof.</strong> A self-adjoint matrix and its unitary exponential specify internal trajectories only after a state and time parameter are chosen. They do not identify a laboratory system, preparation, measured variable, calibration, or outcome probabilities. Incompatible empirical interpretations can share all FIN equations, so no empirical proposition follows from those equations alone. <span class="proof-end">□</span></p>
<h3>9.1 Smallest missing bridge</h3>
<p>The bridge is an <strong>empirical interpretation and measurement package</strong>: a typed map from preparations and FIN states to probability distributions on calibrated outcomes,</p>
<div class="formula">(preparation,θ,t) ↦ ρ<sub>θ,t</sub> ↦ P<sub>θ</sub>(Y∈· | preparation,t),</div>
<p>plus an independently fixed comparison protocol. Minimum additions are:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Domain/state axiom:</strong> identify a physical system and admissible preparations with normalized mathematical states.</li>
<li><strong>Evolution axiom:</strong> select strict or legacy layer, initial/boundary conditions, and the parameter corresponding to time/control.</li>
<li><strong>Observable/readout axiom:</strong> map a named operator/functional to calibrated outcomes. P3089 records the current Born/readout obstruction.</li>
<li><strong>Statistical axiom:</strong> outcome probabilities/noise, identifiability, and likelihood or another falsification statistic.</li>
<li><strong>Unit axiom(s):</strong> one external scale for one dimensionful axis; a full length–time–action bridge needs dimensional rank three or equivalent constants, as P3146 shows.</li>
<li><strong>Selector axiom when needed:</strong> branch/orientation predictions require the conditional A<sub>origin</sub>+A<sub>λ</sub>+A<sub>coupling</sub> package of P3140 or a strict source discharging QW-2191.</li>
<li><strong>Held-out prediction:</strong> preregister a dimensionless or calibrated distribution not used to select the kernel/bridge parameters.</li>
</ol>
<p>P3145 has ten receiver slots but zero source closures. P3168/P3170 find no strict unit source in current classes. These bounded results agree with the general measurement obstruction.</p>
<h3>9.2 Is the bridge impossible?</h3>
<p><strong>No.</strong> A finite operator system can become a physical model after external empirical interpretation. What cannot be derived solely from spectral data is that interpretation: semantic identification and calibration are not matrix invariants. The shortest route is</p>
<pre>freeze one kernel layer → specify preparation/evolution → define one calibrated observable
→ give probability/noise law → fix required units/selector → preregister a held-out prediction
→ compare with data and report uncertainty</pre>
<p>This narrow test is shorter and stronger than attempting to reconstruct all established physics at once.</p>
