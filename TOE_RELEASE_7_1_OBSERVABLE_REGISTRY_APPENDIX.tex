\section{Reading Rule}

Release 7.1 separates three layers:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.]
  \item the current minimal strict bridge admitted by \texttt{F784/F801},
  \item the broader frozen observable package from \texttt{QW-2069} and its source gates,
  \item explicitly excluded non-strict interfaces such as \texttt{P704} host matching and \texttt{P710} proxy-to-GeV calibration.
\end{enumerate}

A broad historical strict package entry is therefore not automatically an
admitted FAR bridge entry. Dimensionful host-side observables, shared-basis
flavor objects, SI-definition constants, and external-input cosmology channels
are documented here, but they are not silently promoted into the minimal strict
bridge.

The current 7.1 reading also keeps the \texttt{P786} warning explicit: the
present \texttt{QW-2093} formula layer for \texttt{sin2\_theta\_w\_eff} and
\texttt{alpha\_s\_boundary\_mu0\_alpha0} is not yet upgraded to a
kernel-invariants-only layer. Release 7.1 therefore documents these objects
with their actual blockers rather than overstating them.

  \item Do not read the mass-chain, flavor-basis, or GeV-level EW/Higgs outputs as strict proxy-to-GeV calibration or Standard Model identification. Those layers remain separated from the minimal bridge.
  \item The explicitly excluded non-strict interfaces remain \texttt{P704} host matching and \texttt{P710} proxy-to-GeV calibration. They are documented in this TeX, but they are not part of the strict bridge claim.
\end{itemize}
