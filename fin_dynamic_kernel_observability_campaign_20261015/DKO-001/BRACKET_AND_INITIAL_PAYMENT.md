# DKO-001 — Initial and bracket payment ledger

1. **Original generator:** exact slot clock=1, denominator N, q_l(n−e_j); null refreshes included only as unobserved attempts, state jumps count when l≠j.
2. **Exact global/private jump:** (ΔF_r,ΔU_r)=(1,c_a)Δf_r/√N. Cross-coordinate brackets use products Δf_rΔf_s, never parity's ±2 shortcut.
3. **Mean-field soft cancellation:** on p_m the transverse f_r mean is exactly zero; derivative in fast direction at m=0 has rate −γ_r; O(m) fast-linearization is negligible because m=O_P(N^-1/4).
4. **Private residual:** q(n−e_j)−q(n)=O(1/N), uniformly, producing L U=−U+O_α(N^-1/2).
5. **Bracket integrals:** E|p−u|→0 by quartic/fast MT moments, E|p_B−p|→0 by conditional hypergeometric moments, stationary reflection and finite T. This yields FF→2I, UU→2cI and FU→0; mixed Fourier coordinates vanish.
6. **Initial product structure:** paid by MT conditional Gibbs and KO joint block CLT, not by assuming independent finite-N tags.
7. **Soft freezing:** critical Z has on fixed fast T a drift and quadratic characteristic tending to zero; it remains a random constant Z(0) in the limit.
8. **Transfer:** reflected labelled process couples to actual parent until genuine exit; outside, failure to mix and exit remain explicit o(1) probabilities, with no postselection.
9. **Open dependencies:** source MT/KO proof completeness is imported via user-approved intake, not re-proved; no numerical N0; no mechanical verification of path tightness.
