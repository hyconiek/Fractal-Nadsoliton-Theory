# DKO-002 — Scalar observed memory is not white-noise Markov reduction

For γ≠1 and c>0, C(t)=γ^-1 exp(−γ|t|)+c exp(−|t|). A stationary nondegenerate scalar Gaussian Markov process with continuous paths has exponential conditional covariance: C(2h)=C(h)^2/C(0), which fails for a strictly positive sum of two distinct exponentials (strict positive variance of the exponential rates under positive weights). The pair (F,U), not V alone, is Markov. The observed V retains classical colored memory; it neither establishes fundamental non-Markov spacetime dynamics nor a physical stored record.

At γ_1=1, both exponentials collapse, so the **null Fourier mode** V_1 is itself an OU with correlation exp(−|t|), regardless of α. This is the internal clock-mode control, not an independently calibrated physical second. The soft Z_0 remains random and frozen on fast O(1) model time; setting it to zero would change the preparation scope.
