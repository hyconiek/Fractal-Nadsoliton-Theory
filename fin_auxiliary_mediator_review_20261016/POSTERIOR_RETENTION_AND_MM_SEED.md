## 1. Full posterior creates extra self-retention

Fix a selected old label i, cavity r=n-e_i, and p_i(theta)=softmax_i(X theta).
By Bayes under the exact MP7 joint law,

    rho_n(dtheta)=nu_r(dtheta) p_i(theta)/q_i(r),
    q_i(r)=E_nu_r p_i(theta)=native LOO probability of repeating i.

Therefore

    E_rho_n p_i(theta)-q_i(r)
          =Var_nu_r(p_i(theta))/q_i(r)>0.

The strict inequality holds for finite N,g>0: nu_r has full support and p_i
is nonconstant for the supplied A7. This proves more than the source's
descriptive caveat that full posterior and cavity densities differ.

For the **unreflected** native model, let R_native(n) be its total count-change
