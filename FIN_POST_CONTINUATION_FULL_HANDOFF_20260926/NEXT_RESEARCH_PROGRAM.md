Prove or refute the missing full-7D phase inequality for the sign-robust functional `rho_crit(phi)`. Acceptance: an interval/SOS/analytic proof that no non-co-phased probe exceeds the certified co-phased optimum, or an explicit counterexample. ## P0 — GEOMETRY-M128 / ASYMPTOTIC DEPTH
Extend the exact convolution/FFT producer to M=128 if computationally feasible. Track peak location, C_H, root fraction and full depth tail. The main falsification target is whether the root deficit continues to decay and whether the apparent geometric internal tail persists. No exponent from M<=64 alone.
