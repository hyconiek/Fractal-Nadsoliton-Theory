# TREE-DEGREE2-EQUIVALENCE-01

Status: **LIMITED COUNTEREXAMPLE + MINIMAL TWO-PORT CLASSIFICATION**.

A positive-storage degree-2 split is generally dynamically observable. Static effective conductance and pole positions alone do not identify the realization; matrix residues are necessary.

Explicit same-static/same-pole counterexample:
`(g1,g2,c)=(1,1,2)` and `((3+sqrt(3))/2,(3-sqrt(3))/2,3)` share `G_eq=0.5` and pole 1 but have different residues/full dynamic responses.

This initial classification is superseded by the constructive maximal-path Cauer uniqueness theorem in `TREE-DEGREE2-EQUIVALENCE-02`.
