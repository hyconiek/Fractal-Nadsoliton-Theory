# FINITE-N-REFRESH-INVARIANT-16 — exact reversible law of the empirical refresh generator

Status: **EXACT_THEOREM_FOR_DECLARED_REFRESH**.

Consider the count process on `Q=12` labels with `p=n/N`, symmetric strict
`A=A7=X X^T`, constant diagonal, and rates

    n -> n-e_i+e_j    at rate n_i q_j(p),
    q_j(p)=exp(g(Ap)_j)/Z_g(p),
    Z_g(p)=sum_l exp(g(Ap)_l).

Then the exact finite-N invariant law is

    pi_N(n) proportional to
      [N!/prod_i n_i!] exp[(N g/2) p^T A p] Z_g(p).             (1)

Moreover the chain is reversible with respect to (1).

## Detailed-balance proof

For `n'=n-e_i+e_j`,

    C(n')/C(n)=n_i/(n_j+1).

Symmetry and the constant diagonal give

    (Ng/2)[p'^T A p'-p^T A p]
      = g[(Ap)_j-(Ap)_i] + (g/N)(A_ii-A_ij).

Also

    (Ap')_i=(Ap)_i+(A_ij-A_ii)/N.

Therefore

    pi(n')/pi(n)
      = [n_i/(n_j+1)]
        exp[g(Ap)_j-g(Ap')_i]
        Z_g(p')/Z_g(p)
      = [n_i q_j(p)] / [(n_j+1) q_i(p')].

This is exact detailed balance.

## Relation to the large-deviation potential

The extra factor `Z_g(p)` is only `O(1)` in `log pi_N`; therefore the N-speed
rate function remains

    D(p||u) - (g/2) p^T A p

(up to its normalization constant).  This explains why the earlier mean-field
quasipotential is compatible with the exact finite-N refresh law while not being
the complete finite-N stationary weight.

## Leave-one-out Gibbs heat-bath as a distinct finite-N convention

If one instead refreshes a selected copy from the conditional field generated by
the other `N-1` copies,

    q_{j|i}^{LOO}(p) proportional to
      exp[g(Ap)_j-(g/N)A_{ji}],

then the exact invariant law loses the `Z_g(p)` factor and becomes the pure
mean-field Gibbs count weight

    C(n) exp[(Ng/2)p^T A p].

The two rules have the same deterministic large-N drift but differ at finite N.
Thus the finite-N activity convention remains a real modeling premise.

## Boundary

This theorem establishes exact reversibility for the declared empirical-refresh
chain.  It does not source the heat-bath rule, its clock, `g`, or physical time.
