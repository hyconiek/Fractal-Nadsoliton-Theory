  theorem-prover verification or peer review.

  Hash and numerical replay do not attest remote HEAD, custody or stochastic proof.

  tied to the stated g0 clock; they are not measurements or natural constants.

  A7, rank7, a physical kinetic rule or reality by itself.

  convergence is admitted.

  splitting/finite-N parity lumpability/autonomous-bit claims remain rejected.

  premises, not a derived origin of the universe or natural critical tuning.

  particles/QM/GR/SM, strict selector/legacy roles, L_total or ToE is exported.
