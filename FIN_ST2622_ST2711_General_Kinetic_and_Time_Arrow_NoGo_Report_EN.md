# FIN ST2622–ST2711 — General Kinetic and Time-Arrow No-Go

## Main theorem

For a local rate depending only on the energy change \(\Delta\), detailed
balance is

\[
\frac{r(\Delta)}{r(-\Delta)}=e^{-\Delta}.
\]

Every positive solution is

\[
\boxed{r(\Delta)=e^{-\Delta/2}h(\Delta),}
\]

where \(h\) is an arbitrary positive even function. Analyticity, positivity
and locality still leave infinitely many choices.

Heat-bath, square-root and Metropolis rules become unique only after different
extra normalizations are imposed. None of these normalizations is strict-derived.

## Nonreversible extension

Every stationary edge flow decomposes into:

1. a symmetric reversible conductance,
2. a divergence-free antisymmetric circulation.

The 12-dimensional hypercube has cycle-space dimension

\[
24576-4096+1=\boxed{20481}.

- exactly the same Gibbs stationary law,

Therefore neither the equilibrium state nor entropy-production magnitude
selects the orientation of time.

## Final interpretation

FIN equilibrium information determines neither kinetics nor a time arrow.
Reversible dynamics needs an activity/mobility and clock. Nonreversible
dynamics additionally needs a nonequilibrium circulation and its polarity.
These cannot be reconstructed from the stationary Gibbs law, the pair kernel,
or entropy production alone.

The highest-value next object is a strict nonequilibrium resource/clock law.
Without it, the kinetic impossibility theorem should be preserved rather than
repackaged as emergent time.

No selector, physical clock, units, apparatus, laboratory evidence, Standard
Model, gravity, \(L_{\rm total}\), or Theory of Everything closure follows.
