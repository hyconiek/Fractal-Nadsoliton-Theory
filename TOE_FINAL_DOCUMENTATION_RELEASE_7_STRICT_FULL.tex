\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Because $s_m(-x)=-s_m(x)$, we may pair terms $x$ and $-x$:
\[
w(x)s_m(x)+w(-x)s_m(-x)=w(x)s_m(x)-w(-x)s_m(x)=(w(x)-w(-x))s_m(x)=0.
\]
The fixed points $x=0$ and $x=6$ satisfy $s_m(0)=s_m(6)=0$, so they contribute nothing. Hence the total sum vanishes.
For the Aut-invariance statement, apply the automorphism $x\mapsto -x$ and invariance of $w$ under $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}_{12})$.
\end{proof}
