Consequently heat, unitary, wave, Green, unnormalized Boltzmann, and other functional-calculus shadows are exactly identical in the coarse sector for infinitely many inequivalent fine generators. This proves that a rigorous projection architecture exists inside the declared FIN refinement model and states its normalization boundary. It simultaneously proves nonidentifiability for records restricted and conditioned inside the coarse sector.

The release does **not** claim that the universe is a FIN projection. It proves mathematical compatibility and exact finite nonuniqueness, while preserving the absence of a physical projection theorem, QW-2191 closure, dimensional source, laboratory evidence, Standard Model, gravity, `L_total`, or Theory-of-Everything closure.
