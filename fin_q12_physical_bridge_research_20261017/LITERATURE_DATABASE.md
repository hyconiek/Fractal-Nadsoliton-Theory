Date: 2026-10-17. Five primary works inspected in the portions listed below,
not an exhaustive review or five FIN theorem applications. The existing
fifteen-work theory bibliography is reused as historical context, not
silently counted as newly read. No complete copyrighted PDF is tracked here.

## PH-L01 — Plefka, 2003

[Dynamics of Ising models coupled microscopically to bath systems](https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0308172).
Read PDF setup, Markov approximation, equations (9)--(17), and discussion of
configuration-dependent relaxation. Spin Hamiltonian, bath response and
scale separation are supplied; rates retain bath susceptibility. This is
a concrete source model to compare with, not derivation from FIN ontology.
It warns against treating equilibrium agreement as kinetic agreement.
The q2 calculation alone cannot provide a q12 reset normalizer.

## PH-L02 — Veszeli and Vattay, 2022

[Relaxation of the Ising spin system coupled to a bosonic bath and the time dependent mean field equation](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0264412).
Read Section 2 through actual browser HTML; equations checked in the
printable PDF through the web fallback. Born/Markov/secular assumptions,
local coupling operators and bath-correlation spectrum precede the
population-rate formula. Ohmic and super-Ohmic zero-gap behaviour differs.
This licenses a restricted compatibility test, not import of a physical
bath into FIN or universal exclusion of all Davies dynamics.

## PH-L03 — Strasberg, Schaller, Brandes and Esposito, 2017

[Quantum and Information Thermodynamics: A Unifying Framework based on Repeated Interactions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1610.01829v3).
First preprint 2016; v3 dated 10 March 2017, PRX 7, 021003.
Read Section IV.A, equations (81)--(98), setup and thermodynamic bookkeeping.
Fresh units, their states, interactions and waiting times are resources.
The Poisson limit gives a reset-channel generator, but neither the clock
nor unit preparation is derived merely by writing it. This is useful for
auditing an engineered FIN realization, not an independent natural source.

## PH-L04 — Mourrat and Weber, 2014/2015 preprint

[Convergence of the two-dimensional dynamic Ising-Kac model to Phi4_2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1410.1179v2).
Read Section 2's model and complete Theorem 2.1 statement. It assumes a
two-dimensional torus, a nonnegative spatial Kac kernel, specified scaling,
temperature renormalization and controlled initial data. FIN supplies none
of those spatial objects by exchangeable tagging. This is a precise model
of how a spatial field limit can be proved **after** incidence is provided,
not an emergent-geometry theorem applicable to A7 by analogy. Full proof
of the 78-page work was not audited here.

## PH-L05 — Chen, Kastoryano, Brandao and Gilyen, 2025

[Efficient quantum thermal simulation](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09583-x).
Read actual browser HTML: inputs, full Theorem 1, Methods' Davies comparison
and equations (9)--(17). It assumes a quantum Hamiltonian, jump operators
and simulation operations; geometric locality is conditional on a supplied
local Hamiltonian. It constructs a sampler, not nature's operator or its
couplings. Its jump operators are not the FIN interaction matrix A7.
The related three-author arXiv:2311.09207v2 was checked as a distinct record,
not falsely equated byte-for-byte with this four-author article.
