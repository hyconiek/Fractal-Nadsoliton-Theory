# Hidden-state dependence of visible finite-N noise

Status: **EXACT_FORMULA_PLUS_NUMERICAL_WITNESS**

## Result
Heat-bath visible drift depends only on mu, but the finite-N visible diffusion
matrix depends on full p. The PHY-004 fiber witness p+ and p- has identical
mu=0 yet different visible diffusion matrices, proving non-lumpability at the
fluctuation level.

## Key formulas
\[D_\mu(p)=X^T[\operatorname{diag}p+\operatorname{diag}q-pq^T-qp^T]X.\]

## Caveat
Effect vanishes from deterministic drift but not finite-N/path statistics.
