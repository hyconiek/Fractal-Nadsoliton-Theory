# SOFT-LEAK — Gaussianity discriminator

Status: **EXACT_GAUSSIAN_IDENTITY_PLUS_CUMULANT_TEST**

## Result
Under a quadratic soft constraint, Gaussian leakage satisfies an exact
matrix line S_tau^-1=S^-1+tau I. For non-Gaussian laws the initial slope receives
a contracted fourth-cumulant correction. This turns soft leakage into a direct
test of Gaussian parent structure.

## Key formulas
\[S_\tau^{-1}=S^{-1}+\tau I\quad(\text{Gaussian}).\]
\[\left.\frac d{d\tau}S_\tau^{-1}\right|_0=I+\frac12S^{-1}KS^{-1}.\]
