# Hierarchy topology stability certificate

Status: **EXACT_BOUND_PLUS_NUMERICAL_SCOUT**

## Result
Because each hierarchy energy is quadratic in leaf states, the energy gap to
a competitor admits a direct perturbation bound. This gives a certified ball
inside which the selected topology cannot change; the radius vanishes at tree
crossings.

## Key formulas
\[\Delta E_H(X+\delta X)\ge\Delta E_H(X)-2\|Q_HX\|_F\|\delta X\|_F-\|Q_H\|_2\|\delta X\|_F^2.\]

## Caveat
Radius values are numerical scouts; bound itself is algebraic.
