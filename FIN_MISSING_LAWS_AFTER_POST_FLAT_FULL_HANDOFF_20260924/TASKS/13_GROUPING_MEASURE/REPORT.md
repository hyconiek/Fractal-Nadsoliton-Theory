# Recursive grouping supplies measure, not conductance

Status: **CORRECTION_PLUS_EXACT_SCALING**

## Result
Equal b-ary grouping gives mass/measure m_d=b^-d. Treating this directly as
conductance yields a zero-order variance operator, not a Laplacian. Combining
measure with independently motivated second-order transport gives the proper
conductance scaling.

## Key formulas
\[m_d=b^{-d},\quad \ell_d=r^{-d},\quad c_d\propto m_d/\ell_d^2=(r^2/b)^d.\]

## Caveat
Calling b^-d a conductance was an earlier overinterpretation.
