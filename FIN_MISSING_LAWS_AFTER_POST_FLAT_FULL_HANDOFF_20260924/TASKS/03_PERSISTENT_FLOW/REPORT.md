## Result
A persistent spatial current can produce telegraph dynamics, finite
characteristic speed and a diffusive long-scale limit. However beta>0 dissipates
energy, beta=0 removes diffusion, and internal carrier flow is not the same
object as spatial current.

## Key formulas
\[\partial_t h+\partial_xJ=0,\quad \tau\partial_tJ+\beta J=-D\partial_xh\]
\[\tau h_{tt}+\beta h_t=D h_{xx}.\]

## Caveat
No physical clock, tau, beta or spatial incidence operator is sourced.
