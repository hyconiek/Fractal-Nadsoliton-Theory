# Metropolis, Barker and heat-bath selectors

Status: **NO_UNIQUE_NATURAL_GENERATOR**

## Result
Three natural extra axioms select three different reversible dynamics:
maximal acceptance -> Metropolis; forward/reverse complementarity -> Barker;
complete conditional refresh -> heat-bath. Naturalness alone therefore does not
close dynamics.

\[a(r)=r a(1/r),\quad a_M=\min(1,r),\quad a_B=r/(1+r).\]
