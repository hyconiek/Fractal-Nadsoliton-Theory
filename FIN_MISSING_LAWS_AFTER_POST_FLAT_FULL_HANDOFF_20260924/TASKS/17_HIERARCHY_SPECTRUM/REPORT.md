# Hierarchy Haar spectrum and r identifiability

Status: **EXACT_FOR_DECLARED_TREE_OPERATOR**

## Result
The scale-weighted balanced-tree quadratic form is diagonal in a normalized
Haar basis. Level d has eigenvalue proportional to r^(2d), making r directly
identifiable from dimensionless spectral ratios. The heat trace obeys an exact
discrete scaling relation.

## Key formulas
\[\lambda_d\propto r^{2d},\quad r=\sqrt{\lambda_{d+1}/\lambda_d}.\]
\[K(t)=\sum_{d\ge0}2^de^{-tr^{2d}},\quad 2K(r^2t)=K(t)-e^{-t}.\]

## Caveat
Identifiability does not equal source selection.
