Derive the complete first Edgeworth/Kramers-Moyal correction after eliminating
the four hidden modes. Determine whether it is representable as one compact
history-dependent operator and which observables detect it most cleanly.

## 2. REACTION-COORD-03

heat-bath effective mobility `m_eff(s)`. Test all 12 symmetry-related localized
transitions and quantify the worst reduction error.

## 3. GEOMETRY-ORDER-01
Study large-M hierarchy ordering for ensembles drawn from the finite localized
phase alphabet. Use exact orbit/Burnside counts or count-vector bounds rather
than labeled-tree entropy. Measure scaling of the energy gap per leaf.

## 4. R-SOURCE-03

with the proved monotone hierarchy energy. Do not guess r from eta or from a
desired dimension.

## 5. LOCALITY-BRIDGE-01

incidence/current operator and test whether PM-001/second-order transport is
closed under refinement.
