the actual MT domain/conditional Gibbs law and the admitted NT strong core.

    b2(p)=-p log p-(1-p)log(1-p), b2(0)=b2(1)=0.

the observed bit need not be. The limiting signal is the admitted

exp(-z^4/12). Define c(t)=E_rho b2(S(t)1{z>0}).

map into critical time. For example on N=144l^2, integer-power scaling
