# Nonconclusions

- Primary FIN is **still** heat-bath at unit per-copy attempt rate; MET/BAR are diagnostic, not new FIN axioms.
- Equality of the static Gibbs measure does not equal equality of finite-time trajectories or raw clocks.
- Canonicalized class-level exit statistics do not source natural A7, derive the true microscopic kinetics, or identify physical seconds.
- The local stopped limit is not a global mixing, capacity, Eyring-Kramers or B4 barrier theorem; no finite-N TV rate or mean residence-time convergence is proven.
- Rounded prepared starts are not an arbitrary physical sample or the full unconditioned Gibbs measure.
- A symmetry of the finite A7 model is not physical space, conserved energy, QM, GR, SM or ToE.
- Numerical diagnostics and source-fold-interval dependence are not formal proof-machine certification.
