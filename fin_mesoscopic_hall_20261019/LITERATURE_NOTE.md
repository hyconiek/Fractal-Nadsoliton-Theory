Jingwei Guo, [On lattice points in large convex bodies](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.4284),
arXiv:1007.4284v1, 24 July 2010.
[Official PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1007.4284).
Read pages 1--4 (classical exponent, Theorem 1.1 and stationary-phase
Lemma 2.1) and pages 21--23 (Section 4's smoothing/Poisson setup).
This is partial primary reading, not a claim to have checked the whole paper.
Its stronger unweighted theorem for dimension at least three is NOT applied
to FIN's weighted changing measures or its two-dimensional phase disk.
The needed weaker translation-uniform weighted ball bound is proved directly
in this package; its general Poisson method is not new.
