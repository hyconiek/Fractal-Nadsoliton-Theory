# SHELL is paid for the frozen radius

Date: 2026-10-20. Uniformly over adjacent compact fibres, N in12N.

Put h=N^-1/2, L=1/10 and consider the RIGHT annulus
L-h/8<||Y||<=L. Both radii stay in [L/2,2L] eventually. Subtract the
two actual weighted-ball estimates MH-W(4). Their errors are O(h^(20/11)),
not an unproved error of order h. On the fixed annulus the continuous
density a_N is uniformly bounded below, and its volume is at least c_L h.
Consequently

    6h^10 W(annulus)/W_N(m_right,0)>=c h             (1)

eventually, with c>0 uniform in the compact soft band and translations.
MH-W(5) bounds the phase-A denominator above and away from zero, so

    W(annulus)/W_(A,right)>=c' h.                   (2)

The annulus is in phase A and outside E. Its UNCORRECTED incoming mass
is J_(A,l) times the ratio in (2). The exact departure redistribution
has total variation at most 2N W(E_right intersect A), negligible relative
to h J_(A,l) by the actual compact carry/slice bounds and exponential E
mass. It is charged, not discarded. Hence for some fixed c_*>0,

    I'_(l+1)(annulus)>=c_* N^-1/2 J_(A,l).          (SHELL)

This is an eventual uniform theorem with unknown practical N0 and c_*.
It does not assert every thin region on an arbitrary count domain has
positive lattice mass. The positive-curvature ball and the weighted
Poisson error strictly below its width are essential.
