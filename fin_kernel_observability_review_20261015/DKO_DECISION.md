share a limit, while a separately declared half-population fast snapshot
distinguishes the matched kernels. More event digits or a larger sweep is
not the next default. Neither does this small ideal gap license a physical
discrimination claim without preparation/readout/source evidence.

The selected new question is temporal and resource-sensitive:

> Can the same microscopic law yield a reusable dynamic kernel signature,
> independent of a common clock-unit choice, and how much observation is
> needed to resolve it?

This activates a **newly scoped use** of the deferred fast-path gate. First
derive the joint global/private process instead of assuming a Gaussian
snapshot determines dynamics. Then derive a bounded two-time signature and
only afterwards a Gaussian-family sample-fraction/time benchmark.

Start DKO-001–003. The resource task must keep N-first fixed-window limits
separate from shrinking observation fractions or growing record lengths.
An information formula for the limit model is not a finite-N laboratory cost.

This is one clearly identified predictive/statistical bridge, not a
promise of fundamental physics. Source selection, physical units, incidence/
locality, conservation and empirical mapping remain distinct missing inputs.
No graph, detector, storage medium or new microscopic noise is added to avoid
those gaps. Empirical promotion remains stopped without independent evidence.
