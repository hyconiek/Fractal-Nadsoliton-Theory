## Abstract

Release 10.95 reports six local analytical and computational research rounds,
ST1002–ST1091, correcting the mathematical type of FIN's annihilation
question. In the ontology studied here, one nadsoliton is the complete
fundamental being, not a localized excitation. The report therefore separates
the total state, internal patterns, a physical vacuum, and literal
nonexistence.

The principal result is a conditional annihilation no-go. If
\(\mathcal M_{\rm tot}\) is a nonempty state space for the complete
nadsoliton, \(\dagger\notin\mathcal M_{\rm tot}\) denotes nonexistence, and
\(\Phi_t:\mathcal M_{\rm tot}\to\mathcal M_{\rm tot}\) is a globally defined
invariant evolution, then no internal trajectory reaches \(\dagger\). For a
positive conserved normalization \(Q\),

\[
Q(\Phi_t x)=Q(x)>0,\qquad Q(0)=0,
\]

so zero is also unreachable. This is a structural theorem, not a new physical
force and not a proof of eternal physical existence.

For the frozen strict operator,

\[
U_t=e^{-itA},\qquad P_t=e^{-tA},
\]

the unitary channel preserves Hilbert norm, while the heat channel preserves
total mass but erases internal contrast at the measured spectral gap
\(\lambda_1=0.7541211542070798\). Thus the total normalized carrier can persist
while internal patterns, records or accessible information disappear.

The report also proves that fixed-point/bootstrap existence does not imply
stability, rejects an external pump as a fundamental explanation for a being
defined as the whole, constructs a compatible internal zero-sum circulation
model, and establishes that total annihilation cannot leave a post-event
record for an observer internal to the total state. Six countermodel classes
show precisely which premises are indispensable. The remaining central task
is a noncircular derivation of the total-state manifold and a globally complete
invariant full dynamics from the FIN kernel.
