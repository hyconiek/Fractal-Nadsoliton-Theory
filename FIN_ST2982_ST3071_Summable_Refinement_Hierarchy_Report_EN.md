# FIN ST2982–ST3071 — Summable Refinement Hierarchy

## Sharp positive-coupling theorem

For the exact prism map and a sequence \(q_n\ge0\), define

\[
r_n=\tanh^3q_n.
\]

For every finite positive initial \(\theta_0\),

\[
\boxed{
\theta_n\text{ has a finite nonzero limit}
\iff
\sum_nr_n<\infty.}
\]

If the series diverges, \(|\theta_n|\to\infty\). A finite number of large
early couplings does not change the criterion.

## Negative hierarchy

For \(q_n\le0\), divergence of

\[
\sum_n|\tanh^3q_n|

\]

drives \(\theta_n\to0\). Absolute summability leaves a finite nonzero limit
with the original sign. Mixed-sign conditionally convergent sequences depend
on their ordering.

## Sharp decay threshold

For

\[
q_n=Cn^{-\alpha},

\]

\[
\boxed{\alpha>\frac13}

\]

Every dyadic/exponential law

\[
q_n=q_0,2^{-\alpha n},\qquad\alpha>0,

\]
