The new standard-library checker validates exact softmax-weight identities,
affine-query word patterns with ties, initial-branch union accounting,
singular-support controls, and adaptive/nonlinear exceptions. It separately
runs BI's existing checker with --check and isolated /tmp output, not its
writer. The 36 BI flags are dependency replay, not new LS assertions.

Published Wilkie/VC-density theorems and their application hypotheses are
analytical inputs. Small fixtures do not prove them, compute C or certify
native asymptotic probabilities. No numerical source simulation substitutes
for the proof. No external package or PDF/browser cache is needed for replay.

--record authors metadata only in this new directory; --check compares it
read-only except its optional /tmp output. Old manifests/current inputs are
rechecked after execution. An absent PDF cache is ABSENT_OPTIONAL_CACHE,
not a missing FIN certificate or reconstructed historical download.
