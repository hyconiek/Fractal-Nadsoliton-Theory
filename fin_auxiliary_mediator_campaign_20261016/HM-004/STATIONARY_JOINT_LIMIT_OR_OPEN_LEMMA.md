# HM-004 stationary reflected path-limit argument

Start Π_D,N=π_D,Nρ_n and κ fixed. Let s=(κm+h)/(κ+1) and Z_N(u)=N^1/4 s(√N u). For the exact generator the drift is rκ[φ(θ)−h+(g_N/gc−1)m]. On the parity line φ=tanh h. Transverse θ has mean-square O(N^-1) under the stationary joint law, and symmetry kills the first transverse derivative at every h. Therefore φ=tanh h+O_L1(N^-1). The h−s gap is O_L2(N^-1/2). Taylor remainder after critical rescaling vanishes by the MT polynomial moments. The scaled bracket converges in stationary L1 to 2rκ; maximal jumps vanish. The MT local-Gibbs initial law converges to ρ_lambda. Standard martingale tightness and uniqueness then identify dZ=rκ(chi*lambda*Z−Z³/3)du+sqrt(2rκ)dB.
