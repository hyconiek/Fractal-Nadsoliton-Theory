# HM-002 reversibility and domains

At fixed θ the labelled categorical generator is reversible under the product conditional Π(x|θ). At fixed labels the OU part is reversible for ρ_n. Their sum is reversible for Π; reflecting count jumps by rejection preserves Π_D. For count parity m, Lm=φ(θ)−m is bounded and L²m has at most linear θ-growth, so Gaussian L² domains justify K′(0), K′′(0) on this test.
