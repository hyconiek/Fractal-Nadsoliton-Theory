### H2. New impossibility targets worth proving

Do not count the already proved finite-prefix, single-cycle, static-clock or
generic selector no-go results again. The genuine extensions are:

1. Exact rank-seven **observable algebra** closure versus valid curved Gibbs
   families, rather than rank selection from symmetry alone.
2. Nontrivial exact **spectral-cut functors** under explicitly stated composition
   axioms, rather than the old quadratic energy additivity result.
3. Uniform continuum-order inference from **finite noisy bounded-time data**
   without a lower tail/onset restriction, rather than another finite shell.
4. Frequency-independent **dynamic edge closure** under every subdivision,
   with a quantitative minimal memory/response order.
5. Fair **one-reset composite e-factors** under a convex-hull-rich null, while
   retaining the distinction from batch tests and e-processes.
6. A three-state **positive all-horizon extension** of a finite block table,
   rather than ordinary matrix rank alone.
7. System-memory attribution with **unknown detector memory/postselection**,
   beyond the old one-step process alias.
8. Scalar positive-weight **pointwise consistency on fixed RNG support**,
   beyond the known unweighted anisotropy example.
9. Autonomous drift in a derived **unbiased gradient phase action**, rather
   than failure to find a moving structure in a finite numerical scan.

An elegant negative result is a primary success: it identifies precisely the
smallest new datum that physics would need.

## I. Physical promotion gates

The claim ladder remains `M + explicit PM -> PB -> PP -> EV`. The task claim
types in this programme label **targets**, not achieved results. Use PROVEN,
STRONG_NUMERICAL_EVIDENCE, CONDITIONAL, HYPOTHESIS and SPECULATION independently
of execution status. Finite conditional mathematics is not observational EV.

| Proposed physical word | Necessary bridge before promotion | Current decision |
|---|---|---|
| Physical space | Operational separation/influence, stable metric/operator refinement and an identified state/measurement interface | Internal carrier/conditional geometry only |
| Physical time | Temporal law, consistent clocks, calibration and treatment of rate gauges | No sourced clock; clock-free records are not seconds |
| Energy | Generator/conservation or explicit bath/work balance, composition and units | Cost/Dirichlet data or conditional balances only |
| Matter | Localized transported identity, nonlinear persistence/stability, conservation/balance and a measurable state map | Nonlinear lane conditional; no particle claim |
| Quantum | New operational state/effect/composition structure, affine preparation consistency and a nonclassical distinction under stated memory/locality assumptions | Closed gate unless genuinely new data/object appears |
| Gravity | Dynamic geometry, reciprocal backreaction, conserved source, probe universality, controlled continuum and causal propagation | Closed gate; graph deformation is not GR |

Nothing here sources physical g, hbar, c, G, a canonical state selector,
legacy-role transfer, L_total, Standard Model or ToE closure. Effective cells
remain internal subsystems of the informational nadsoliton, not a lower
informational substance. No external experiment, contact or purchase is
authorized by this plan.
