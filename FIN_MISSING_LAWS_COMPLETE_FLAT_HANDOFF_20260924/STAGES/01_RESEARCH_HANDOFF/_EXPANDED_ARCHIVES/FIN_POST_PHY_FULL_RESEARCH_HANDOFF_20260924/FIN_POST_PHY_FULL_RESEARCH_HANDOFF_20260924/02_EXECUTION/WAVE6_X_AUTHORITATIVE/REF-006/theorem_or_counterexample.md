# REF-006 theorem / obstruction

**Theorem (band-limited fixed-stencil continuum).** Let `w_d>=0`, d=1..R, be fixed and let `L_h` be the periodic symmetric finite-range operator above. For every P<infinity,

`sup_{|p|<=P} |lambda_h(p)-kappa p^2| <= (h^2 P^4/12) sum_d w_d d^4`,

with `kappa=sum_d w_d d^2`. Hence every fixed Fourier band converges uniformly to `-kappa d^2/dx^2`; resolvents on `Re z>=rho>0` converge on that band by the resolvent identity.

**Nonuniqueness corollary.** The theorem does not select this refinement family from the finite FIN kernel. Existing finite-range/heavy-tail continuations and exact coarse-intertwining fiber moduli have identical admitted coarse data but incompatible fine/continuum behavior. Therefore FIN currently supplies a conditional local continuum family, not a unique continuum operator.

**Fine-scale negative control.** For `p h=pi/2`, the strict six-shell symbol/continuum-symbol ratio is `0.210703994906` for every M, so fixed-band convergence is not uniform over frequencies of order 1/h.
