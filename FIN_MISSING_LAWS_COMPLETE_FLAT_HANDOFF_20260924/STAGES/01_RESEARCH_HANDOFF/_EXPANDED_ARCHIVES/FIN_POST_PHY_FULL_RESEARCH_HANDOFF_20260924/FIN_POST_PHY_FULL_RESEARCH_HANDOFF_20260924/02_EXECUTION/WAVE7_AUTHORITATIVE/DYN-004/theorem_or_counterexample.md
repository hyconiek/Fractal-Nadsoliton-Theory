# DYN-004 nonexistence certificate

**Proposition.** In the declared dissipative phase dynamics inherited from DYN-002/DYN-003, suppose `a(x-ct)` is a finite-energy front joining two translation-related minima with equal U. Multiplication of the travelling-wave equation by `a'` and integration gives `gamma c integral(a'^2)=Delta U=0`. For a nonconstant front the integral is positive, so c=0.

**Disposition.** The requested moving/relative-periodic branch is absent in the currently admitted kinetic model, so validated continuation/stability of a nonzero-speed branch terminates at the analytic obstruction. Reopening transport requires a genuinely new kinetic/source atom.
