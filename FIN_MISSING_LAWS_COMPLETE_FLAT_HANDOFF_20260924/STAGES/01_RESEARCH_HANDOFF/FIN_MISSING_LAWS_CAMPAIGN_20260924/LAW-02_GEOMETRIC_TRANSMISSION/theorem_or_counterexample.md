# Exact rectangular theorem

On a periodic orthogonal rectangle grid with spacings `h_x,h_y`, cell volume `h_x h_y`, x-face measure `h_y`, y-face measure `h_x`, and center distances `h_x,h_y`, the finite-volume generator with `m=mu h_x h_y`, `c_x=kappa h_y/h_x`, `c_y=kappa h_x/h_y` is exactly the standard anisotropic-spacing discretization of the isotropic Laplacian. Its low Fourier symbol converges to `(kappa/mu)(p_x^2+p_y^2)` with O(max(h_x^2,h_y^2)) error.

Hence microscopic sampling aspect ratio is not an effective material anisotropy in this operator class.
