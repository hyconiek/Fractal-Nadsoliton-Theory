# Working hypotheses supplied for this campaign

1. A mediator field is exactly neutral to monopole/dipole/quadrupole content, potentially removing k=0,±1,±2 and leaving rank q-5.
2. Geometric storage and transmission are represented by cell volume and shared-face area over separation, rather than an unweighted adjacency Laplacian.
3. Translation/geometry phase and internal deformation should be distinct variables; alpha=arg(z4 conj(z3)) is geometry-like and beta=arg(z3 z5 conj(z4)^2) is translation-invariant.
4. The full relational state stores flow/momentum; reversible full dynamics may reduce to memory after hidden variables are eliminated.

These were treated as hypotheses, not as facts imported from the previous campaign.
