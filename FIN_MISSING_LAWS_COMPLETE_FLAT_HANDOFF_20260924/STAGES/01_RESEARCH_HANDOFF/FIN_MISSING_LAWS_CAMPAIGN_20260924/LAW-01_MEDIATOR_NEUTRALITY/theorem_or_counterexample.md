# Theorem / control

For a real symmetric circulant PSD matrix `A` on `Z_q`, with only the constant sector null and positive Fourier eigenvalues elsewhere, let `C_L` span the real harmonics `k=1,...,L`. Conditioning a zero-mean Gaussian with covariance `A` on `C_L eta=0` yields the covariance obtained by deleting exactly those Fourier sectors. For `L<q/2`, its rank is `q-1-2L`.

At `L=2`, q=12 gives rank 7. This theorem is representation-independent under cyclic origin shifts and orientation reversal because the zero subspace of each sine/cosine pair is invariant under the corresponding O(2) action.

A one-edge perturbation is the negative control: conditional moment neutrality continues to define a rank-7 covariance, but it no longer equals top-seven spectral truncation.
