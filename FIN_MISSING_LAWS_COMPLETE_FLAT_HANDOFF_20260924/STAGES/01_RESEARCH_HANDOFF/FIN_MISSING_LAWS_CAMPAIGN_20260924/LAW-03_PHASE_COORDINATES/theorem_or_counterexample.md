# Coordinate theorem

The matrix
`[[-1,1,0],[1,-2,1],[4,-3,0]]`
has determinant 1 and maps the translation tangent `(3,4,5)` to `(1,0,0)`. Therefore `(alpha,beta,gamma)` is an exact integer coordinate basis on the local phase torus (modulo angular periodicities). On the all-orders phase-locking manifold, both invariant coordinates vanish.

This proves the proposed alpha/beta distinction is directionally correct but incomplete: one extra invariant exists already in the three-mode subsystem.
