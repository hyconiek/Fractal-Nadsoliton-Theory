# External references used for context, not as FIN derivations

- Gallouët & Herbin (2004), *Convergence of linear finite elements for diffusion equations with measure data*, DOI 10.1016/j.crma.2003.11.024. The paper explicitly compares a Delaunay triangulation with its Voronoi dual and identifies the finite-volume transmissibility as dual-face measure divided by primal edge distance.
- Eymard, Gallouët & Herbin (2004), *A finite volume scheme for anisotropic diffusion problems*, DOI 10.1016/j.crma.2004.05.023.
- van der Schaft & Maschke (2011), *Port-Hamiltonian systems on graphs*, arXiv:1107.2006. Used only to confirm that graph flow/storage Hamiltonian structures are established mathematics.
- Mori–Zwanzig coarse-graining literature: eliminating unresolved variables yields generalized Langevin equations with memory/noise. The campaign itself uses an elementary exact two-oscillator Schur complement, so no external theorem is needed for that calculation.
