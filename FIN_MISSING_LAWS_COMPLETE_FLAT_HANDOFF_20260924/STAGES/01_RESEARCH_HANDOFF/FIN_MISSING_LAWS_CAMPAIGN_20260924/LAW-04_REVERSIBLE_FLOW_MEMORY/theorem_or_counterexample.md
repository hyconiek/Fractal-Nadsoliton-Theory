# Exact elimination theorem (finite linear case)

For `M x_ddot + K x=0`, partition `x=(u,v)` and the corresponding matrices. Wherever `s^2 M_v+K_vv` is invertible, eliminating `v` yields

`[s^2 M_u+K_uu-K_uv(s^2 M_v+K_vv)^-1 K_vu] u = source_from_hidden_initial_data`.

The rational Schur term has poles at hidden normal modes; inverse Laplace transform produces a convolution memory kernel. Therefore exact coarse dynamics is generically non-Markovian even though the full system is finite-dimensional, reversible and energy-conserving.
