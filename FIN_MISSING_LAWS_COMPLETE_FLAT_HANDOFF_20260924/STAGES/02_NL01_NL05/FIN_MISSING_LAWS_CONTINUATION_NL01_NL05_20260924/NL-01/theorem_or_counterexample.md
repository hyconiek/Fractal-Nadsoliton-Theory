# Theorem / no-go

Let `H_L(q)` be the real subspace spanned by Fourier modes 0, ±1, ..., ±L on
`Z_q`, with q>2L. Then:

1. `dim H_L(q)=2L+1`.
2. `H_L(q)` and its orthogonal complement are invariant under the dihedral action.
3. The construction exists for every admissible L and is closed under applying the same constraint componentwise to independent composed carriers.

Therefore dihedral covariance plus this composition rule cannot distinguish
L=2. Any claim that these premises derive rank seven at q=12 is underdetermined.
