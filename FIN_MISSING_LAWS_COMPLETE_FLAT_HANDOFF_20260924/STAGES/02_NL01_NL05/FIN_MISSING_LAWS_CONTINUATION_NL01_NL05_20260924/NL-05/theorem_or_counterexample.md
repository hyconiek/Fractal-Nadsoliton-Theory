# Positive-quadratic no-localization baseline

For rho>0 and kappa>0, the static energy

E[r] = 1/2 int (kappa |r_x|^2 + rho |r|^2) dx

is strictly positive except at r=0. Its Euler-Lagrange operator
`-kappa d_x^2 + rho` has no nonzero decaying zero-energy solution on R.
Thus a localized static branch cannot originate from the certified positive
quadratic transverse sector alone. Any soliton-like object needs additional
nonlinear structure, topology, drive, or another sourced ingredient.
