# Synthesis — where the missing-law programme now stands

## 1. Phase deformation is now structurally clean

On the actual localized branch there are two internal phase-deformation
coordinates, beta and gamma, not four.  The four-dimensional quotient belongs
to the full T5 strict phase torus; the present branch activates only k=3,4,5.

The all-orders FIN potential has one global phase-vacuum orbit after removing
carrier translation.  This closes the most direct phase-kink route.

## 2. Fisher reduces one ambiguity, but only conditionally

The quotient Fisher metric is positive and rapidly convergent in q on the
certified continuum branch.  A Čencov-type Markov-invariance postulate would
make Fisher canonical up to scale, substantially reducing NL-04's arbitrary
positive kinetic tensor.

However FIN has not yet shown that its refinement maps form the required
Markov-morphism category, and state-space metric uniqueness is not the same as
a sourced momentum/bracket/time law.

## 3. Rank-seven mediator source remains one premise short

The moment-neutrality law is genuinely predictive away from q=12, but the
integer cutoff L=2 is not selected by current symmetry, composition or
refinement principles.

## 4. The matter-like route moved from phase to radial coexistence

At the first global MP7 transition the uniform and localized radial states are
exactly the right kind of pair for a domain-wall problem: inequivalent
degenerate minima with an index-one barrier between them.

The central numerical saddle recovered here has energy `0.046555459529`.
This is much more promising than forcing a second phase vacuum that FIN does
not possess.

The remaining blocker is now sharper:

`source radial intercell stiffness + source reversible kinetic metric`
`-> certify full vector static wall`
`-> only then test moving-wall persistence`.

This is a narrower and more falsifiable programme than another generic soliton
search.
