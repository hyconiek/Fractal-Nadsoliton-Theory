# NL-08 — source challenge for the quadrupole cutoff L=2

Status: **NO_GO_L2_CUTOFF_NOT_SELECTED_BY_CURRENT_PRE_RANK7_PRINCIPLES**

The neutral-mediator proposal belongs to an exact hierarchy.  Neutrality
through multipole order `L` removes

`k=0, ±1, ..., ±L`

and therefore, for `q>2L+1`, produces rank

`q-(2L+1)`.

For `q=12` the sequence is rank 11, 9, **7**, 5, 3, 1 for `L=0,...,5`.
The desired rank seven is simply the `L=2` member.

The key source test is negative:

- every fixed L is origin-independent and orientation-independent;
- every fixed L is Dq-covariant;
- independent composition preserves every L;
- fixed-L carrier refinement is consistent for every L;
- no current FIN conservation law terminates the hierarchy specifically at
  the quadrupole.

Thus symmetry, covariance, composition and the existing refinement principles
do not select L=2.  A rule such as "the mediator is a rank-two/stress-like
object" would select it, but that would be a **new typed law**, not a result
already present in FIN.

The candidate remains scientifically useful because once L=2 is declared it
predicts `rank=q-5` and differs from top-eigenmode truncation away from the
circulant training case.  What remains open is its sourcehood, not its algebra.
