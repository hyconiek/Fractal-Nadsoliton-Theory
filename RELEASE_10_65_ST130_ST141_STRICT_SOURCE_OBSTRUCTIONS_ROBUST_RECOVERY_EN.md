## Abstract

Release 10.65 executes FIN research programs ST130–ST141. The batch asks whether the operational fine gauge, selector resources, robust statistical inference, and thermal dynamics can be generated from the strict spectral operator alone. It obtains a class-specific no-go theorem, several exact conditional constructions, an independent interval replay, and explicit boundaries on what those results do not establish.

The strict operator has seven interval-separated spectral classes with multiplicities

\[
(1,2,2,2,2,2,1).
\]

Thus \(C^*(A)\) is abelian of complex dimension seven, while its commutant has complex dimension twenty-two. On a doubled space, \(I\oplus U\) preserves \(A\oplus A\) for every \(U\in U(12)\) only if \(A\) is scalar. Since it is not, the full fine \(U(12)\) gauge used in the preceding operational quotient cannot emerge from strict functional calculus, finite direct sums, and strict dynamical covariance alone. This does not exclude dilations, environments, state-dependent redundancies, or operational equivalences introduced as new typed objects.

ST132 removes the published numerical center from the transcendental-fold replay. Starting from a coarse localized ansatz, damped Newton iteration reaches residual \(6.94\times10^{-17}\) in four steps. The independently reconstructed interval system then proves strict Krawczyk inclusion in a radius-\(10^{-8}\) box with margin \(9.999987060638205\times10^{-9}\). This is a local root theorem, not an exhaustive global root count.

The batch also proves optimal recovery under a declared depolarizing noisy-pinch model, shows that unconstrained symmetry breaking has zero resource-cost infimum, certifies a local Chernoff stationary root uniformly over interval nuisance parameters, derives affine-cohomology consistency for finite refinement diagrams, restores a reduced positive count exponent under a certified finite dependence length, constructs an exact matched-gap bath dilation for one selected transition, and proves a noisy non-diagonal minimax value of \(1/12\).

## Central conclusion

The strict spectral operator remains a coherent generator of multiple mathematical channels, but it does not generate the operational fine gauge in the tested natural class. State-space geometry alone does not assign a positive cost to selection; a pump does not remove the first modal degeneracy; abstract conditional independence does not identify an algebra; and one matched energy gap does not thermalize the full spectrum.

The next missing theorem must therefore concern a typed operational category and constrained resources, not another relabeling of \(f(A)\).

No strict fine-gauge source, QW-2191 discharge, dimensional clock or unit, laboratory apparatus or record, legacy-to-strict completion or physical-role transfer, Standard Model, gravity, \(L_{\rm total}\), physical projection theorem, or Theory-of-Everything closure is claimed.
