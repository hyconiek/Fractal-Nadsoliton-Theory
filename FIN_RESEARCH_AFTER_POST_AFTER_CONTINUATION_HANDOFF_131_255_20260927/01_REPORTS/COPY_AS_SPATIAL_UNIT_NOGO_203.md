# COPY-AS-SPATIAL-UNIT-NOGO-203
## Internal finite-copy labels cannot serve as emergent spatial cells under the existing leave-one-out law

Date: 2026-09-26

Reports 200-202 test a particularly tempting shortcut:

    N microscopic copies
      ->
    N simultaneous spatial units.

The shortcut fails operationally.

## Direct influence

Every copy directly affects every other copy's conditional update law.

## Event dependence

Every pair of local refresh kernels is generically noncommuting.

## Scaling

Pair influence weakens as O(1/N), but each copy has O(N) partners.

Thus the interaction remains all-to-all mean-field.

## Geometry

Any influence-weighted graph generated this way is permutation-homogeneous.

It has no:
- finite neighbor shell;
- directional structure;
- bounded graph degree;
- spatial dimension selected independently of N.

## Consequence

The same N that successfully controls:
- finite-population fluctuations;
- large deviations;
- metastability;

cannot simultaneously be reinterpreted as the number of spatial sites without
changing the microscopic law.

This cleanly separates:

    internal statistical multiplicity N

from

    number n of simultaneous physical/effective units.

The multicell incidence problem therefore remains genuine.
