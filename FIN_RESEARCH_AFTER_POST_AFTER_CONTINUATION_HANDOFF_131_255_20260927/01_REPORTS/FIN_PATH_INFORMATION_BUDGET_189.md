# FIN-PATH-INFORMATION-BUDGET-189
## Exact reversible realization requires environment information capacity growing linearly with refresh history

Date: 2026-09-26

Status:
exact entropy lower bound plus exact finite-N evaluation for N=3..8.

For the discrete one-refresh version of the exact leave-one-out Gibbs count
chain, let

    K_N

be the row-stochastic count-state transition kernel, including self-refreshes.

At stationarity define the Markov entropy rate

    h_N
      =
      H(X_(t+1)|X_t)
      =
      -sum_x pi_N(x)
       sum_y K_N(x,y) log K_N(x,y).

For a stationary Markov chain:

    boxed:
    H(X_1,...,X_T | X_0)
      =
      T h_N.

## 1. Deterministic dilation bound

Suppose the T-step stochastic record is generated from:
- fixed initial subsystem state;
- a deterministic invertible global transformation;
- an environment E carrying the required initial randomness.

A deterministic map cannot generate more conditional output entropy than is
present in E.

Therefore

    boxed:
    H(E)
      >=
    T h_N.

For a finite environment with |E| states,

    boxed:
    log |E|
      >=
    T h_N.

Thus exact Markov randomness is paid for by linearly growing hidden information
capacity if the environment is not reset/replenished.

## 3. Uniform positivity of label redraw

Because the retained field is bounded on the simplex, every softmax label has a
strict positive lower bound.

For the current strict A7 and working g:

    max column field range
      ≈ 1.982033312353;

    q_j
      >= 3.103682117481e-06.

This gives a weak but N-independent positive lower bound on the entropy of the
raw label redraw:

    H(label | microscopic prestate)
      >= 6.739423493877e-04 bits.

Therefore exact all-time refresh randomness cannot be generated by a fixed
finite information reservoir.

## 4. Interpretation

Global information continuity is compatible with the observed heat bath.

But the hidden-history budget is real.

The theory must choose among:
- infinite internal history;
- fresh/reset ancillas with bookkeeping cost;
- an approximation valid only for finite time;
- a different non-Markov closed dynamics.

There is no free erasure.
