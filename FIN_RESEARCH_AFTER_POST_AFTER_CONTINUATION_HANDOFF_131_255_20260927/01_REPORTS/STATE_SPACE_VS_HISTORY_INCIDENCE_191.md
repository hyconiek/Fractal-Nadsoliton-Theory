# STATE-SPACE-VS-HISTORY-INCIDENCE-191
## The transformation-cycle candidate splits into two distinct geometrical roles

Date: 2026-09-26

Reports 183-190 reveal two mathematically similar but physically different
two-port structures.

### A. Finite transitive permutation on units

    T:V->V

with one orbit gives

    C_n.

This is a candidate simultaneous incidence topology IF a typed rule identifies
unit-transform edges with spatial relations.

### B. Natural extension shift on histories

    sigma:Omega->Omega

gives, almost surely,

    Z

along one realized trajectory.

This structure already has an operational meaning:
successive history states.

So it is a candidate temporal ordering.

## Why they must not be conflated

A successor relation in state history connects:

    same system at different update positions.

A spatial incidence relation should connect:

    different simultaneous subsystems.

Both graphs can be degree two.

Graph isomorphism does not imply physical-role identity.

## Consequence

FIN now has a credible mathematical mechanism for:

    transformation order / history axis,

but still lacks a sourced simultaneous-unit incidence law.

This separation prevents a major category error:
deriving one-dimensional TIME-like order and calling it one-dimensional SPACE.
