## Theorem

Let G be a finite connected simple graph with

    degree(v)=2

for every vertex.

Then

    boxed:
    G is isomorphic to the cycle C_n.

Proof:
start from any vertex and follow unused edges. Finite degree two prevents
branching. Finiteness forces eventual return. Connectedness implies every
vertex lies on that closed walk. Hence the graph is one cycle.

## Why d=2 is special

For d=3 and above, reports 165-167 give inequivalent connected regular graphs
with different spectra.

For d=2, connectedness removes that ambiguity completely at the UNLABELED
topology level.

Thus the conditions

    exact valence 2
      +
    connectedness

would give a coordinate-free one-dimensional cyclic incidence topology.

No preferred vertex or orientation is required.
