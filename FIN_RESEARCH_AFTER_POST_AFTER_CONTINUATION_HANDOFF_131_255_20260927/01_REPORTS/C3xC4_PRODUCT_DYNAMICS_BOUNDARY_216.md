# C3xC4-PRODUCT-DYNAMICS-BOUNDARY-216
## C3 square C4 is an exact transition skeleton, but not yet the full effective generator

Date: 2026-09-26

The d3 and d4 edges generate exactly

    C3 square C4.

It is tempting to keep only:
- q3 fiber jumps;
- q4 base jumps.

That would give a strict Cartesian-product Markov generator.

The full memory-aware 12-state generator, however, also contains positive
rates q1,q2,q5,q6.

Quantitatively:


    N=3:

        = 58.142 %

        = 39.122 %

    N=4:

        = 58.963 %

      relative spectral error if only d3,d4 are kept
        = 38.262 %

    N=5:
      d3+d4 share of total shell-rate weight
        = 60.145 %

      relative spectral error if only d3,d4 are kept
        = 37.061 %

    N=6:
      d3+d4 share of total shell-rate weight
        = 61.607 %

      relative spectral error if only d3,d4 are kept
        = 35.590 %

    N=7:
      d3+d4 share of total shell-rate weight

The product skeleton becomes more prominent over N=3..8, but even at N=8:
- d3+d4 account for only about 65% of total shell-rate weight;
- deleting the other shells changes the Fourier spectrum by about 32%.

Therefore:

    boxed:
    exact algebraic/product skeleton
      !=
    exact product stochastic dynamics.

This is a useful negative result.

The CRT factorization organizes the state space exactly, while the effective
dynamics still contains mixed/nonlocal transitions generated by the full
transition layer and coarse elimination.

A true product-dynamics theorem would require proving the off-product q_d
vanish or become parametrically negligible in a controlled limit.
Current N<=8 data do not establish that.
