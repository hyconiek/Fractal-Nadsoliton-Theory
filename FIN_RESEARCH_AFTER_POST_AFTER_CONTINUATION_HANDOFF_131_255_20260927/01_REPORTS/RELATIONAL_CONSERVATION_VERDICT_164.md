# RELATIONAL-CONSERVATION-VERDICT-164
## A global relation charge is useful but insufficient for emergent locality

Date: 2026-09-26

The conservation-law campaign gives a mixed result.

## Positive

An extensive exactly conserved edge number

    M=(d/2)n

can generate finite mean degree without:
- coordinates;
- log(n) edge chemical-potential tuning.

This is the first tested mechanism that obtains genuine graph sparsity from a
size-independent FORM of law.

## Negative

It leaves three unsolved questions.

### Charge-sector selection

Why is M proportional to n, and what fixes d?

### Incidence identity

The MaxEnt fixed-M ensemble still treats all pairs equivalently.

It selects how MANY edges exist, not WHICH pairs should remain related.

### Persistence

Natural reversible edge-swap dynamics decorrelates each edge on O(1) time.

So neighborhoods are annealed rather than persistent.

## Sharpened requirement

A successful relational law needs at least one conserved or metastable
quantity carrying PAIR IDENTITY, not merely total edge count.

Examples of the required mathematical type could include:
- local flux conservation at each unit;
- cycle/holonomy charges;
- matching constraints;
- dynamically conserved relational labels.

But none may be inserted ad hoc.
