## 1. Effective twelve-state generator

Let the twelve localized minima be labelled by Z12.

For symmetric separation rates k_d, d=1,...,6,

    (Qf)(n)
      =
      sum_(d=1)^5
        k_d[
          f(n+d)+f(n-d)-2f(n)
        ]
      +
        k_6[
          f(n+6)-f(n)
        ].

This is a convolution/circulant generator.

## 2. Three four-state sectors

Partition Z12 into residue classes modulo 3:

    C0={0,3,6,9},
    C1={1,4,7,10},
    C2={2,5,8,11}.

For every microscopic state inside a given C_a:
- d=3 and d=6 transitions remain inside the same block;
- each d in {1,2,4,5} sends exactly one of ±d to each of the two other blocks.

Therefore the total rate from any state in C_a to either other block is the
same number

    boxed:
    kappa
      =
      k_1+k_2+k_4+k_5.

This is the strong lumpability condition.

## 4. Why modulo 3 is dynamically selected

A circulant chain admits many algebraic subgroup quotients.

The large-g barrier ordering adds the dynamical selection.

The d=3 transition is exponentially fastest among pair-saddle classes.

The subgroup generated by step 3 is exactly

    H=<3>
      ={0,3,6,9}.

So fast motion first equilibrates inside the cosets of H.

The next important inter-coset rate is d=4.

Thus the rate hierarchy selects the quotient

    Z12/H
      ~= Z3

without imposing binary refinement.

## 5. Fast/slow spectral separation

Within one four-state block, the internal generator contains:
- nearest steps ±3 with rate k3;
- the antipodal step 6 with rate k6.

Its nonzero decay rates include

    2(k3+k6),
    2(k3+k6),
    4 k3.

Hence its internal gap is

    gamma_fast
      =
      min[
        2(k3+k6),
        4k3
      ].

The block process has slow gap

    gamma_slow
      =
      3(k1+k2+k4+k5).

In the asymptotic ordering

    k3 >> k4 >> k5 >> k2 >> k6 >> k1,

this becomes

    gamma_fast
      ~ 2 k3,

    gamma_slow
      ~ 3 k4.

So

    gamma_fast/gamma_slow
      ~ (2/3)(k3/k4),

which is exponentially large on the large-N metastable scale if the previously
identified capacity exponents are confirmed.
