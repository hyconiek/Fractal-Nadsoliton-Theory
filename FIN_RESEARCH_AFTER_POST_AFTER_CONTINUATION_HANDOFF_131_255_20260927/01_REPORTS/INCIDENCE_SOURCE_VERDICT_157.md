# INCIDENCE-SOURCE-VERDICT-157
## The next missing law is a pair-specific relational degree of freedom

Date: 2026-09-26

Reports 153-156 close the simplest incidence-source possibilities.

## Ruled out

### Homogeneous intrinsic-state sourcing

Permutation equivariance forces:
- empty graph; or
- complete graph.

No sparse locality.

### Finite state-type sourcing

A q-type unit state gives only a q-block complete graph.

For fixed q it cannot distinguish neighborhoods of same-type units.

### Reusing metastable transition conductance

This is a type mismatch:
alternative states of one unit are not simultaneous physical units.

## Surviving architecture

A genuine multicell FIN theory requires a pair-specific relational object

    r_xy.

The minimal next question is:

> Can r_xy itself be generated dynamically from one common microscopic law,
> rather than inserted as a fixed graph?

A successful theory would need:
1. a state space for r_xy;
2. an equilibrium or invariant law;
3. a kinetic law;
4. coupling to the unit states;
5. a rule making most r_xy effectively inactive so locality is sparse;
6. a coarse-graining theorem showing that the relational layer remains
   controlled across scales.

## New research atom

`DYNAMIC-RELATION-LAW-158`

Do NOT begin by choosing a lattice or random geometric graph.

Start from the smallest permutation-equivariant pair-state model and test
whether sparsity/locality can arise spontaneously from its own FIN-compatible
potential and dynamics.

Acceptance:
- sparse incidence selected without external node coordinates;
- no separately fitted graph for each system size;
- held-out n=3 or n=4 prediction from parameters fixed at n=2;
- memory/slow-mode analysis using the same standards as reports 133-145.

Failure:
record a no-go and do not rename the complete/block graph as emergent space.
