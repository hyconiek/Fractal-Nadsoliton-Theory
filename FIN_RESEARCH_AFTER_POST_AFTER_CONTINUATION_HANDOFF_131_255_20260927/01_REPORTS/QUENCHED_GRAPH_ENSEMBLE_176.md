# QUENCHED-GRAPH-ENSEMBLE-176
## A one-shot random graph selection gives persistent but realization-dependent geometry

Date: 2026-09-26

Status:
exact conceptual fork.

Suppose a graph B is sampled once from a permutation-invariant distribution and
then conserved forever.

This is a quenched relational ensemble.

## Advantages

- sparse graph realizations can be obtained;
- pair identity persists;
- no continual annealing destroys neighborhoods.

## But the physical predictions are conditional on B

For a declared multicell coupling such as PM-001,

    collective gains
      =
      g-kappa ell_r(B),

where ell_r(B) are graph-Laplacian eigenvalues.

Different B drawn from the same degree/edge-count ensemble generally have
different:
- spectra;
- resistance distances;
- cycle structure;
- transport.

So a single realized graph carries additional physical information not present
in the one-unit FIN state.

## Annealed versus quenched prediction

If one averages over B before computing long-time physics:
- permutation symmetry returns;
- the mean interaction becomes block/complete mean-field.

If one conditions on the realized B:
- locality survives;
- but B is extra quenched data.

Therefore spontaneous one-shot graph sampling is a logically possible
extension, but it is not a parameter-free derivation of geometry unless the
distribution of B and its observable consequences become sufficiently
universal/self-averaging.

No such universality theorem currently exists in FIN.
