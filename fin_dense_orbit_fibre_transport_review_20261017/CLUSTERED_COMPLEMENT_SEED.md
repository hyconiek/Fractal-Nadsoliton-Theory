## 1. A genuinely clustered, bounded-hole extension

Choose the source ramp orbit Y of twelve separated **right-fibre** centres.
Let A be the union of all 36 upward predecessors of each centre. For large
N the centres and their three-step neighbourhoods are central, in phase A,
and outside E. The clusters have diameter <=4, while distinct clusters are
separated by more than4. This A is G_+-invariant but not 4-separated.

For T=X_l\A, every nondeparture right hole must be a centre. Indeed it has
a predecessor in A and is within four count units of a centre, hence is
central and has all 36 predecessors. Those predecessors have diameter <=4,
so lie in one cluster. The two 36-element predecessor sets must coincide.
Their barycentre is y-sigma/6, which identifies y uniquely. Conversely all
centres lose every predecessor. Thus R_bulk=Y, with any other holes in E.

Using the accepted reverse neighbour-weight ratio >9/10, row lower .173NW
and bulk column upper .35NW gives

    O'(A)-I'(R) >= N[(6569/1250) W(Y)-W(E_right)].

The centre mass is polynomial and E is exponential by the same uniform
Stirling payment as in PROOF_COMPLETIONS_AND_SCOPE.md. Therefore these
clustered dense-complement cuts satisfy Hall eventually on every adjacent
compact pair. This pays one first clustered family, not arbitrary clusters.
More bounded predecessor blocks are not the default continuation.

## 2. Freeze a positive-mass, growing cluster instead

On a left fibre let p_m=u+m sigma/12, w=p-p_m, Y_N=sqrt(N)w and freeze

    L=1/10, A_ball={x in X_l: ||Y_N(x)||_2<=L}.

This is a count-lattice analysis norm inherited from MT, not physical
distance. Its squared membership test is rational in the counts and N.
The hole set has order N^5 lattice points and strictly positive limiting
conditional Gaussian mass; it is not another finite-orbit perturbation.
It is G_+-invariant. Since R_k3<=12||Y||²<=.12 while T>=Tmin>1, it and its
fixed-step neighbourhood are phase A and central for large N. Phase ties,
actual D_N and the full H support are unchanged.

## 3. Pay the predecessor erosion geometry exactly

For the 36 upward moves from odd a to even b put

    v_ab=e_b-e_a-sigma/6, P_fast=I-11^T/12-sigma sigma^T/12.

Exactly, average(v)=0, average(vv^T)=P_fast/6 and ||v||²=5/3.
For Y in the fast space, the projections Y.v have mean zero, variance
||Y||²/6 and absolute value <=sqrt(5/3)||Y||. If M is their positive
maximum and B that absolute bound, averaging (a+B)(M-a)>=0 gives
M>=||Y||/sqrt(60)>||Y||/8. The same applies to -Y.

A right state's predecessors have coordinates Y-v_ab/sqrt(N). Hence

    max_pred ||Y_pred||²
      >=||Y||²+||Y||/(4sqrt(N))+5/(3N)
      >(||Y||+1/(8sqrt(N)))².

If all its predecessors lie in A_ball, its radius is at most
`L-1/(8sqrt(N))`. Any such non-E right hole is central and has all36
legal predecessors, by the same fixed-neighbourhood argument as section1.
Thus R_bulk is contained in the right ball eroded by this amount. Actual
E holes remain separately charged, never deleted from exact Hall.

## 4. The two quantitative lemmas still needed

Let B_right be the un-eroded right ball and J_A,l the exact phase-A current.
One sufficient proof route is, uniformly over the fixed soft band,

    I'_{l+1}({L-1/(8sqrt(N))<||Y||<=L})
       >=c_* N^(-1/2) J_A,l, c_*>0;                 (SHELL)

    |O'_l(A_ball)-I'_{l+1}(B_right)|
       =o(N^(-1/2) J_A,l).                           (ADJACENT)

The E-column payment is exponentially smaller. The exact complement
identity then proves Hall for this frozen growing cluster. Neither SHELL
nor ADJACENT has been proved here. Weak local convergence is insufficient
for a shell whose width is a single lattice scale. Individually o(1)
Stirling errors do not supply the differenced ADJACENT rate.

A future proof must pay lattice translation, shrinking-annulus arithmetic,
adjacent-level cancellations, exact source/carry corrections and all E terms.
If this route fails, retain the precise failure rather than tune L or claim
universal Hall. Even success pays only this mesoscopic family. Full dense
orbit Hall and the whole-domain kinetic/IBA gates remain open.
