## Abstract

This release presents a self-contained current-state compendium of the Fractal-Nadsoliton Theory (FIN) research programme. It reconstructs FIN from its founding postulate—that the nadsoliton is primordial self-similar information in a persistent solitonic state—while keeping that postulate distinct from mathematical results and empirical claims. The intended internal order is nadsoliton, light, matter, and emergent observer; no separate informational substrate is placed beneath the nadsoliton.

The monograph audits the complete kernel genealogy. The ontological legacy kernel is retained as an intermediate bridge object, the strict gate kernel as the later enriched finite working profile, and the reconstructed Legacy* class as a corrected historical formula rather than a third final kernel. It records algebraic errors in the original coupling narrative and shows that no complete legacy-to-strict completion theorem or physical-role transfer theorem is presently available.

On the twelve-cycle, the strict kernel defines a symmetric weighted adjacency operator \(W\), row sum \(s\), and positive Dirichlet generator \(A=sI-W\). The same self-adjoint operator generates unitary, heat, wave, resolvent, Green, filtering, and variational calculi. These constructions share spectral data but do not thereby acquire identical physical meanings. The report develops the associated spectral measure, inverse-action reconstruction, Schur-complement compression, conditional adaptive dynamics, information identities, observer-relative scale architecture, operational measurement model, and executable laboratory-transfer proposal.

The compendium also synthesizes the later local analytical and certified-computational programme through ST461 and the current FAR state map: equivariant twelve-branch localization, symmetry and selector obstructions, exact invariant relations, refinement and coarse-observer results, scale-torsor no-section theorems, interval-certified stationary branches, a certified global-transition bracket, infrared continuation, and finite causal-discrimination certificates. Every result is classified as proven, strong evidence, conditional, speculative, open, or refuted in its declared scope.

The deepest conclusion surviving the audit is that FIN currently supplies a coherent, finite, dimensionless spectral-information framework with contextual dynamics and controlled symmetry-breaking constructions. It does not yet derive a unique selector, an absolute orientation, dimensional units, a physical clock, a complete legacy-to-strict bridge, a state-preparation law, a laboratory record, a continuum spacetime, Standard Model fields, gravity, or a Theory of Everything. The shortest credible route to physics requires an explicit non-circular source law, conversion and sector axioms, an operational process–tester model, a refinement or continuum family, and blinded held-out experimental comparison.
