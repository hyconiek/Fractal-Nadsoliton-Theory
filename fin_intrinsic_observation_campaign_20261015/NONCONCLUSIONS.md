- No MET/BAR tagged integrated-record theorem was proved; their own state-dependent Poisson corrector remains outstanding.
- Calculated risk decimals and generator checks are not high-precision/outward interval bounds or measured phenomena.
- No finite-N sample complexity, explicit local-window K or N0, arbitrary-start theorem, global metastability, B4/FIN-M0/task335, source/rank/kernel selection, physical geometry/QM/GR/SM/ToE is inferred.
