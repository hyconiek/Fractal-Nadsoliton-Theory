## Admission by task

### RG-F01 — reusable positive process

Accept the positive difference CTMC and fixed noninvasive thirteen-output
instrument, conditional on the admitted circulant/zero-field/equivariant
model. Arbitrary initial difference laws, mixture-affinity and complete
record marginalization are valid. The observed noisy outputs need not
themselves be Markov. Physical disturbance or evolving invalid states
needs an explicit outcome/successor instrument or full-parent fallback.

The synthetic emission example has .03 invalid+.02 cross-talk=.05 row-TV
error relative to ideal readout. It is a composition test, **not** evidence
that the .005 discrimination budget is met. All synthetic records/probability
vectors remain labelled numerical design inputs.

The helper assumes its input contract rather than validating every premise.
Its heat-bath shortcut takes pstat and returns a reset law; calling it with
an inconsistent generator/interaction is not licensed. Future use needs
model-bound and probability/instrument checks before inference.

### RG-F02 — symmetry and readout controls

### RG-F03 — error accounting

Accept the exact same-representation Duhamel/TV telescoping theorem and
the explicit OPEN_INPUT components. Source Poisson-tail numbers were point
evaluated at high precision, not outward interval propagation; despite the
filename PREDICTION_ENCLOSURES, the source correctly exports
`all_operation_error_included=false`. They are numerical tail diagnostics.

The illustrative extra error allocation leaves rho=.00039114, not .00119114.
It is hypothetical and disjoint from the .005 per-hypothesis output radius;
do not count components twice or ignore them in the statistical class.

The burn-in value4.258596596 is a conservative **sufficient** dimensionless
wait at eps=.0002 using TV(initial,p)<=1. For a fixed positive p the exact
worst initial distance is1-min(p), so this is not generally a necessary
wait despite the source JSON key's wording. Physical seconds and production
clock stability remain unpaid.

### RG-F04 — statistical theorem, prototype and scope corrections

Accept the iid thirteen-symbol subset-union concentration theorem and
conservative composite-hypothesis rejection in **exact arithmetic**. Gain-
mesh payment is analytically valid; the source uses twice the required
Lipschitz factor. Gain/readout nuisance settings are properly allowed
separately under each hypothesis.

The integer threshold **40,278,676** is independently interval-verified for
rho=.00119114, eps=rho/3 and alpha_validation=.025. With the F03 illustrative
remaining rho=.00039114 the corresponding threshold is **373,539,331**.
These are sufficient counts for that bound, not actual records, necessary
experimental costs or proof of feasibility.

No source test sampled that many outcomes: SYNTHETIC_COMPOSITE_TESTS uses
theoretical probability vectors together with a design n. It is a software
check, not Monte Carlo false-positive estimation or empirical power.

The software's floating grid distances are not outward lower certificates
for arbitrary inputs near the rejection boundary. Likewise a lower bound
below eps means **not rejected**, not certified membership/compatibility.
The alpha_calibration=.025 allocation has no calibration coverage procedure
or actual data; alpha_total=.05 is therefore not an admitted experimental
guarantee. The secondary kinetic comparison is not separately powered.

In-memory interface probes find that the syntax parser accepts empty input,
NaN elapsed time, infinite latency, blank record IDs, malformed timestamps
and reuse of a reset across dataset roles. It correctly rejects negative
elapsed time and missing invalid reasons and preserves noisy label/output
mismatches as warnings. It explicitly does not certify iid or calibration.
These findings restrict it to a prototype, not an evidence admission gate.

The decision API also accepts NaN, infinite and fractional sample sizes.
Finite positive integer n, valid alpha and empirical counts consistent with
n must be checked before any actual decision. Preserving these source
helpers does not mean approving unrestricted deployment.

Finally, the intake proves a stronger result than the source's inconclusive
one-event bound: **every one fixed binary event fails uniform discrimination
in the stated gain/output-law class**. Complete8190-event coverage and exact

See [the statistical scope and proof](STATISTICS_SCOPE_AND_BINARY_NO_GO.md).
