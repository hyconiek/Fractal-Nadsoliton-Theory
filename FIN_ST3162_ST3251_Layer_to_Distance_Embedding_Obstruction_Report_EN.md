# FIN ST3162–ST3251 — Layer-to-Distance Embedding Obstruction

## Executive result

The exact FIN refinement currently exported by the repository is a product
refinement,

\[
A_{24}=A_{12}\otimes I_2+I_{12}\otimes
\begin{pmatrix}q&-q\\-q&q\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Re_i=e_i\otimes\frac{(1,1)}{\sqrt2}.
\]

It satisfies

\[
A_{24}R=RA_{12}
\]

with replay residual \(7.69\times10^{-16}\). Heat and unitary functional
calculi intertwine consequently.

This construction introduces a separate two-point fibre coordinate. It does
not identify refinement level with doubled kernel distance.

## Relabelling test

Under the interleaved labels

\[
(i,b)\longmapsto2i+b,
\]

horizontal separations acquire numerical \(C_{24}\) label distance \(2d\).
However, their conductance remains the inherited coarse value

\[
W_{24}\big((i,b),(j,b)\big)=W_{12}(d),
\]

not \(K_{\rm strict}(2d)\).

The product operator commutes with the shift by two, but generically not with
the shift by one. The latter commutator residual is

\[
1.385640646055102.
\]

Therefore it is not a distance-circulant operator on \(C_{24}\). Relabelling
changes coordinate numbers, not operator weights.

For \(d=1,\ldots,6\), the maximum discrepancy between inherited
\(W_{12}(d)\) and resampled \(K(2d)\) is

\[
0.2779421209549173.
\]

## Even-embedding no-go

Consider the group injection

\[
E:e_i\longmapsto e_{2i}.
\]

For a connected distance-circulant \(C_{24}\) Laplacian obtained from the
strict formula, the even-supported subspace is not invariant. Its measured
leakage to odd sites is

\[
\|(I-EE^*)A_{24}E\|=2.349074757511797.
\]

More generally, the even-supported subspace of a circulant operator is
invariant only when every odd-distance coupling vanishes. That repair splits
the parity components and removes the connected fine geometry. A nonzero
vertical distance-one coupling also violates even-support invariance.

The correct coarse space is the pair-uniform subspace selected by \(R\), not
the subspace supported only at even sites.

## Typed-role obstruction

The exact refinement contains two different objects:

- horizontal conductance \(K(d)\) inherited from the base operator;
- vertical fibre rate \(q\) acting in the new binary coordinate.

The conditional recurrence

\[
q_n=K_{\rm strict}(2^n)

\]

equates these distinct typed roles. It can be adopted as a new layer-distance
axiom, and its summability theorem remains correct, but it is not generated by
the existing product intertwiner.

## Final interpretation

The repository possesses an exact and useful fibre refinement, but that very
construction contradicts the inference that binary refinement automatically
doubles the argument of the kernel. A layer-to-distance law would be a new
geometric axiom or theorem, not a consequence of current intertwining.

The highest-value next step is either to derive a typed geometry mapping fibre
depth to base distance, or to keep \(q_n\) independent of \(K(d)\).

No selector, physical distance, clock, units, legacy role transfer, Standard
Model, gravity, \(L_{\rm total}\), or Theory of Everything closure follows.
