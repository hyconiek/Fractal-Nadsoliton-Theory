## Abstract

Release 10.34 reports FIN Research Programs P394–P410. It sharpens the proof
boundary of the strict signed-moment and radial-kernel results, constructs
new noisy operational observables, and provides an event-level executable
specification for the Jordan Sampling Realization.

The principal exact result is the contact-topology audit of the frozen P380
dual. Exact rational Sturm arithmetic proves that its derivative has nine
roots in ((0,1)), each isolated in a box of width at most (2^{-50}).
Exact interval evaluation proves strictly positive complementary slack at
all seven P366 primal atoms. The frozen P366/P380 pair is therefore a
certified near-contact pair, not an exact complementary optimizer pair. The
same program constructs a new 25-equation KKT candidate with objective
0.7073534677231137 and residual 3.42e-28. Its Jacobian condition number
2.73e15 keeps interval certification open.

P394 reflects the complete depth-fourteen Bernstein subdivision algorithm in
Lean. Exact adaptive execution visits 147 nodes and certifies the full 16,384
cell frontier. Moment Taylor, fifth-root generation, and Krawczyk generators
remain outside the local proof kernel. P396 records the injectivity reduction to
Lindemann–Weierstrass without pretending that transcendence theory has been
mechanized locally.

P397 refutes one proposed origin for the damping-completion atom: it is not a
multiplicative character of additive distance. P398 performs four wide-box
photonic alias searches. Every fit returns near the canonical origin and no
distant alias is found, but the search is not exhaustive.

P400 constructs noise-adapted symmetric codebooks improving the better
product/GHZ baseline by up to 0.06138. P401 optimizes the full twelve-mode
one-use channel and finds no multimode improvement over the extremal two-mode
state in the supplied isotropic noise. P402 solves twelve finite-grid clock
maximin designs; the best worst-case feasible distance is 0.99999047.

P403 freezes a custody-aware experiment contract and a separate 50,000-event
moment-reconstruction integration test. Both validators pass, while physical
evidence remains explicitly false.

P404 proves the JSR-specific law I(S;X)=H_2(q_-)=0.874517 bits. P405
constructs a sign-phase encoder but refutes it as an increasing negativity
monotone or non-premise selector. P406 selects a unique outer parameter only
after the dimensionless quarter-tail normalization 1/4 is supplied.

P399 and P407–P410 remain external gates. No selector closure, complete
legacy-to-strict bridge, legacy-role transfer, internal dimensional source,
laboratory validation, total action, Standard Model, gravity, or
Theory-of-Everything closure is claimed.
