# After orbital persistence: phase, charge and actual outward transport

Date: 2026-10-17. Bounded proof support, not an executed radiation theorem.
All statements concern the already displayed conditional radial field action.

## 1. Reuse the symmetry; do not invent a gauge source

The potential built from |Psi_i|², their radial powers and Hermitian
Psi^dagger K_s Psi is invariant under one **global constant** phase rotation
Psi->exp(i theta)Psi. This is not a new local gauge field, electromagnetic
charge or a strict unit-bearing current. Related conditional phase/charge
identities and their source restrictions already exist in AGENTS
(ST912–1001 and P3085); do not relabel generic Noether theory a discovery.

For the displayed canonical wave action, direct substitution of the full
equations into

    q=sum_i(u_i v_i,t-v_i u_i,t),
    j=sum_i(v_i u_i,x-u_i v_i,x)

gives partial_t q+partial_x j=0. With decay, Q=int q is conserved in model
units. The cancellation is exactly
sum_i(u_i V_v_i-v_i V_u_i)=0. It uses all real/imaginary components.
Noether symmetry does not add a zero mode at the Psi=0 wall, whose phase
orbit is a single point; nonzero excitations can carry Q.

## 2. A mandatory phase countercontrol for any emission claim

Suppose the actual transverse operator has a real localized mode f with
L_perp f=omega² f, omega>0. This existence is a **future proof gate**,
not a consequence of positive gap alone. At leading order choose

    Psi=epsilon f[a cos(omega t)+i b sin(omega t)]

with real a,b. Its contribution to the Phi force is proportional to

    2K epsilon² f^T Y f [ (a²+b²)/2
                         +(a²-b²)cos(2omega t)/2 ].

For real oscillation b=0 there is a second harmonic. For circular rotation
a=b that harmonic cancels exactly: intensity is constant and
Q=epsilon² a b omega ||f||² at this order. Thus a nonzero interaction,
orbital stability and local sector work do not imply a common nonzero
radiative forcing for every complex preparation.

This is linear-mode forcing algebra, **not** an exact nonlinear periodic
solution or a zero-radiation theorem. In particular starting Phi at an
undressed K while switching on a circular mode creates a static-force
mismatch that can itself launch a transient. Do not erase it from records
or claim the cancellation removes it. An exact rotating bound state would
require solving the same action's coupled stationary profile equations.

## 3. The genuinely new target

Freeze one neutral localized-mode preparation with Phi(0)=K, Phi_t(0)=0,
Psi(0)=epsilon f, Psi_t(0)=0, no separately supplied free radiation packet.
First prove the bound mode exists in the admitted parameter region. Then
test whether its second harmonic lies in the scalar continuum and has
nonzero **on-shell** coupling. A nonzero L2 continuum projection alone
does not establish a radiating spectral density at that frequency.

Only after that certificate, control the nonlinear/Duhamel error sufficiently
to prove positive outward energy through a declared spatial window on a
paid time range. For total canonical current J=-phi_t phi_x-z_t.z_x,

    int_0^T[J(t,R)-J(t,-R)]dt
       =E_[-R,R](0)-E_[-R,R](T)

is the exact balance. Positive outward flux, localization of initial data,
background tails and time/size error bounds must all be paid. Sector-energy
increase alone is not that statement. The supplied geometry remains an
assumption, not emergence of space or identification of light.
