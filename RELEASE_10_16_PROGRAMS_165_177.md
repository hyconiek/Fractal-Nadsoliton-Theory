## Abstract

Release 10.16 reports thirteen executed mathematical and computational
research programs testing the weakest points of the axiomatic
informational-spectral architecture developed for FIN. Its purpose is
falsification and operational completion, not confirmation of cosmological
or Theory-of-Everything claims.

The principal result is negative and decisive. The one-copy
modular-equilibrium proposal

$$
\beta_F=\frac{\alpha_{\rm geo}}
{|\operatorname{Hom}(U(12),\{\pm1\})|}
=\frac{\alpha_{\rm geo}}4=\log2
$$

fails tensor intensivity under the natural independent-copy rules
$\alpha_n=n\alpha_{\rm geo}$ and $h_n=4^n$. It therefore cannot be treated
as a derived thermal law. It remains only a consistent one-copy axiom until a
composition-compatible source theorem is proved.

The spectral core remains intact. A conservative positive Dirichlet
generator produces wave, diffusion and Green dynamics through one spectral
measure. The release then proves that state selection, signed preparation,
measurement instrument, dimensional/apparatus calibration and a sourced
legacy-to-strict completion map remain independent mathematical obligations.

New positive results include a cancellation-aware Fourier–Dirichlet tail
estimate improving the previous absolute bound by a factor greater than
5,100; a finite algorithmic Diophantine modulus; a categorical valuation
no-go; a full-state counterexample to completeness of a scalar reflection
monotone; distribution-free iid IQR confidence with bounded detector pixels;
a minimal reference control restoring exponent identifiability; a frozen
six-model adversarial protocol; an exact phase-character obstruction; a
typed nonlinear completion audit; and a finite operational double-slit model
with preparation, environment, POVM, detector and persistent record.

The exact nonlinear completion reproduces the strict target only after
inserting strict-side parameters and fails at the first unsourced damping
edge. It is therefore classified as receiver-complete but source-incomplete.
No legacy physical role is transferred.

## Main scientific conclusions

1. The spectral generator remains the smallest common object behind unitary
   and diffusive dynamics.
2. The proposed cardinality relation for $\beta_F$ is refuted as a
   tensor-intensive derivation.
3. Standard tensor and direct-sum naturality select Hilbert dimension, not
   the desired uniform central-sector state.
4. AFIS capability dependence is verified over all 64 six-axiom subsets.
5. The reflection resource requires more than one scalar monotone on the full
   state space.
6. iid detector confidence is rigorous, but undeclared memory invalidates it.
7. A known-exponent shared-gain control is a minimal object that restores
   identifiability.
8. High synthetic classifier accuracy is not external physical validation.
9. The strict phase requires new representation data not contained in the
   period-eight legacy phase.
10. Numerical reproduction of the strict kernel is not completion
    provenance.
11. A double-slit experiment requires an instrument, environment, detector
    and record in addition to the common operator.
12. QW-2191, canonical units, positive damping provenance, bridge closure,
    role transfer and experimentally validated physics remain open.
