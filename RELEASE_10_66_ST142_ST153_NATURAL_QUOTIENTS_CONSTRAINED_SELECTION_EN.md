## Abstract

Release 10.66 executes FIN research programs ST142–ST153. It asks whether operational quotients, selection, statistical inference, and thermal preparation follow from the strict FIN operator, or require separately typed resources.

The main no-go result strengthens the strict-source boundary. For the unlabeled doubled datum

\[
(\mathcal H\otimes\mathbb C^2,A\otimes I_2),
\]

the full unitary commutant has complex dimension 88, but its conjugation-fixed algebra is only the seven-dimensional spectral center. Therefore a quotient natural under every dynamical automorphism cannot recover the desired fine-blind algebra (M_{12}\oplus\mathbb C), of dimension 145. Named sectors, instruments, environments, region nets, and smaller symmetry groups can evade the theorem only by adding visible operational typing.

ST144 changes the fold picture materially. Sixty-nine starting points produce 35 numerical clusters. Of the first 32 representatives tested by outward interval arithmetic, 29 possess strict local Krawczyk certificates, and their boxes are pairwise disjoint. Thus at least 29 distinct locally unique fold roots are proved. The report does not promote the 35 numerical clusters to an exact root count and does not claim exhaustive coverage.

The batch also proves a complete 78-member coordinate-edge star at the lift vertex, optimal recovery for a declared coherent-involution leakage model, sharp selector-resource costs under a supplied outcome gap, a bounded (C_{12})-equivariant potential with twelve stable but symmetry-equivalent branches, a uniform nuisance-box stationary-root theorem, an exact refinement-DAG cohomology algorithm, algebra selection by a labeled atomic causal net, a positive Markov-modulated count exponent, a sharp minimum bath dimension of twelve for exact global Gibbs reset, and the complete minimax detector set for the declared nonnegative adversary cone.

## Central conclusion

Full naturality does not generate the missing operational structure; it erases it down to spectral labels. A positive selector cost appears only after a preparation constraint is supplied. Equivariant nonlinear dynamics can create twelve stable alternatives, but cannot select which alternative is realized. A typed causal net can identify local algebras, but the net itself remains operational input. Exact full-system thermal reset has a sharp environmental resource cost.

The best surviving mathematical interpretation is therefore a finite spectral-information core with explicitly typed operational completions. The strict operator generates common spectral channels and strong symmetry constraints, but does not generate preparations, a realized selector, dimensional scale, calibrated clock, instruments, environment, apparatus, or independent measurement record by itself.

No strict fine-gauge source, QW-2191 discharge, strict anisotropy source, dimensional unit, laboratory result, physical projection theorem, legacy-to-strict completion, legacy physical-role transfer, Standard Model, gravity, (L_{\rm total}), or Theory-of-Everything closure is claimed.
