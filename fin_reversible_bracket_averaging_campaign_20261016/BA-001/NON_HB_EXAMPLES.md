Same-parity thinning by `eta(g)=1/(1+g²)` preserves each parity-changing intensity of native reflected HB. Therefore its statewise `L m`, statewise `Gamma(m,m)`, stationary Gibbs weights, and all parity-cut fluxes are identical. It is nevertheless not the same full count generator: same-parity replacements have been thinned. Under the usual positive HB edges it remains irreducible because opposite-parity edges remain positive.

The six-node ring exact stress supplied by the new verifier is independent of this q12 example: it redistributes parity-changing conductance with unchanged drift but changes `Gamma` pointwise. It is a warning against inferring microscopic noise from parity-cut data.
