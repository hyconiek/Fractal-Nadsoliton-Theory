# BA-001 — true invariant components and exact centering

Let `E_N` be all allowed (nonzero count-change) single-slot edges inside the actual reflected MT domain. The **permitted** graph is connected by MT-001, but the **positive-rate** graph of a general diagnostic generator need not be: nonnegative symmetric conductances are allowed. Denote its connected components by `C`. The invariant sigma-field is generated by these components; for a finite reversible chain `P_ker(L) f = sum_C 1_C E_pi(f|C)`.

For `d(n)=L_native^D m(n)=L_tilde m(n)` and `G=N Gamma_tilde(m,m)`, stationarity **on each component** gives the exact identity

`E_pi(G|C) = -2N E_pi(m d | C)`.

Thus, with `r=G-E_pi G`, its invariant projection is exactly

`h(n)=sum_C 1_C(n) [-2N E_pi(m d|C)+2N E_pi(m d)]`,

and `||h||²_pi = sum_C pi(C)[ E_pi(G|C)-E_pi G ]²`.

This expression is neither automatically zero nor controlled by the global mean relation. In the restricted subclass where **every permitted edge has strictly positive rate**, connectedness of MT D_N implies `h=0` for each N. This does not impose a uniform lower edge rate or an estimate on the inverse generator. Merely setting a diagnostic generator's proposal rate zero on some edges cannot be undone by referring to connectedness of the native Gibbs graph.

## Exact parity-flux identity (new)

Write the occupied parity levels as `a_0<...<a_J`, with adjacent spacing `δ=2/N`, `p_l=pi(m=a_l)` and set `d(x)=L_tilde m(x)`. All nonzero parity-changing edges connect adjacent occupied levels. Define undirected equilibrium conductance across the cut

`K_l=sum_{x:m=a_l} sum_{y:m=a_(l+1)} pi(x) q(x,y)`.

By summing the statewise drift equation below the cut and cancelling both directions of every internal edge by detailed balance,

`δ K_l = sum_{x:m(x)<=a_l} pi(x) d(x)`.

The right side is fixed by native parity drift and the inherited Gibbs measure. Therefore **every** member of the frozen class has the identical `K_l`, including for cuts near the reflected boundary. Zero `K_l` is allowed; do not divide by it. The directed cross-cut equilibrium traffic is equal in reverse by detailed balance. This is a sum over conductances; it does not identify the individual microscopic rates.

The equilibrium parity-compressed generator (conditional instantaneous rates, **not a semigroup quotient**) is consequently fixed:

`(P_m L_tilde P_m f)(a_l) = [K_l(f_(l+1)-f_l)+K_(l-1)(f_(l-1)-f_l)]/p_l`, `K_-1=K_J=0`.

All Dirichlet pairings on parity-measurable functions are exactly fixed:

`E_tilde(f(m),g(m)) = sum_{l=0}^{J-1}K_l(f_(l+1)-f_l)(g_(l+1)-g_l)`.

Similarly the *full conditional averaged noise* is fixed at **every finite N**:

`E_pi[G|m=a_l] = N δ² (K_(l-1)+K_l)/p_l = E_pi[G_native|m=a_l]`.

This sharpens the prior weak `E[h(Z)(G-2)] -> 0`: conditional parity noise matches the native rate family **exactly**, not merely asymptotically after smooth testing. Native bracket continuity and MT stationarity show `E_pi|G_native-2| -> 0`. Conditional Jensen therefore pays

`E_pi|E_pi[r|m]| <= E_pi|G_native-E_pi G_native| -> 0`,

with `E_pi G=E_pi G_native` from the same cut sum. What can survive is **within-parity-fibre** microscopic noise variation and its temporal correlation. The compressed generator need not generate the observed `m` path, and none of these identities proves IBA.

## Small rational negative control (BA verifier)

On a six-state reversible ring with parity levels `(0,1,2,1,0,1)` and rational invariant weights proportional to `(2,3,5,7,11,13)`, the symmetric six-edge stress `(-1,-1,1,1,-1,1)` changes microscopic `Gamma` but preserves the statewise drift and all `K_l`. At the fully stressed endpoint the actual positive-edge components are `{0,5}`, `{1}`, `{2,3,4}`. Their centered conditional-noise means are `(-8/75,-6/5,26/115)` and are nonzero. This illustrates an actual finite reversible generator obstruction to an unqualified component-centering deduction, **not** an admissible q12/A7 asymptotic counterexample.
