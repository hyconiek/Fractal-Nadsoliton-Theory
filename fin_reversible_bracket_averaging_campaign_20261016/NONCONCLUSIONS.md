# BA nonconclusions and guardrails

* Exact parity-cut conductance and conditional Gamma **do not** imply trajectory-wise bracket averaging or an exact parity Markov quotient.
* Zero invariant projection `h=0` for a strictly positive finite chain does **not** provide a uniform Poisson bound as N grows.
* Failure of `R=o(sqrt N)` is failure of a sufficient criterion, not a different-limit theorem.
* The six-node rational stress is a finite abstract graph, not a q12/A7 admissible all-N countermodel.
* The genuine q12 KR wheel spike has vanishing Gibbs support and does not contradict NC or IBA.
* Same stationary distribution and same scalar drift do not source unique microscopic activities.
* No unconditional stationary critical diffusion theorem, iid-start diagnostic preparation, sharp N0, finite-N risk or practical model clock is asserted.
* No new physical A7/activity/initial-state source, units, geometry, causality, conserved energy, experimental validity, QM, gravity or ToE.
