# Negative controls retained

1. **Near-disconnected finite graph:** same drift/Gibbs/cut conductances, `R~1/tau`; this defeats abstract Poisson-energy domination, **not** q12 IBA.
2. **Connected native domain versus diagnostic connectivity:** permitted `D_N` edges do not impose strict positivity of diagnostic conductances.
3. **Same-parity thinning:** positive non-HB rate family with the identical parity bracket; not evidence that every member has NC or IBA.
4. **Localized q12 wheel spike from KR:** violates statewise noise equicontinuity at a central state, but support Gibbs mass vanishes, so NC can remain true. Do not name it nonuniversality.
5. **Static `E[G|m]` identity:** exact parity-projected expectation is compatible with persistent microscopic noise variation; it does not prove integrated time averaging.
6. **Large R:** failure of the *sufficient* Poisson estimate cannot, by itself, prove failure of IBA. A true counterexample needs a nonvanishing time-integrated observable on the full `sqrt(N)` scale in the actual q12 family.
