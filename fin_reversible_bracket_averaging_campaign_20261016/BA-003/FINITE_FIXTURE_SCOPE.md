# Exact finite control — scope

`verify_ba.py` builds a fresh six-node rational reversible cycle unrelated to the q12 model; it checks exact statewise drift, conductance cut flux, parity-conditional noise, nonzero invariant component, and positive-activity Poisson blowup. Exact rationals establish the algebra but do not verify the asymptotic KR class. The fixture intentionally retains a negative scientific result: an apparent 'gap follows from drift' shortcut is false for general reversible finite graphs.
