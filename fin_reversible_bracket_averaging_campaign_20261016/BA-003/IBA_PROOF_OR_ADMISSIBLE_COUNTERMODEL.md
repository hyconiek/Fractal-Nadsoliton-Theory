# BA-003 — one bounded q12 attempt and terminal disposition

**Outcome:** `OPEN_Q12_BRACKET_AVERAGING_WITHOUT_NC`. Neither universal IBA nor a fully admissible opposite limiting process was established.

## What the new exact flux theorem buys

For all actual q12/N admissible rate choices, the net equilibrium rate traffic across each critical parity level and the whole parity-compressed first-order operator are identical to native HB. This forces the exact conditional mean `E[G|m]` to equal the native conditional mean. Therefore the only possible failure of IBA must arise from sufficiently persistent, nontrivially **within-parity-fibre** fluctuations of `G`, or from invariant diagnostic components with distinct mean brackets. A counterfamily cannot merely choose a different parity-level aggregate activity: such a choice violates the exact cut identities.

## Why the finite example does not settle q12

The six-node reversible wheel provides legal finite-graph `h!=0` and a diverging Poisson cost as intercomponent conductance tends to zero. It lacks the actual q12 count domain and Gibbs law, the exact native q12 reflected drift vector at every state, and a single uniform rate cap verified along all N of the true critical sequence. Consequently it is a **negative proof control** for BA-002, not a candidate with a different critical limit.

The inherited localized genuine q12 ten-type wheel spike changes `G` on a set of vanishing Gibbs weight and therefore does not disprove IBA. An arbitrary frozen latent bit, graph, external noise or modified `pi_D,N` is forbidden by the order and was not introduced.

## Exact unpaid all-N lemma

Let `C_N` range over all conductance matrices indexed by the actual MT count states with `c_xy>=0`, `c_xy=c_yx`, support on reflected single replacements, `sum_y c_xy/pi_x<=CN` and the exact statewise flux constraints `sum_y c_xy(m_y-m_x)=pi_x L_native^D m(x)`. With `G_N=N/pi_x sum_y c_xy(m_y-m_x)^2`, show, for every fixed S,

`E_pi sup_(t<=S)|int_0^t[G_N(X_(sqrt(N)s))-E_pi G_N]ds| ->0`,

or exhibit a **specific** sequence `C_N` satisfying every condition for which that expectation stays bounded away from zero (or another genuinely different bracket/process limit).

The stronger *sufficient* decomposition is to prove `||P_ker L_N r_N||_2->0` and `sup_f[2<r_N-h_N,f>-D_N(f,f)]=o(sqrt(N))`. These are useful proof targets, not asserted necessary conditions. A valid proof may bypass them.
