## 2. Minimal carrier and composition testbed

Start with finite configuration spaces, joint probability laws, stochastic
kernels or bijections, and composition of transformations. A graph specifies
allowed interaction supports without an embedding. **Its support is supplied,
not already emergent locality.** Use categories only to organize these defined
maps; hypergraphs only if a specified pairwise consistency test fails; causal
sets only after an event order exists; Hilbert/operator structures only behind
the quantum gate.

M = number of effective cells, N = copies inside each cell, q = internal label
count, initially 12. Cells are internal effective patterns of the informational
nadsoliton, not an additional substrate or multiple primordial beings.

One explicit PM testbed, with p_x an empirical distribution and m_x=X7^T p_x:

```text
P_Gamma,N ∝ product_x [N!/product_j n_xj! * 12^(-N)]
  * exp{ N g/2 sum_x ||m_x||²
         - N kappa/2 sum_{unordered edges xy} w_xy ||m_x-m_y||² }.
```

Gamma, w_xy>=0, kappa and a common internal label frame are added premises.
The feature metric must be carried under coordinate changes. Compare one
equally explicit alternative interaction; do not adjust a new law for every
desired outcome.

Required elementary checks:

1. kappa=0 reproduces the exact product of MP7 finite-N ensembles.
2. The joint state space admits correlations; it is not restricted to products
   of marginals. Marginalization may create higher-order interactions.
3. Cell/label relabelings act covariantly. Relative internal frame maps are
   explicit if labels in different cells are not identified.
4. For fixed M, derive the N→∞ rate functional
   `sum_x V_g(p_x)+(kappa/2)sum_xy w_xy||m_x-m_y||²` from this law.
5. Two cells then three test composition and marginals. N=1 tests algebra only:
   A7 has constant diagonal, so its one-copy onsite law has no collective
   transition. N=2 has 78 occupation states/cell, 6,084 pair states and 474,552
   triple states. Collective phases require larger N or a controlled limit.

Construct two local detailed-balance generators with different symmetric
attempt factors for the same equilibrium law. Construct a bijective control
separately. Equilibrium does not select their rates or trajectories.

Only the zero-coupling limit automatically inherits the isolated-cell phase
diagram. Do not transfer MP7 global alignment, coexistence or root exhaustion
to the interacting network without a new argument.

Primal/dual equality of minimum values is **not off-shell equality of their
potentials**. The displayed joint law directly yields a primal functional of
the p_x. Either work with that functional or derive its joint auxiliary dual.
For this particular coupling the block interaction on feature means is
`K_Gamma=(g I_M-kappa L_Gamma) tensor I7`. A real Gaussian auxiliary transform
requires its positive definiteness (a sufficient condition is
`g>kappa lambda_max(L_Gamma)`); its quadratic dual cost uses K_Gamma inverse,
not an arbitrary sum of uncoupled Phi terms. When this condition fails the
finite-label Gibbs law still exists: it is the real Gaussian representation
that is unavailable. This distinction is essential for a correct stiffness,
fluctuation and field-theory derivation.

## 3. Informational continuity is not one property

| Property | Test | Does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Invertibility | Two-sided inverse on the specified state set | Locality or physical time |
| Injectivity | Distinct inputs remain distinct | Positive/stochastic inverse |
| Recoverability | One admissible R satisfies R K p=p for all p in a stated family | Recovery for every state or discarded environment |
| Sufficiency | Statistic retains likelihood information for a parameter family | Complete microstate conservation |
| Entropy conservation | H(Kp)=H(p), with pointwise versus all-state quantifiers | Preservation of all correlations |
| Mutual-information preservation | Specify reference register and preparations | State-independent conservation by default |
| Invariant Q | Nonconstant, operationally relevant Q survives | Complete information preservation |
| Classical reversible channel | K has a stochastic inverse on the full simplex | QM; these finite-simplex automorphisms are permutations |
| Isometric/unitary extension | State space, complex structure and ancilla specified | Derivation of those additions |

An exact warning from FIN itself: let `a_j=cos(pi*j/3)` and
`p_±=u0±a/24`. Both distributions are positive and distinct, but Fourier
orthogonality gives `X7^T p_+=X7^T p_-=0`. Thus the full-simplex mean map is
noninjective. This refutes universal preservation by **that** coarse map, not
restricted-family sufficiency. Strict convexity of log Z can instead provide
injectivity within the full-rank Gibbs exponential family.

A reversible dilation must count its ancilla/bath, memory capacity and reset
cost. Hidden history is not free. Preservation of total probability or Hilbert
norm is too weak to certify persistence of a localized pattern.

## 5. Causality, influence and time

A successor relation X→S(X) need not be antisymmetric. Ergodic reversible
chains have cycles; collapsing their strongly connected components can yield
one trivial node. Attaching an external step counter does not derive time.

Use events and supported transformations. Disjoint updates should commute,
or their failure to commute must be quantified. Different linear orderings of
independent events should give equivalent records. Remove connecting update
paths and test whether an intervention still changes a remote marginal.

Define I_xy(t), for example, as a supremum total-variation change at y from two
allowed preparations differing only at x. Fix the other preparation variables.
Then distinguish:

- discrete finite-support rules: possible exact cone in **update depth**;
- continuous-time jumps: generally nonzero probability of arbitrarily many
  jumps in any positive time; seek exponential/factorial tails, not exact cones;
- dense/long-range generators: potentially no uniform finite-speed scaling;
- quantum Lieb–Robinson estimates: conditional bounds with their own locality
  hypotheses, not automatic FIN or exact Lorentz cones.
  [Nachtergaele–Sims](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0506030).

A clock needs additive/composable duration, agreement between independently
constructed clocks, controlled path dependence and calibration. Reversibility
does not choose an arrow. Low-entropy boundary conditions, reservoirs and
coarse observation remain candidate PM premises.

## 6. Geometry admission

Graph distance measures supplied support. Fisher geometry measures state
distinguishability. Covariance geometry depends on metric/normalization and
may be degenerate. Diffusion distance needs a Markov generator and a scale.
Effective resistance requires a connected positive graph Laplacian; a generic
Green difference can be a squared distance instead. Spectra can be geometrically
nonunique. Optimal transport imports a ground cost. Entanglement distance
first needs a quantum tensor/state bridge; mutual information is not entanglement.

Required gates: positive separation on the correct quotient; justified symmetry
or asymmetry; triangle/path property; relabeling and coordinate covariance;
agreement with interventions; dimension stable over an expanding scale window;
coarse-graining consistency; and sensitivity to physically distinguishable fine
modes. Compare paths/rings, bounded-degree expanders and dense controls.

Do not retune thresholds until every graph looks low-dimensional. Diffusion
maps show a construction from selected kernels/transitions, not a spacetime
source. [Coifman et al.](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.0500334102).

## 8. A traceable effective-field term

For the declared mean-difference interaction, take a paid branch and normalized
slow coordinate xi with `m(xi)=m_*+u xi+...`. Its quadratic coupling is

`(kappa ||u||²/2) sum_xy w_xy (xi_x-xi_y)²`.

Thus stiffness has a source **conditional on that interaction**. Eliminating
hard modes requires a gap and a coupling/gap error bound; the resulting Schur
complement need not be nearest-neighbor. A controlled refinement limit is
needed before replacing the graph term by a continuum gradient.

Gradient kinetics gives relaxation poles; a Hamiltonian lift adds momenta and
kinetic normalization and may give waves. A static inverse Hessian is not
propagation. A frequency gap is not rest mass without action units and a
dispersion bridge. At first-order coexistence the restoring curvature need
not vanish; the fold is a different, metastable regime.

## 9. Gravity stopping point

Before gravity language require state-dependent geometry, reciprocal
backreaction, a nonproxy conserved source, causal propagation, approximately
universal probe response and a stable low-energy limit. Approximate Lorentz
symmetry must survive comparisons between species and refinement corrections,
not one fitted dispersion curve. FIN is currently far from this gate; no
Einstein–Hilbert coefficient-fitting campaign is recommended.
