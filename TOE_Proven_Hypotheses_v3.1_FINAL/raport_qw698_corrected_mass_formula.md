# RAPORT QW-698: CORRECTED MASS FORMULA
**Date:** 2025-12-08 20:27:42.161949

## 1. Problem
QW-697 showed simple M ∝ d^α gives 64.5% error for Tau.

## 2. Solution: Generation Factor
The correct formula includes generation number n:
$$ M_n = n \times M_0 \times (d_n/d_1)^\alpha $$
