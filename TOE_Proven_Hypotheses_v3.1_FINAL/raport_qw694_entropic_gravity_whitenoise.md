# RAPORT QW-694: ENTROPIC GRAVITY FROM WHITE NOISE
**Date:** 2025-12-08 18:49:43.367703

## 1. Question
Can gravitational attraction emerge from a **pure White Noise vacuum** (QW-693)?

## 2. Method
- Generated 3D white noise field (N=256).
- Inserted two 'topological defects' (mass sources) at varying distances R.
- Measured entropy gradient dS/dx between defects.
- Calculated entropic force F = T * dS/dx (averaged over 50 realizations).

## 4. Verdict
### ❌ NO ENTROPIC FORCE DETECTED
The 'force' is indistinguishable from random noise. White Noise vacuum does NOT generate gravity.

### ⚠️ IMPLICATIONS FOR FIN THEORY
If the vacuum is truly White Noise (QW-693), then:
1. **Gravity cannot be purely entropic** from the vacuum itself.
2. **Defects (masses) must create local order** that then generates entropy gradients.
3. Forces may emerge from **topological interactions**, not vacuum entropy.
