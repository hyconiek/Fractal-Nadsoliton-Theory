# Localized feedback can be absorbed; extended continuum source remains

LF splits QH's residual into localized interactions and four extended
terms: -12Kvu, -6v^2u, -g4u^3 and -g6u^5, with the continuous projection.
All fields are the whole actual physical continuum fields.

Localized interactions obey a positive-weight forcing bound Cepsilon E,
where E is the actual local continuum energy norm. Direct finite-window
absorption has a coefficient of order sqrt(S), which small epsilon alone
does not improve. An exponential time weight on the analysis norm gives
a coefficient of order mu^-1/2. Choosing a fixed sufficiently large mu
and combining the bound with VD's cumulative virial estimate absorbs the
localized feedback without assuming the desired energy bound.

On each fixed critical interval the result is

    integral E^2 <= C exp(2mu S)[epsilon^2+integral |q_X|^2].

The extended-source trace q_X remains unbounded at the required order.
The exponential factor is an estimate constant, not a physical damping
rate. A translated-packet norm-class control shows why global smallness
alone cannot supply the positive weights needed for the extended source.
The actual initial data are never changed.
