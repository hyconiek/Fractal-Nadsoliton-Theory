  test, two frozen operators and one joint sequence. It is one-sided
  sufficiency, not an optimal/necessary sample requirement.
- Native sign-only transfer, all Gaussian resource laws, full path/observer
  equivalence, mutual information and entropy claims remain unproved.
- No useful N0, native numerical probability at a fixture N, practical
  observation budget or physical runtime follows from the existential bound.
- Corrector/OU functions are analysis tools, not new microscopic laws or
  physical noise. Phi need not be Markov and is not extra classifier data.
- Parent preparation/exit errors transfer only the final bounded event.
  No survivor postselection or unbounded-moment transport through TV occurs.
- Source point-tail/floating-floor checks are not general arithmetic proofs.
  The review supplies explicit geometric tail bounds and exact finite roots;
  asymptotic estimates remain analytical, not mechanized or peer reviewed.

  Its common-scale invariance does not license independent channel gains,
  offsets or a real common-gain detector. Its new inequalities are not admitted.

  ontology/selector/legacy-role, FIN-M0/B4/task335 or QM/GR/SM/L_total/ToE gate
