# Nonconclusions for KR

* The sufficient process theorem is conditional on NC (or separately paid IBA) and on **accepted** MT/UF spectral, local geometry, drift and moment inputs. It does not prove that NC is automatic.
* Exact stationary scalar noise mean and weak conditional noise law do not imply pointwise concentration or time-integrated bracket control.
* The q4/N2 wheel is an exact finite rational negative control; it is neither a q12/A7 critical kinetic family nor a counterexample to the stationary quartic diffusion limit.
* The user-reported local wheel spike has the same negative-control restriction. No original source archive or full current repository was verified.
* No new time scale, source of A7, independently selected activity, rate rule, physical mediator, empirical instrument, quantum or gravitational dynamics is derived.
* No diagnostic iid-start theorem, full operator convergence, finite-N numerical `N0`, uniform large-horizon theorem, finite-data readout limit, or rate of convergence is obtained.
* AS-002/003 and KB-002/003 stay inactive: mathematical rigidity does not independently justify a physical activity provider.
