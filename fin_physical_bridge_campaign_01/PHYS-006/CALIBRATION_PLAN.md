# Calibration plan
1. N=1,g=0: uniformity, readout-confusion matrix, RNG autocorrelation.
2. Clamp one N=2 label; sweep pre-registered logit patterns and compare all 12 conditional probabilities to softmax.
3. Verify self-removal and label permutation covariance.
4. Measure attempt-rate distribution, delays and serial/parallel scheduling.
5. Repeat on independent calibration records; validation records remain unopened.
6. Go/no-go for static test: calibrated conditional prediction must fit within pre-registered TV envelope. Dynamic FIN test requires a stronger rate/schedule contract and is not authorized here.
