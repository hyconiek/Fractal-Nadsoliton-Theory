# CAMPAIGN-I NONCONCLUSIONS

- This campaign does not establish that A7 is necessary in nature.
- It does not establish that the seven-dimensional feature factor is physical space.
- It does not derive a physical source of g, theta, generator-time units, the actuator or measurement apparatus.
- It does not derive QM, GR, the Standard Model, particle content, SI units or a theory of everything.
- A programmed A7 sampler would validate an implementation/model bridge, not the origin or fundamental status of A7.
- Shared equilibrium does not make heat-bath, Metropolis and Barker dynamically equivalent.
- Published Potts/p-bit/oscillator hardware does not by itself demonstrate exact q=12 A7 leave-one-out FIN sampling.
- The N=2 preregistered sample counts are conditional design figures, not laboratory guarantees.
- PHYS-005 returned `NO_NEW_SOURCE`; no additional fundamental principle was invented to convert that into a positive result.
- `Gamma=B4` remains subject to the current repository intake's arithmetic proof-accounting gates.
- Task337 remains WIP and task335 remains unopened.
