## 1. Research direction: what should change now

The previous campaign made real progress: boundary-Ising and intraparity
lemmas, global tails, a repaired local safe union, and 120 corrected local
phase-root certificates are available. The remaining work is not another
generic source audit and should not default to an indefinite chain of one-box
extensions guided by an optimizer hitting artificial walls.

The next campaign has two primary lanes:

1. **A genuinely global four-amplitude theorem**, beginning with a deliberately
   weaker rational ceiling that is sufficient for a useful Hessian statement,
   then attempting the sharp ceiling only with a complete domain strategy.
2. **Global phase-root exhaustion for the fixed, exactly declared amplitude
   fixture**, replacing numerical saturation and unverified root neighborhoods
   with an actual complement proof.

A bounded secondary lane may improve a fixed-gain full-seven-coordinate
stationary/global result. It must not displace the primary lanes merely because
another numerical atlas is easier to generate.

### 1.1 Two distinct four-amplitude targets

Let sigma denote the accepted spectral expression, approximately
0.267443244229. Keep these targets separate in code, ledgers, and prose:

- **Target P — practical global ceiling:**
  `lambda2(M4(J)) <= tau0`, with exact `tau0=67/250=0.268`, for all four
  shared nonnegative fields J.
- **Target S — sharp global ceiling:**
  `lambda2(M4(J)) <= sigma` on the same domain, with an independently justified
  equality classification if one is claimed.

Both are currently open as global statements. Target P is not an already
known theorem. It is weaker than Target S and must never be presented as a
proof of Target S. Its advantage is a positive separation from the known
boundary equality value, which may remove the need to resolve very small
equality-neighborhood geometry before obtaining a useful global result.

If Target P is proved, then in the **four-amplitude Cartesian model**

`H4=I4/g-M4`

has at most one strictly negative eigenvalue for every supplied
`0<g<=250/67`. This follows because `1/g>=tau0`. The existing local event
near g=3.71834489812 lies below 250/67. Check the rational gain inequalities
explicitly in the implementation. Do not infer nonsingularity from a non-strict
ceiling at the endpoint g=250/67.

This conclusion is not a full-X7 theorem, a stationary-point census, a global
minimizer theorem, or a physical gain/source law. The full-seven-coordinate
everywhere index-one claim is already false. The unrestricted stationary-only
version is separately false at exact g=5.

### 1.2 What counts as a successful campaign

Any of the following is a meaningful primary success:

- a complete, replayable proof of Target P;
- a complete, replayable proof or certified admissible refutation of Target S;
- an exhaustive quartic or full phase census for the stated fixed fixture;
- a strictly stronger certified partial domain accompanied by a complete,
  machine-readable residual and a demonstrated reduction in proof complexity.

A certified counterexample is a valid result. A bounded attempt that leaves
explicit unresolved cells is also valid, but must not be titled global closure.
All 64 tasks need an honest disposition, not necessarily a positive theorem.

## 4. Mathematical contract and formulas to verify before reuse

### 4.1 Coordinates and probabilities

Use spectral sector labels lambda3,...,lambda6 from the full strict Laplacian.
Let C4 contain normalized cosine modes 3,4,5 and the alternating mode 6.
Let X7 contain both cosine/sine components of modes 3,4,5 and the alternating
mode. Do not silently switch between these feature spaces.

In the boundary-aligned compact chart,

```text
r = exp(-2 J3)
s = exp(-3 J4/2)
t = exp(-J5/2)
y = exp(-2 J6)
```

the twelve unnormalized weights, in label order j=0,...,11, are

```text
1,
sqrt(r) s t^(2+sqrt(3)) y,
r s t,
sqrt(r) t^2 y,
s t^3,
sqrt(r) s t^(2-sqrt(3)) y,
r t^4,
sqrt(r) s t^(2-sqrt(3)) y,
s t^3,
sqrt(r) t^2 y,
r s t,
sqrt(r) s t^(2+sqrt(3)) y.
```

Normalize by their sum. The anchor is exactly one, so the denominator never
vanishes on the compact closure. Independently verify this formula against
the original field definition before deriving new bounds.

Put

```text
A_even = 1 + 2 s t^3 + r t^4 + 2 r s t
B_odd  = 2 sqrt(r) [s t^(2+sqrt(3)) + t^2 + s t^(2-sqrt(3))]
q_even = A_even / (A_even + y B_odd)
e_odd  = y B_odd / (A_even + y B_odd).
```

The conservative compact residual hull after removing the accepted tails is

```text
r in [1/900, 1],
s in [1/128, 1],
t in [1/9, 1],
y in [1/1000000, 1].
```

Closed endpoints are intentionally retained: overlaps with safe tails are
harmless, but missing a boundary is not. The unresolved set is a subset of
this hull after removing the **proved** union, not the buffered search masks.
Exact algebraic equalities and irrational powers still have to be respected.

### 4.2 Local masks use a different coordinate map

The accepted masks use

```text
x = r-r_star,
u = 1-s,
v = 1-t,
e = 1-q_even,
```

with r_star itself depending on the strict spectral tuple. A compact cell can
be declared covered by a local mask only after its entire interval image is
contained in that mask, with r_star uncertainty paid. Testing the midpoint
or any single possible value of e is insufficient.

If a compact cell is covered by a **union** of masks but by no single mask,
produce a finite containment/subdivision proof. Do not convexify the union.

### 4.3 Threshold-dependent Schur reduction

For an explicit threshold a>lambda6/12,

```text
M4 = W_par + b b^T,
eta_a = 1 - b6^2/a > 0,
Mtilde_a = W_par[345,345] + b345 b345^T / eta_a.
```

The negative inertia counts of `a I4-M4` and `a I3-Mtilde_a` agree.
If a changes from sigma to tau0 or to a smaller comparison threshold,
**rebuild Mtilde_a and eta_a**. The transform made for sigma is not the
correct numerical spectral-gap matrix for another threshold.

When forming the characteristic test, introduce a dummy variable z and use

`P_a(z)=det(z I3-Mtilde_a)`.

Compute P_a(a), its z-derivative at z=a, and the shifted trace coefficient
while **holding Mtilde_a fixed**. Differentiating the composite function
`det(a I3-Mtilde_a)` with respect to a also differentiates the Schur transform
and gives a different expression. This is a specific implementation hazard.

The disjunction `P_a(a)<=0 or P_a'(a)>=0` may be used only where the required
positive shifted-trace coefficient and PSD hypotheses are proved. It is not
an unconditional black-box test over every large compact cell.

### 4.4 A second, often cheaper, local proof method

For any rational 4-by-3 full-column-rank matrix B, proving

`B^T(a I4-M4)B` positive semidefinite

is sufficient to show at most one eigenvalue of M4 exceeds a. B need not be
orthonormal; verify its rank, and use congruence/inertia rather than treating
its eigenvalues as unchanged physical eigenvalues.

Choose B numerically as a candidate near a cell center, then freeze it as
rational and certify the compressed form on the whole cell. Allow different
cells to use different B. The historical failure of a **single fixed global
hyperplane** does not invalidate a correctly certified cell-adaptive method.

### 4.5 The phase-complement issue to address explicitly

The archived phase-complement script uses `ROOT_RADIUS=0.05`, rounded phase
coefficients, and floating endpoints. The accepted root boxes are radius
10^-7. A small unique-root certificate does not justify removing the entire
radius-0.05 neighborhood from a new complement search.

The stored old run removed **zero** root-neighborhood leaves, so do not claim
that it already lost those annuli. However, its reusable code path is unsafe
for a future complete proof unless the larger neighborhoods receive separate
uniqueness/exclusion certificates. Its 396 “safe” and 1272 unresolved leaves
also require revalidation for the corrected exact fixture; the old rounded
coefficient proof flags are not new exact inputs.

Each R7N task needs:

```text

input hashes, command, source version, random seeds where used,

```

Execution statuses: NOT_STARTED, RUNNING, DONE, RESOURCE_STOP,
BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY, SUPERSEDED_BY_COUNTEREXAMPLE, OUT_OF_SCOPE.

Scientific statuses: EXACT_PROVED, INTERVAL_CERTIFIED, CONDITIONAL_LEMMA,
NUMERICAL_REPRODUCED, NUMERICAL_NEW, COUNTEREXAMPLE_CERTIFIED,
COUNTEREXAMPLE_NUMERICAL, UNRESOLVED, NOT_A_SCIENTIFIC_CLAIM.

DONE is not a synonym for proved. A blocked or refuted dependency is not a
licence to assume its conclusion. Continue independent ready tasks instead.

### 5.3 Resource limits and anti-loop policy

Use these conservative defaults, subject to the actual environment:

- S: at most 5 minutes per run and 2 GiB.
- M: at most 20 minutes per run and 4 GiB.
- L: at most 60 minutes per run and 6 GiB, with durable checkpoints.

Keep long-running processes interruptible and checkpoint every 30–60 seconds.
Do not launch duplicate heavy jobs. Initial covers should have a 10,000-leaf
pilot; a justified next pass may use at most 200,000 leaves within one L run.
Further expansion requires a written complexity/resource decision and the
appropriate authority, not an automatic larger grid.

Allow at most three consecutive hand-added wall-extension tasks without a
measurable reduction in the certified residual or global-cover difficulty.
After that, change the proof architecture or return a bounded no-progress
certificate. The next label FR224 is not itself a scientific reason to work
on that particular wall.

A smaller cell rejected by a conservative checker is not a counterexample.
A negative sampled maximum is not a global bound. Reaching a timeout is not
mathematical falsification. Record these distinctions in the result table.

## 6. Implementation recipes for difficult steps

### 6.1 Exact compact-cell classifier

Use a dispatch like the following, with proof reasons stored for every leaf:

```text
classify(cell, threshold):
    validate_chart_and_exact_endpoints(cell)
    if subset_of_an_accepted_tail(cell):
        return SAFE_BY_TAIL with dependency certificate
    if proved_subset_of_safe_mask_union(cell):
        return SAFE_BY_UNION with containment/split proof
    M4 = rigorous_covariance_enclosure(cell)
    if trace_upper(M4) <= 2*threshold:
        return SAFE_BY_TRACE
    B = proposed_rational_rank_three_basis(cell)
    if rank(B)==3 and interval_PSD(B^T(threshold*I-M4)B):
        return SAFE_BY_COMPRESSION
    Mtilde = schur_matrix_rebuilt_at(threshold, cell)
    if paid_characteristic_disjunction(Mtilde, threshold):
        return SAFE_BY_CHARACTERISTIC
    if a_certified_admissible_violation_exists(cell):
        return COUNTEREXAMPLE_CERTIFIED
    return UNRESOLVED
```

Do not use the ordinary trace bound when a covariance enclosure is not tied to
an actual PSD covariance model. The model is PSD analytically, but arbitrary
independent interval entries need not form a PSD matrix; this distinction
matters when deriving further inequalities from the enclosure itself.

A cell may be safe for tau0 but unresolved for sigma. Store the threshold as
part of its identity, not as a mutable global setting.

### 6.2 Coverage proof: measure is not enough

Store the exact root rectangle and a binary split tree. Each internal node
records an axis and exact split point; its children must cover the entire
parent with the agreed closed-boundary convention. Each terminal node records
its inequality or valid dependency inclusion. A global proof must connect
the compact root rectangle to all accepted unbounded tails.

When importing local masks, do not compare numeric volumes or sampled corner
containment unless an interval/affine theorem makes that sufficient. The
y-to-e map is nonlinear and depends on r,s,t. The separate conversion
x=r-r_star depends on the strict spectral inputs through the equality anchor.
All containment hypotheses must be paid.

The old raw FR223 list is a locator database. The consolidated 99 direct plus
18 repair certificates are the proof database.

### 6.3 Candidate subspaces and eigenvalue decisions

Numerical eigenvectors are allowed to propose a cell-specific B. Rationalize
B, prove its rank by an exact nonzero minor, and certify the compressed
quadratic form using interval LDL or a verified bound. Do not turn a numerical
eigendecomposition into a theorem by printing extra digits.

For an alleged violation of a ceiling a, a rank-two basis U with

`U^T(M4-a I)U` positive definite

is a sufficient witness. Prove rank two and the sign on the whole chosen
parameter/root box. Two isolated large Rayleigh quotients are not sufficient
unless their cross term is controlled on the entire span.

### 6.4 Larger root neighborhoods require a separate theorem

Suppose a small certified box contains one root x_star and lies in a larger
convex chart B. One useful route to uniqueness in B is a fixed nonsingular
preconditioner Y satisfying a verified operator-norm bound

`sup_{x in B} ||I-Y J_F(x)|| < 1`.

Together with small-box existence and containment, this can justify excluding
additional roots from B. State the precise norm and theorem. Merely proving
J_F(x) nonsingular at every sampled point, or even pointwise on a region without
the necessary global argument, is not a global injectivity certificate.

If no larger uniqueness neighborhood is obtained, the annulus between radius
10^-7 and a convenient plotting radius remains part of the complement problem.
Do not silently erase it from the search.

### 6.5 Phase symmetry is fixture-dependent

For a label shift j->j+a, the k6 term changes by (-1)^a. With a fixed nonzero
alternating amplitude, only transformations preserving that sign act within
the same fixed-sign fixture. Derive the exact phase action and use it for
deduplication only after checking this condition.

A result for both signs may be related by the full D12 action; that does not
allow arbitrary full-D12 identifications inside one negative-sign phase torus.
Keep Cartesian, phase-coordinate and constrained-sphere Hessians separate.

### 6.6 Remainder transfer must use the actual exclusion domain

If a quartic complement cell has a certified gradient lower bound m and the
full-minus-quartic gradient remainder is at most epsilon throughout that same
cell, then epsilon<m excludes a full root there. Both bounds must use the same
coordinate scaling, fixture and norm.

A global numerical maximum of sampled remainder values is not epsilon. A
gradient gap outside unverified radius-0.05 neighborhoods is not a gap on the
true complement of the certified small root boxes. Match the domains exactly.

### 6.7 Precision, certificates and verification layers

Keep three layers separate:

1. Discovery: floating optimization, eigenvectors, empirical difficulty maps.
2. Proof objects: exact endpoints, coefficient intervals, root boxes, bases,
   contraction bounds, cover trees and analytic dependency statements.
3. Replay: recomputation of the proof inequalities and domain coverage.

Hash/schema checks protect provenance and shape, not mathematical truth.
Outward interval arithmetic protects enclosure, not the correctness of the
formula being enclosed. A correct proof needs both the model/formula derivation
and the checked numerical inequalities.
