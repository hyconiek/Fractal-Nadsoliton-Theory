## Abstract

This technical note studies a precisely defined twelve-site FIN matrix and the two canonical temporal calculi generated by its weighted graph Laplacian. Let (W) be the zero-diagonal real symmetric cyclic-distance matrix on (mathbb Z_{12}) sampled from

\[
K(d)=\frac{\cos(0.18575d+0.16250)}{1+d^{1.8}},
\]

using cyclic distances (d=1,ldots,6). Its common row sum is

\[
s=1.660307278766099
\]

to fifteen decimal places, and

\[
A=sI-W.
\]

All off-diagonal weights used at (N=12) are positive. Therefore (A) is a connected weighted graph Laplacian and

\[
\langle f,Af\rangle
=\frac12\sum_{x,y}W_{xy}|f_x-f_y|^2\ge 0.
\]

The same positive self-adjoint operator generates the unitary group

\[
U_t=e^{-itA}
\]

and the irreducible, symmetric, uniform-reversible Markov semigroup

\[
P_t=e^{-tA}.
\]

Although the two evolutions share Fourier eigenvectors and eigenvalues, their operational predictions are inequivalent. Markov diffusion has linear short-time escape, exact Chapman–Kolmogorov composition and exponential relaxation to the uniform law. Unitary position probabilities have quadratic short-time escape, interference, failure of population-level Chapman–Kolmogorov composition and no pointwise equilibrium.

The release includes a reproducible finite double-slit analogue. A coherent preparation at vertices (-2) and (+2) is compared with a perfectly distinguishing which-path record and with classical diffusion. The interference term is displayed across all twelve detectors and through time. At the first local maximum of total interference, (t_*=1.5525), the symmetry detector has unit ideal visibility; reducing the overlap of the two environment/apparatus records continuously suppresses the fringe.

The note also identifies the missing structure needed to convert the operator into an experimental model. The smallest established-style candidate is a **calibrated process–tester model**: a causal process tensor (quantum comb), an admissible tester containing preparations and instruments with classical records, and a clock/reference calibration. This object carries state, multitime dynamics, preparation, instruments, environmental memory, apparatus and records up to operational equivalence. The operator alone does not select a physical clock, dimensional scale, environment, apparatus, Born rule, orientation or temporal semantics.

No cosmological, Theory-of-Everything or continuum-physics claim is made.

## Plain-language significance

One finite operator can generate both wave-like and diffusion-like mathematics. That fact does not mean an observer turns one physical law into another. It means the static operator underdetermines the experiment. State preparation, phase reference, clock, environment, measurement instrument and stored record must be supplied before either family becomes a testable physical model.

The included double-slit analogue makes the distinction visible: coherent alternatives interfere; a which-path record removes the cross term; classical diffusion produces a different distribution. These are three operational protocols built around the same finite matrix.

## Double-slit simulation

The simulated slit modes are (a=10\equiv-2\pmod{12}) and (b=2). The coherent system–record state is

\[
|\Psi_\phi\rangle
=\frac{|a\rangle|E_a\rangle+e^{i\phi}|b\rangle|E_b\rangle}{\sqrt2},
\qquad \eta=\langle E_b|E_a\rangle.
\]

With (u_j(x,t)=\langle x|U_t|j\rangle), the detector law is

\[
p_{\phi,\eta}(x,t)
=\frac{|u_a|^2+|u_b|^2}{2}
+\operatorname{Re}\!\left(\eta e^{-i\phi}u_a\overline{u_b}\right).
\]

The figure compares:

1. coherent propagation, (eta=1);
2. a which-path record / incoherent mixture, (eta=0);
3. classical diffusion (P_t(\delta_a+\delta_b)/2).

This is a finite two-path interferometer on (mathbb Z_{12}), not a continuum optical diffraction calculation.

## Important finite-size limitation

The Markov conclusion uses positivity of all off-diagonal weights present at (N=12). The fixed profile becomes negative at distance (8):

\[
K(8)=-0.0017958784.
\]

A naive cyclic extension first includes that distance at (N=16), where the resulting off-diagonal matrix entry cannot be a Markov jump rate. Any truncation, rectification, absolute-value prescription or parameter running would be an additional axiom. The release therefore makes an exact (N=12) claim and no automatic continuum-limit claim.

## Release limitation statement

It does not establish that either evolution is the physical law of FIN, that the matrix is a measured Hamiltonian or Markov generator, or that any displayed dimensionless time corresponds to seconds. Physical realization requires an independently specified preparation, clock calibration, phase/reference frame, detector instrument, environmental coupling and empirical model comparison.
