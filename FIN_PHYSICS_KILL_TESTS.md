# FIN KILL TESTS

Date: 2026-09-23. Falsification gates for the proposed PHY campaign.
These are **tests to execute**, not assertions that all tested hypotheses
already failed. Existing counterexamples are explicitly identified below.

## Rules of engagement

Before each test freeze the candidate class, assumptions, observable, null
alternatives, approximation norm, tolerances and finite-size/time range.
Exact identities need exact or validated arithmetic; numerical nonconvergence
is not a nonexistence proof. A finite-size scout cannot certify an infinite
limit. Thresholds are set from theorem/error or measurement requirements,
not tuned to save the model after seeing outcomes.

Record PASS_IN_SCOPE, REFUTED_IN_SCOPE, INCONCLUSIVE or NOT_TESTED. Each
refutation applies to its declared lane/class, not every possible future FIN.
Two failed attempts with the same cause trigger a method stop. Reopening
requires a genuinely new premise, source or observable, plus a new claim ID.

## KT-01 — Target-dependent source or unconstrained composition

**Claim challenged:** A7 or the physical extension is derived, not encoded.

**Test:** Freeze source constraints before accessing target coefficients.
Count free spectral weights, graph/frame choices and inverse-design freedom.
Construct same-core extensions with incompatible predictions. Test a held-out
carrier/response without refitting. Existing coarse-source no-go results are
inputs, not discoveries to repeat.

**Kill outcome:** The target operator is hidden in the constraints, every
extension is allowed, or each carrier requires a new fit.

**Action:** Reject the claimed derivation/uniqueness. Keep an explicitly PM
effective model only if it has independent held-out predictions. If neither
source nor representative-class predictive content survives, suspend the
fundamental-kernel claim. Tasks PHY-003, PHY-011–013, PHY-030.

## KT-02 — Information preservation reduces to a trivial invariant

**Claim challenged:** H-R3 provides substantive informational continuity.

**Test:** State whether the target is invertibility, stochastic recovery,
sufficiency, entropy, reference mutual information or a specific invariant.
Apply the exact FIN mean-map witness
`p_±=u0±cos(pi*j/3)/24`, whose retained means coincide. Test a single recovery
map on the whole declared family, and bound error accumulation under B2∘B1.
Count every environment/history register.

**Kill outcome:** Only normalization/norm survives, recovery is preparation-
dependent or unbounded hidden memory supplies the lost information.

**Action:** Reject universal preservation by that map. Restricted sufficiency
may survive; classical reversibility is not quantum structure. Tasks PHY-004/008.

## KT-03 — Causality/time is only an imposed schedule

**Claim challenged:** Transformation order generates a nontrivial causal order
or clock rather than receiving one as input.

**Test:** Inspect cycles and strongly connected components. Permute independent
updates and compare records. Remove connecting supports and intervene locally.
Compare two clocks and different paths without a preferred global counter.

**Kill outcome:** The order collapses to one component, every event influences
every event directly, or all “before/after” content comes from an appended
step index. Inconsistent clock comparisons kill the corresponding time map.

**Action:** Retain a scheduled stochastic/deterministic model as PM if useful;
stop strict emergent-causality/time claims. Tasks PHY-005/009/011.

## KT-04 — No size-uniform locality or propagation bound

**Claim challenged:** Global connectedness supports physically local influence.

**Test:** Bound interventional influence for growing M with fixed rules.
Compare sparse, dense and long-range models. Distinguish exact discrete cones
from continuous-time tails; a continuous jump process can reach arbitrarily
far through rare multiple jumps at any positive time.

**Kill outcome:** Effective velocity/constants diverge with size, direct tails
violate the asserted bound, or an exact cone is claimed despite nonzero tails.

**Action:** Reject that causal/locality class or weaken it honestly to a paid
tail bound. Do not identify a fitted graph velocity with c. Tasks PHY-006/020/023.

## KT-05 — Geometry is a representation artifact

**Claim challenged:** Propagation/correlation defines a stable effective space.

**Test:** Check metric axioms on the intended quotient, coordinate covariance,
zero modes, positivity repairs and normalization. Compare admissible coarse
partitions and graph controls using the same rule; compare distance with actual
intervention response. Test an expanding dimension window, not one crossing.

**Kill outcome:** Triangle/separation fails, or geometry/dimension changes
arbitrarily with labels, regularizers, embeddings or coarse partitions.

**Action:** Retain a state-space metric/pseudometric only; stop spatial
interpretation until a new bridge exists. Do not retry thresholds until an
attractive dimension appears. Tasks PHY-007/008/019/020.

## KT-06 — Claimed universality or continuum is absent

**Claim challenged:** The finite mechanism represents more than its special
rank-seven/D12 instance.

**Test:** Compare normalized perturbations, broken symmetry, real q extensions
and inequivalent refinements. Include replicated labels as a null control.
Keep N, M, q and rank limits separate; examine extensivity and limit order.

**Kill outcome:** Only copied rows reproduce the result, admissible perturbations
destroy the mechanism, or no compatible stable limit exists in the declared
family. A failed sufficient bound alone is INCONCLUSIVE, not this outcome.

**Action:** Drop that universal/continuum claim. Do not build QFT on its failed
limit. A finite effective model can remain valid and interesting. Tasks PHY-015–020.

## KT-07 — No physical-type energy or stable transported pattern

**Claim challenged:** Relational cost acts as energy and localized patterns
can represent matter-like excitations.

**Test:** Require generator/conservation or explicit bath/work balance,
composition/extensivity and coarse-grained consistency. Define pattern identity
before searching; test actual motion, decay, pinning, perturbations and size
scaling. Keep pump-sustained regeneration separate from autonomous transport.

**Kill outcome:** The quantity has no required energy/balance role, or patterns
only exist as tuned copying, transient peaks or externally maintained shapes.

**Action:** Retain cost/Lyapunov and phase results but reject the proposed
energy or particle bridge. Failure of ballistic waves does not refute every
diffusive effective model; it refutes the stated propagation claim. Tasks
PHY-010/022–024.

## KT-08 — Quantum extension has only classical operational content

**Claim challenged:** Informational continuity or complexification provides
a quantum bridge.

**Test:** Prove the classical finite-simplex reversible-map restriction; define
preparations/instruments/composites. Test a two-path protocol against classical
coherent controls. For the stated local two-setting/two-outcome experiment,
test the 16-vertex classical correlation polytope with its independence and
no-postselection assumptions. Check noncommutativity/contextuality without
silently changing the operational scenario.

**Kill outcome:** All records have a classical stochastic model, apparent
violations use communication/postselection, or the result was obtained by
assuming the Born rule and tensor structure it was supposed to derive.

**Action:** Stop the claimed derivation of QM. An imported quantum model may
remain a PM platform comparison, but classical interference is not enough.
Tasks PHY-025/026. No observed quantum violation is claimed by this plan.

## KT-09 — Network deformation lacks gravity prerequisites

**Claim challenged:** A dynamical relational geometry can be called gravity-like.

**Test:** Check reciprocal backreaction, nonproxy conserved source, causal
response, approximately universal behavior of different probes, controlled
low-energy limit and required approximate Lorentz behavior.

**Kill outcome:** Geometry is assigned after the fact, changes independently
of conserved matter response, or different probes require arbitrary unrelated
metric laws.

**Action:** Stop gravity language and coefficient fitting. Return to the
specific missing prerequisite only if a new source/input exists. PHY-027.

## KT-10 — No identifiable, non-tautological physical prediction

**Claim challenged:** The extension predicts measurable physics economically.

**Test:** Derive scale/rate gauge freedoms; separate calibration and validation.
Freeze a low-dimensional parameter family and at least three independent
held-out observables with identifiable sensitivity and power against controls.
Count hidden functions, graph choices, detector freedoms and boundary tuning.

**Kill outcome:** The target fixes its own unit/parameter, every observable
requires another fit, simpler controls are indistinguishable, or PM/PB links
to measurements are absent. Synthetic self-agreement is not EV.

**Action:** Do not call the result a physical prediction. If this persists
across the preregistered admissible extension class, keep FIN as a finite
mathematical/statistical model and suspend its fundamental-physics claim.
Tasks PHY-014/017/028–030.

## Programme-level stopping decision

An isolated lane failure is not a proof that every FIN extension is impossible.
Nevertheless, the project should **stop expanding its physical claims** if its
admitted classes cannot produce (i) a constrained composition/source law,
(ii) a stable influence/geometry regime when spatial claims are made, and
(iii) a low-parameter operational prediction. Additional formalism or more
digits do not repair these failures. Reopening needs a new typed object,
independent physical input or empirical state change, not a renamed old lane.

No current result in this document announces EV, quantum emergence, gravity
or a successful particle model. These tests protect the programme from such
premature promotion.
