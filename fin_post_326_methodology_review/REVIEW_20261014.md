## Generative roadmap: one reproducible next question

Marginalization supplies a concrete question rather than an assumption of exact pairwise closure. For N surviving labelled copies after eliminating one of N+1 copies, the log weight is, up to a constant,

`g/[2(N+1)] n^T A7 n + log sum_j exp[g/(N+1) (A7 n)_j]`.

The N+1 denominator must be retained; the known multinomial factor is added only when switching to count states. For 4→3 labelled copies, use the eight configurations `(a,b,c)` with `a∈{0,1}`, `b∈{0,2}`, `c∈{0,4}`. The replay's alternating third difference is `+0.27127058458855` with plus sign at `(0,0,0)` (the opposite convention gives minus). A sum of constant, one-body and pairwise log weights has zero such contrast. This finite numerical witness therefore excludes exact pairwise-only representation for this example; it does not prove a compact polynomial RG closure, a fixed point or FIN-specific universality.

The justified next generative sequence is: exact marginalization -> identify generated operators -> specify a coarse/truncation map -> quantify probability/observable error -> compare normalized countermodels -> iterate only under controlled closure/memory -> freeze a held-out prediction. Equilibrium coarse-graining, stochastic evolution and physical time are separate maps. Relative entropy and Shannon entropy must be compared on well-defined probability spaces; marginal entropy does not universally grow when variables are discarded.

## Existing FIN-M0 statement requires evidence qualification

The working AGENTS draft referred to `fin_m0_simulator/`, Kaggle execution and an independently replayed clique experiment. No such directory or source/output package is available in this checkout. Those execution and sweep claims cannot be accepted from the prose alone. The draft also gave the wrong sign and strictness for the triangle gradient. For symmetric zero-diagonal nonnegative W with independent edge coordinate w_ij,

`d[-alpha Tr(W^3)/6]/d w_ij = -alpha (W^2)_ij <= 0`.

It can be zero (for example W=0 or disconnected edges with no common neighbor). Thus the triangle term rewards filling links; the complete graph globally maximizes triangle count, but this alone proves neither one-step saturation nor uniqueness of every noisy/memory attractor. The memory term and EMA dynamics must be included, and discretization/projection effects checked. Sweep, no-SOC and no-soliton conclusions require actual trajectories, specified statistics and bounds; they cannot be derived from that gradient sign alone. The earlier unaudited execution assertions are superseded by this evidence-qualified statement.
