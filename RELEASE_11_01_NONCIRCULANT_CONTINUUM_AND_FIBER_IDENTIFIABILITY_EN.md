## Abstract

Release 11.01 reports six research rounds, ST1542–ST1631, extending the
conditional FIN Operational/State discriminator beyond circulant uncertainty
and finite nuisance grids.

For positive self-adjoint graph Laplacians \(A,B\), Duhamel bounds give

\[
\|e^{-tB}-e^{-tA}\|\le t\|B-A\|,
\qquad
\|e^{-itB}-e^{-itA}\|\le t\|B-A\|.
\]

Consequently every global return-gap maximizer remains in \([0.59,0.60]\)
whenever

\[
\|B-A\|<9.720652006795929\times10^{-8}.
\]

This is a noncirculant location theorem, not a uniqueness theorem and not a
dephasing-generator perturbation theorem.

The continuous bidirectional-KL minimax value for the declared four times and
nuisance rectangle is certified by an adaptive interval lower cover and a
two-point dual upper bound:

\[
0.05\le V_*\le0.05370588868619479.
\]

The exact optimizing weights remain open.

For a uniform continuous prior on the nuisance rectangle,

\[
E_n=\int L_{\theta,n}\,\pi(d\theta)
\]

is a nonnegative mean-one martingale under the classical null; crossing 100
has anytime Type-I error at most one percent. A positive 7-by-9
Gauss–Legendre construction supplies a valid 63-component finite-mixture
implementation. It does not equal the exact integral by claim.

Independent classical and quantum affine calibration boxes with up to five
percent uniform preparation contamination and two percent false-positive/
false-negative rates retain disjoint base return ranges. A complete composite
cover proves

\[
\mathrm{RSS}\ge0.022937735365240054.
\]

For the refined fiber, classical heat return
\((1+e^{-2qt})/2\) globally identifies \(q\ge0\) from one nontrivial time,
whereas quantum return \(\cos^2(qt)\) is aliased outside a chosen monotone
branch. A two-time interval cover proves heat/unitary separation for
\(q\in[0.1,2]\):

\[
\mathrm{RSS}\ge0.009270580748993435.
\]

At \(q=0\), both channels return one for all times and are exactly
indistinguishable.
