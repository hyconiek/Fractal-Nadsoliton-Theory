- No strict natural source of evolution, A7, carrier, Gibbs pi, entropy
  principle, hidden preparation or physical clock is derived. The user-
  authorized hypothesis is explicit additional modelling input.
- The rate shape was already known. The new theorem concerns the actual
  supplied spectator-family limit, its error budgets and path topology.
- Fixed-grid records converge in TV, and paths converge after small J1
  time warps. Full continuous-path TV does not vanish; information/filter/
  posterior or arbitrary timestamp-decision convergence is not inferred.
- The visible carrier and pi are fixed. No interacting full-N mean-field,
  UF critical SDE, metastable window or native iid-start theorem is imported.
  An explicit rate cap and shared Gibbs law do not pay KR drift matching.
- Uniformization is an auxiliary construction of the derived finite CTMC,
  not a Poisson bath/control law added to the finite source.
- Gauge transformations must include the spectator completion. Changing D
  alone can alter relative activities. Non-adopted diagonal examples are
  countercontrols, not a fit to rescue HB or a new physical model.
- Count square-root factors and normalized activity are not quantum
  operators, physical particles or a conserved-energy/geometry derivation.
- Native HB/MET/BAR clocks and prefactors are preserved. The derived SQRT
  law is a distinct conditional limit; its HB mismatch is not concealed.
- Exact small fixtures are not actual numerical A7 data, empirical evidence,
  a peer review or proof-assistant certification. No spending, experiment,
  external messages, subagent, stopped heavy algebra or commit/push.
- All source/units/incidence/conservation/calibration/EV, nadsoliton ontology,
  strict/legacy/selector/role-transfer, FIN-M0/B4/task335/task337 and
