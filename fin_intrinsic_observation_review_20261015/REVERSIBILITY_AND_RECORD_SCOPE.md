# Equilibrium observation is not an emergent thermodynamic arrow

Date: 2026-10-15. Analytical corollary, conditional on the admitted local
stationary comparison; not a claim about preparation or physical storage.

The auxiliary reflected labelled process is reversible in the lifted local
Gibbs law. Its stationary path law on a fixed interval is invariant under
time reversal. Parity is an even state function, with no momentum variable
to reverse. For an integrated path Y(s)=integral_0^s f(x(r))dr the induced
reversal is

    (R Y)(s)=Y(S)-Y(S-s),

not `Y(S-s)` with the original origin retained. This map commutes with
projection/integration of the reversed microscopic path. A deterministic
time-reversal-equivariant pushforward of a reversible law is reversible.
Thus the stationary integrated record carries no intrinsic path-law arrow
under this stated reversal. Its path-law relative entropy against reversal
is zero for the exactly stationary comparison.

The limit has the same property: stationary quartic Z is reversible and
the independent Brownian observation increment is reversible under the
displayed map. Learning Z from the past and preferring a causal estimator
do not change this unconditional path-law symmetry.

The actual post-burn-in unreflected law is close in TV to that comparison
over the admitted finite scaled horizon. Its time-reversal TV asymmetry
is bounded by twice that path-comparison error and therefore tends to zero.
Small TV alone does **not** imply convergence of relative entropy or
microscopic entropy production; those stronger claims remain unpromoted.

This does not deny the nonstationary iid preparation, controller entropy,
an explicitly modeled recording medium or physical arrows of time. None
of those is derived by integrating a tagged history. No extra drive or
storage degree of freedom is inserted to evade this corollary.
