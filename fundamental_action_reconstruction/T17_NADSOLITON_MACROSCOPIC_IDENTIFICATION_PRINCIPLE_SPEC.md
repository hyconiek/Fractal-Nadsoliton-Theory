## Principle statement

### Informal statement

The single nadsoliton remains the primordial information-bearing object of the
theory.

Within that ontology:

1. `K_legacy_ont` is a macroscopic/effective identification tool used to
   recover nadsoliton signatures from the phenomenological side,
2. `K_strict_gate` is the strict source-topology working kernel used on the
   later strict route near the source limit,
3. therefore the absence of one absorption/compression/phase-merging theorem
   between those two role layers is not, by itself, a failure of ToE closure,
4. it is an admissible scale-separation outcome unless and until a separate
   bridge theorem is exported.

## Physical meaning

`T17` fixes one role split:

### 1. Legacy kernel role

`K_legacy_ont` is allowed to remain the macro-identification route that exposes
phenomenological nadsoliton signatures such as visible `alpha_geo`-bearing
structure.

That makes it:

1. observationally useful,
2. physically informative,
3. but not identical to the strict source-topology kernel by default.

### 2. Strict kernel role

`K_strict_gate` remains the later strict route used to track the cleaned
source-topological working kernel near the source limit.

That makes it:

1. structurally closer to the source-side strict route,
2. operationally usable for strict selector work,
3. but not automatically the theorem-level carrier of the full legacy package.

### 3. Scale separation consequence

Because the two kernels now have different legal jobs, failure to absorb:

1. `alpha_geo`,
2. `beta_tors`,
3. or the legacy phase/frequency pair

into one strict kernel object is no longer automatically a closure defect.

It may instead be the mathematically expected consequence of role separation
across scales.

```text
T15/T16 remain real comparison frontiers,

selector closure
```
