## Minimal ansatz

Let:
- `M_pair` be the current mode-pair carrier generated by the strict mode scaffold,
- `L_int` be an internal light-channel carrier derived from nadsoliton degrees of freedom,
- `Q_mat` be an internal matter-response carrier,
- `O_em` be an internal emergent-observer readout carrier.

Define a minimal feedback operator on the mode-pair sector by the composition

`K_obs := lambda_obs * E^* G_light R_mat O_obs R_mat^* G_light^* E`

where:
- `E : M_pair -> L_int` is an internal emission map,
- `G_light : L_int -> L_int` is an internal light propagation operator,
- `R_mat : L_int -> Q_mat` is an internal light-to-matter response map,
- `O_obs : Q_mat -> Q_mat` is an internal observer/readout feedback operator,
- `E^*`, `R_mat^*`, `G_light^*` are the corresponding adjoint return maps,
- `lambda_obs >= 0` is a feedback strength parameter.

## Why this ansatz is admissible

### A. Internal-only

All carriers are declared internal to the nadsoliton.
No external apparatus, external photon bath, or external observer enters.

### B. Operator-level explicitness

The candidate is not a story loop but a concrete compositional operator.

### C. No selector smuggling

The ansatz contains:
- no hard-coded `theta_1`, `theta_2`,
- no fixed `u_1 = c_1`, `u_2 = c_2`,
- no built-in orientation convention.

### D. Switch-off consistency

At `lambda_obs = 0`, the added term vanishes and the system returns to the current strict-core selector status.
