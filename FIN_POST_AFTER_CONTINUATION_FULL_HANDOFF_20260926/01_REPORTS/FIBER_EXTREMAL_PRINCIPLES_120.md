## Simple entropy/energy extremal principles do not select a nonzero finite fiber rate

Status:

Take

    L_mu

      L tensor I_2

## 1. Minimum added Dirichlet energy

The vertical contribution is proportional to

    mu (f_+-f_-)^2.

Minimizing the added fiber Dirichlet form over mu>=0 gives

    boxed:
    mu=0.

This disconnects the two fiber children dynamically.

So minimum-energy refinement selects a degenerate non-resolving fiber.

## 2. Maximum fiber mixing

The antisymmetric fiber mode decays at rate

    2mu.

Maximizing the fiber spectral gap gives

    mu -> infinity.

There is no finite maximizer.

## 3. Maximum path randomization per physical time

Increasing mu adds independent vertical jump opportunities while leaving the
coarse projected process unchanged.

Without a separately fixed event/activity budget, the path-randomization rate
can be increased without bound by increasing mu.

So a maximum-randomness principle does not select a finite intrinsic mu.

## 4. Fixed activity budget

If one imposes a total refined activity budget, a finite optimum can be
created, but the budget value is an additional scale/physical premise.

Different budgets give different mu.

## 5. Conclusion

The simplest extremal principles land on the boundaries:

    least extra activity -> mu=0,
    most extra activity  -> mu=infinity.

A nonzero finite fiber rate requires a balancing condition not supplied by the
current FIN refinement or coarse heat-bath data.
