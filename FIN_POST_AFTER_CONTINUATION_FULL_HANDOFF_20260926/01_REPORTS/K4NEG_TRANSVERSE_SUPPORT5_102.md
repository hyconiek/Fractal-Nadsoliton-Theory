## The four-label k4×k6 sheet emits a five-label daughter through a subcritical Z2 crossing

Date: 2026-09-26

Status:

## 1. Parent branch

Take the negative-k4 factorized sheet from report 101.

For large g it tends to

    S4={2,4,8,10},
    p*=1/4,

with asymptotic index 3.

Its stabilizer is the order-4 subgroup generated by:
- the reflection R;
- the half-turn T^6.

## 2. Transverse crossing

Along this branch a simple transverse eigenvalue crosses zero at

    boxed:
    g_T≈5.764771557083876.

The critical eigenvector is, numerically,

    v
      ≈ 0.3048952411 e_(3c)
        +0.9523858945 e_(5c).

It is:
- even under the reflection R;
- odd under T^6.

Therefore the critical coordinate is a Z2 symmetry-breaking mode reducing the
parent stabilizer from order 4 to order 2.

The full Hessian at the crossing has:
- three negative noncritical eigenvalues;
- one simple zero;
- three positive eigenvalues.

## 3. Crossing slope and quartic

The critical eigenvalue changes with

    d lambda/dg
      ≈ 0.037809876356
      >0.

Lyapunov-Schmidt elimination gives

    boxed:
    D4 Phi_eff
      ≈ -1.342316669853
      <0.

Thus the daughter is subcritical in the local Z2 coordinate.

It exists on the high-g side, where the parent has already dropped to index 3,
and the daughter itself has index 4.

## 4. High-g daughter support

Continuation of the daughter to g=200 converges to the strict support

    boxed:
    S5={2,4,6,8,10}.

The exact equal-field weights are

    (0.189156793989940,
     0.218672087453942,
     0.184342237112238,
     0.218672087453942,
     0.189156793989939).

Its outside gap is

    Delta≈0.245787130556946,

Its stabilizer has order 2 and full D12 orbit size 12.

The support theorem therefore predicts

    index=5-1=4,

exactly matching the continued finite-g branch.

## 5. Local/global interpretation

This gives an explicit finite-g realization of

    S4 / index 3
      -> symmetry-breaking crossing
      -> S5 / index 4.

So the support/Morse ladder is now realized one level higher than reports
91-93.

The mechanism is the same broad pattern:
- a parent support has one residual discrete symmetry;
- a transverse odd mode breaks it;
- the asymptotic support gains one effective label;
- the Morse index increases by one.
