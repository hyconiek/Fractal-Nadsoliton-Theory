## The twelve localized minima acquire a two-scale barrier graph at large g

Date: 2026-09-26

Status:

## 1. Twelve asymptotic minima

For sufficiently large g the global minima form one D12 orbit of twelve
one-label states

    M_i -> delta_i,
    i in Z12.

Every persistent isolated index-one large-g saddle must have support size two,
because the support theorem gives

    index = |S|-1.

Thus the index-one saddle graph is the complete graph on these twelve minima,
with edge class determined only by cyclic separation d=1,...,6.

## 2. Pair barrier

For a pair support at separation d,

    p*=(delta_i+delta_(i+d))/2.

Relative to a one-label minimum,

    Delta V_d(g)
      =
      alpha_d g - log 2 + o(1),

where

    alpha_d
      = [mu_vertex-mu_d]/2.

Numerically, ordered from cheapest to most expensive:


    d=3:
      mu_d=0.721595902726829
      alpha_d=0.275091465633409

    d=4:
      mu_d=0.709506943817111
      alpha_d=0.281135945088269

    d=5:
      mu_d=0.612537415303406
      alpha_d=0.329620709345121

    d=2:
      mu_d=0.574156322827628
      alpha_d=0.348811255583010

    d=6:
      mu_d=0.561776644984393
      alpha_d=0.355001094504627

    d=1:
      mu_d=0.280762177816950
      alpha_d=0.495508328088349


Therefore the strict ordering is

    boxed:
    d3 < d4 < d5 < d2 < d6 < d1

on the O(g) barrier scale.

## 3. First barrier level

Keep only the cheapest d=3 edges.

The twelve minima split into exactly three connected components:

    (0, 3, 6, 9)
    (1, 4, 7, 10)
    (2, 5, 8, 11).

Each component is a four-cycle generated by step ±3.

So the fastest asymptotic transitions do NOT mix all twelve minima.

They create three metastable four-state sectors.

## 4. Second barrier level

Add the next-cheapest d=4 edges.

Because

    gcd(3,4,12)=1,

the graph becomes connected immediately.

Thus the first two barrier classes already connect the complete twelve-minimum
set.

No d5,d2,d6 or d1 edge is required for global connectivity.

## 5. Barrier hierarchy

At leading order:

    intra-sector communication:
      B_intra(g)
        = alpha_3 g-log2+o(1)

      alpha_3=0.275091465633409;

    inter-sector/global communication:
      B_global(g)
        = alpha_4 g-log2+o(1)

      alpha_4=0.281135945088269.

Their separation is

    boxed:
    alpha_4-alpha_3
      =0.006044479454859.

For the N-copy reversible Gibbs heat bath, the ratio of the corresponding
exponential time scales therefore grows like

    exp[
      N (alpha_4-alpha_3) g
    ]

up to subexponential factors.

This produces a genuine two-stage metastable hierarchy.
