## 1. Metric graph and fields

Let a state-sourced metric graph have positive conductances

    w_ij = w_ji > 0

on its admitted edges.

Let psi_i be a visible/intercell scalar field.

Let u_i be the scalar hidden backreaction field constructed conditionally from

    hidden H4 + localized phase + zero-order point locality.

The unperturbed Dirichlet energy is

    E0[psi]
      = 1/2 sum_{i<j} w_ij (psi_i-psi_j)^2
      = 1/2 psi^T A psi.

## 2. Refinement-rigid conductance response

Reports 36-38 reduce the first-order edge response to

    delta w_ij / w_ij
      = eps beta (u_i+u_j)/2.

Thus

    w_ij(u)
      = w_ij [1+eps beta (u_i+u_j)/2]

to first order.

## 3. One common interaction energy

Substitute the modulated conductance into the same Dirichlet form:

    E[psi,u]
      = 1/2 sum_{i<j}
          w_ij [1+eps beta (u_i+u_j)/2]
          (psi_i-psi_j)^2
        + E_u[u].

The first-order interaction term is

    boxed:
    E_int[psi,u]
      = eps beta/4
        sum_{i<j} w_ij (u_i+u_j)(psi_i-psi_j)^2.

No separate response law is needed once this interaction is declared.

## 4. Variation with respect to psi

Define D_u=diag(u).

The induced Laplacian variation is

    delta A(u)
      = beta/2 [
          D_u A + A D_u - diag(Au)
        ].

Then

    delta E_int / delta psi
      = eps delta A(u) psi.

Therefore variation of the common energy reproduces exactly the
refinement-rigid hidden->geometry/operator bridge derived earlier.

## 5. Reciprocal variation with respect to u

For each vertex i,

    partial E_int / partial u_i
      = eps beta/4
        sum_{j~i} w_ij (psi_i-psi_j)^2.

Thus the visible/intercell field sources u through the local incident
Dirichlet-energy density.

This is the reciprocal partner of the hidden modulation.

The coupling is therefore not intrinsically one-way.

## 6. Energy-exchange interpretation

If a future hidden dynamic law has generalized force

## 7. Positivity / stability window

For every edge it is sufficient that

    1+eps beta (u_i+u_j)/2 > 0.

Since

    |(u_i+u_j)/2| <= ||u||_infinity,

a simple global sufficient condition is

    |eps beta| ||u||_infinity < 1.

Then every modulated conductance remains positive and

    E_Dirichlet[psi,u] >= 0

with constants as the usual zero mode on a connected graph.

More quantitatively,

    E_Dirichlet[psi,u]
      >= (1-|eps beta| ||u||_infinity) E0[psi].

So the conditional interaction has a controlled small-coupling stability
region.
