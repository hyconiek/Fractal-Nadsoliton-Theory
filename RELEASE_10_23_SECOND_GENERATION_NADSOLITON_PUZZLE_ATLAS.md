## Abstract

This release reports a controlled second-generation search over twelve
mathematical FIN characteristics, denoted `C01` through `C12`.  The search
exhausts all singles, all 66 pairs, all 220 triples, and seven explicitly
selected groups of four to six characteristics.  A finite typed construction
grammar separates mathematically meaningful compositions from visual analogy:
305 combinations are screened, 109 typed or inherited constructions are
retained, and 184 untyped juxtapositions are rejected.

Fifteen derived objects (`O01`–`O15`) and ten higher-order composites
(`G01`–`G10`) are defined, ranked, and assigned explicit falsification tests.
The strongest exact synthesis is a **Stieltjes–Schur context functor**:
nested Schur reductions compose, while the hidden-sector self-energy

```text
Sigma_E(z) = A_EH (z I + A_HH)^(-1) A_HE
```

is a positive operator-valued completely monotone function.  It provides an
exact representation of memory generated by eliminating hidden degrees of
freedom.  The report also constructs a frequency-dependent effective action,
a chiral memory susceptibility, a projective spectral fingerprint, an
operational calibration torsor, and an information-contraction ledger.

All finite numerical identities are reproduced by an executable script and a
12-test regression suite.  The release deliberately does **not** claim a
physical selector, an absolute scale, a completed legacy-to-strict bridge,
thermodynamic entropy, laboratory validation, Standard Model or general
relativistic dynamics, or a Theory of Everything.

## Principal results

1. **Exact dynamic reduction.**  Hidden-node elimination produces a
   frequency-dependent self-energy and hence exact reduced memory, not
   generally a reduced Markov semigroup.
2. **Complete monotonicity.**
   `(-1)^n Sigma_E^(n)(z)` is positive semidefinite for every nonnegative
   derivative order on the positive resolvent ray.
3. **Context composition.**  Direct and nested Schur reductions agree to
   numerical residual `2.47e-16` in the declared finite audit.
4. **Chiral memory response.**  The derived susceptibility is nonzero and
   inversion-odd, but it receives a supplied orientation and does not source
   one.
5. **Operational information loss.**  Relative distinguishability contracts
   under the heat channel; this is not by itself thermodynamic entropy or
   dimensional energy.
6. **Two independent missing sections.**  Positive scale calibration and
   orientation selection remain distinct operational/mathematical
   obligations.
