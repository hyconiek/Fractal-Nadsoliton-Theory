# SQ-003/004: late iid paths and the actual finite source sequence

Date: 2026-10-20. All transfers below concern bounded path laws/weak
convergence, not free transfer of unbounded iid moments through TV.

## 1. Actual SQRT parent after its paid local relaxation

Let t_N be a deterministic origin with t_N>=t_loc(N) from SR and
t_N+sqrt(N)S<=exp(zeta N), for a fixed zeta<b. Let e_N^prep denote
the SR one-time TV bound between the unreflected iid count law at t_N
and pi_D; it tends to zero. Couple those two initial count laws maximally
and evolve their actual identical full Q_N kernel. The complete future
count-path TV costs at most e_N^prep, not a factor per observation.

Starting from pi_D, couple the full Q_N and stationary reflected Q_D,N
until their first actual departure. On a model horizon T the suppressed
jump compensator is at most RN T pi_D(B_D). For T=sqrt(N)S this is
polynomial times exp(-Nb), hence vanishes. Consequently

    TV(future iid count paths, stationary reflected count paths)
       <=e_N^prep+RN sqrt(N)S pi_D(B_D)->0.           (1)

Pushforward by the parity scaling and SQ-002 give (8) for the actual late
iid parent. There is no postselection, exact stationarity claim for it,
or inference of its high moments/entropy from an unspecified o(1) error.

## 2. Reflect the already selected finite source, not select a new one

Take the actual full visible source P_c obtained from global Gibbs pi_N
and reference K_N+cI. Let r_x be its SL scaling ratio. Its reflection
P_c,D retains internal off-diagonal entries and redirects each departure
to the same-state entry. It is NOT the entropy optimizer obtained by
replacing pi_N,K_N with pi_D,K_D.

Set Q_c,D=c(P_c,D-I). For internal edges the exact identity remains

    Q_c,D(x,y)=Q_D(x,y)r_x r_y,
    0<=1-r_x<=A_N/c, A_N<=1+RN.

The conservative diagonals therefore give, on D,

    ||Q_c,D-Q_D||_infinity<=4A_N^2/c,
    ||Q_c,D||_infinity,||Q_D||_infinity<=2A_N.         (2)

These are sufficient for the SL one-step/grid/time-warp proof: it uses
only these conservative rate bounds, not a fresh scaling equation on D.
Thus it applies to P_c,D itself, keeping the actual finite source discrete.

## 3. One paid growing-N/critical-horizon sequence

Freeze exactly

    c_N=(q-1)ceil(N^3/(q-1)), L_N=c_N/(q-1),
    T_N=sqrt(N)S.

There is no parameter sweep, time fitting or exponentially long path
equivalence. The SL construction, with the same initial pi_D, couples
the reflected finite source X_N(t)=Y_floor(c_N t) and Q_D paths so that

    Pr(no matching time warp with displacement <=1/c_N)
       <=[(7/2)A_N^2 sqrt(N)S+2A_N]/c_N
       =O_S(N^(-1/2)).                               (3)

The constant is inherited/symbolic, not a practical error at an example N.
The construction pays skeleton mismatch, multiple jumps per grid cell and
both endpoint cells. It is not full continuous-path TV, whose timestamp
atoms remain different.

Rescale the coupled model-time warp to [0,S]. Its displacement is at most
1/(c_N sqrt(N)); on the good event the count states, hence the scaled
parity values, agree exactly. There is no N^(1/4) multiplier on the bad
event probability. For bounded Lipschitz critical-path functionals the
expectation error is bounded by the time-warp modulus plus twice (3).
This proves the same weak J1 critical limit for stationary reflected P_c,D.

For its actual unreflected iid diagnostic choose deterministic integer
k_N with k_N/c_N>=t_loc(N) and k_N/c_N+sqrt(N)S<=exp(zeta N).
The finite-source SR theorem gives initial TV e_N^finite->0. Its count
projection is exactly Markov; propagating this initial coupling costs that
error once. Reflecting from pi_D adds at most

    floor(c_N sqrt(N)S)(RN/c_N)pi_D(B_D)
       <=RN sqrt(N)S pi_D(B_D).                      (4)

Combining (3),(4) with the stationary SQRT theorem gives

    N^(1/4)m(Y_(k_N+floor(c_N sqrt(N)s))) => Z(s),

with the same SDE (8). These are the actual finite source histories, not
histories generated by an inserted CTMC. Their full fine carrier has
N+L_N slots and the supplied visible-Gibbs times uniform-hidden static
law. It is NOT the all-pairs A7 Gibbs law of an enlarged interacting whole.

The previous SR O(N)-spectator local-window theorem remains valid, but it
does not license the same critical mobility at c_N=O(N): its r_x r_y need
not tend to 1. No such process theorem is claimed here.
