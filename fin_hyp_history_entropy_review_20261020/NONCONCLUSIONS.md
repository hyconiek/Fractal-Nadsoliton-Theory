# Nonconclusions

- This is a same-author second pass. A separate implementation and copy
  replay are not independent authorship, peer review or formalization.
- Neither exact nor approximate entropy maximization has been established
  as nature's law. No tolerance delta is physically selected by this work.
- The finite-block bound requires stationary histories with the same pi,
  the same reference/support and the actual entropy-rate deficit. It is
  not an arbitrary-start or actual iid-parent transfer theorem.
- Finite-prefix entropies supply lower approximations to the deficit; they
  are not sufficient evidence for its upper bound or a growing-horizon law.
- The first-exit theorem retains nonexit mass and an explicit reference
  tail. It does not license general unbounded stopped/readout functionals.
- The rational q4 threshold is not an A7 numeric constant, sharp gap,
  physical time/memory rate or uniform statement in N/g. No huge lattice
  enumeration or physical data enter the checks.
- Reference weights and step conventions remain fixed. Dropping the
  microscopic multiplicity correction would change the objective, not
  improve the same model. The marked-self variant stays non-adopted.
- Strict F01/F03/F06 and all source/units/incidence/conservation/calibration/
  EV, ontology/strict-legacy/selector/role-transfer, FIN-M0/B4/task335/task337
  and QM/GR/SM/L_total/ToE gates remain unchanged. No lower informational
  substrate, physical law adoption, external experiment or commit/push.
