## Abstract

Release 10.11 reports twelve executed mathematical and operational research
programs on the FIN strict kernel. It independently certifies strict-lattice
Schur nonclosure, proves a bounded graphon continuum theorem, constructs a
conditional singular local limit, derives the fractional lattice order
\(4/5\), tests far-tail propagation and adaptive information geometry,
falsifies a finite inverse-variational source grammar, freezes a
process-tensor experiment, extends the apparatus-inclusive information-work
ledger, and audits a compact source skeleton for the strict exponent
\(\eta=9/5\).

The main independent certificate uses exact rational kernel inputs,
50-decimal directed interval arithmetic, a 20,000-term sum, and an analytic
infinite-tail bound. At mass \(1/4\), the native spectral ratio lies in

\[
[0.1567658391,0.1568720170],
\]

while the alternating-site Schur ratio lies in

\[
[0.3064589929,0.3067033963].
\]

The disjoint margin is \(0.1495869759\). A partitioned interval calculation
remains positive for every mass in \([0.01,2]\).

The continuum study separates three inequivalent scaling routes:

1. regular coordinate scaling gives a bounded circle-convolution graphon;
2. a local Laplacian appears only after adding singular spatial localization
   and an \(\epsilon^{-2}\) clock/operator scaling;
3. the unscaled infinite lattice has
   \[
   1-\widehat p(q)\sim C|q|^{4/5},
   \]
   with fitted exponent \(0.7983228\) and predicted Abelian constant
   \(1.1474680\).

The exact conditional chain

\[
4\ln2
\longrightarrow
2^{-4/5}
\longrightarrow
d^{-4/5}
\longrightarrow
d^{-9/5}
\]

is identified as the most compact current explanation of the strict tail
exponent. It is not promoted to a source theorem: current artifacts do not
derive the fivefold quotient, its coupling to strict damping, positive
\(\beta\), or a canonical length/UV standard.

No new chiral source is found. QW-2191 remains open. No external experimental
data are admitted, and no local spacetime, Lorentz symmetry, physical unit,
completed legacy-to-strict bridge, legacy role transfer, Standard Model,
general relativity, or Theory-of-Everything closure is claimed.

## Executed programs

### Program 101 — Independent directed-interval certificate

Rechecked strict Schur nonclosure with an arithmetic engine independent of the
previous million-term floating summation. The theorem survives with a
\(0.1495869759\) certified margin.

### Program 102 — Nonclosure region

Certified the frozen-profile obstruction on the full mass interval
\([0.01,2]\). A 243-point perturbation grid also passes, but is retained only
as pointwise robustness evidence.

### Program 103 — Graphon convergence theorem

Derived an explicit \(O(N^{-1})\) operator-norm bound for regular midpoint
sampling and diagonal removal. The resulting continuum operator is bounded
and nonlocal.

### Program 104 — Singular localizing limit

Proved convergence to \((\sigma^2/2)(-\Delta)\) under an additional
support-contraction and \(\epsilon^{-2}\) rescaling. These are explicit
conversion premises, not FIN-derived physics.

### Program 105 — Fractional tail universality

Used phase equidistribution and a weighted Abelian law to derive fractional
order \(4/5\). The numerical fitted exponent is \(0.7983228184\).

### Program 106 — One-big-jump propagation

Confirmed the standard subexponential far-tail law for the strict heat
semigroup. Polynomial direct jumps prevent a uniform exponential propagation
cone in this lattice model.

### Program 107 — Adaptive information manifold

Constructed a Fisher–Shahshahani gradient flow preserving the probability
simplex and dissipating a declared information functional. The strict profile
is not stationary for this natural source-neutral functional.

### Program 108 — Inverse source and MDL falsification

A six-feature variational grammar produces two nonunique stationary
coefficient families on the training graph. Both are saddles, fail held-out
graph transfer, and are not shorter than direct storage of the strict tuple.

### Program 109 — Chiral-source intake

The fractional, graphon, local-moment, and information-geometric structures
are inversion-even. No signed strict source is admitted.

### Program 110 — Process-tensor preregistration

Frozen machine-readable protocol for a two-slot wave-versus-diffusion
experiment. No external dataset has been admitted.

### Program 111 — Correlated apparatus ledger

For Markov detector memory and imported finite-time dissipation, proved

\[
W_{\rm complete}-\Delta F
=h_{\rm rate}(E)+\Sigma(\tau)\ge0.
\]

### Program 112 — Conditional \(\eta=9/5\) source skeleton

Proved the exact algebraic scale-law chain from \(4\ln2\) and an added
fivefold quotient. Necessity remains open because the fivefold structure and
damping coupling are not strictly sourced.
