# Failed routes and exact negative controls

## 1. The forbidden->permitted same-tail pairing is not a cut injection

The nine phase-safe upward neighbours in SC-001 are not necessarily *outward* edges of a chosen orbit-union cut. Conditional on any actual state x carrying a forbidden upward edge, let V be the G_+-orbit closure of {x} and **all upward H-neighbours of the entire orbit of x**. Then x belongs to V; its entire set of upward H-neighbours lies in V; hence x is shielded from the outward H boundary even though its upward native H degree is at least nine. The forbidden target(s) might also join V via some other orbit image, so this construction is a **failure test for the proposed local charging inference**, not a certified negative signed cut. It cannot be promoted to `Xi_N(V)->infinity`.

## 2. Total shell mass is not a relative cut bound

The prior exact shell theorem bounds `sum_{F}u_e <= N Z_N^W pi_D(Shell_N)` and `pi_D(Shell_N)->0`. This is an **absolute stationary-mass** estimate and does not bound `Fout/(N B)` on cuts concentrated in the shell. Likewise, the proven `D(V)>=-N Z_N^W pi_D(Shell_N)` concerns the opposite sign of the source deficit and does not establish (SC).

## 3. The 9-way pointwise rate ratio does not yield a global flow

`F_up(x)<=H_up(x)/(9a0)` charges edges at the same *tail x*, but H_up(x) may be an internal edge of V and disappears from Hout(V). Summing the pointwise inequality does **not** give `Fout(V)<=Hout(V)/(9a0)`; that would require a genuine escape/expansion lemma. No such lemma has been smuggled into the result.

## 4. No adverse actual q12 cut constructed

No orbit-union V_N from the true MT D_N is shown to have negative lower-ideal source or divergent signed residual. An N-specific float classification of a near-threshold transcendental tie is not a certificate. No large lattice or parameter sweep was run.
