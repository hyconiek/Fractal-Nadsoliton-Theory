## Abstract

Release 10.28 executes fourteen research programs, P309–P322, aimed at
quantifying the additional mathematical and operational resources required by
any legacy-to-strict FIN completion.

The central theorem is a signed path-resource lower bound. For the strict
attenuation samples \(h_d=(1+d^{1.8})^{-1}\),

\[
\Delta^4h_0=-0.0715275581.
\]

Every positive exponential-distance mixture is a Hausdorff moment sequence
and has nonnegative fourth difference. Therefore any normalized signed
representation requires negative Jordan mass at least \(0.0715275581\).
Finite moment-grid linear programs stabilize near negative mass \(0.4067\).

The second principal theorem is a phase obstruction. The legacy phase subgroup
is finite of order \(24\), whereas the exact frozen strict decimals are
generated by the infinite-order element

\[
r=e^{i/4000},
\qquad
e^{i\omega_S}=r^{743},
\qquad
e^{i\phi_S}=r^{650}.
\]

A torsion-preserving legacy map cannot generate this strict phase resource.

The release also constructs a cross-supported Joint Feature Parent for
repaired legacy and strict operators, but proves that it is not a derivation
because its cross block imports the strict square root. It obtains finite-menu
operational distances \(0.2304\) for heat dynamics and \(0.4221\) for wave
dynamics after optimizing the legacy scale, excludes positive mixtures of
legacy hyperbolic scales by regular variation, executes a loss-completed
current-measurement audit, constructs a drift-aware sequential detector,
enumerates all 720 spectral order chambers, designs a restricted-clock
experiment, and refutes the current spectrum-only electroweak role-transfer
certificate.

The resulting **Minimal Bridge Resource Budget** keeps signed negativity,
nontorsion phase, parent cross law, pointing, dimensional scale, and
operational data separately typed. The release does not claim that FIN
internally generates these resources.
