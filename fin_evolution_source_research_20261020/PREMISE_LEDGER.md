Date: 2026-10-20.

| Object | Status in this work | What it licenses |
|---|---|---|

| Maximum path entropy / unmotivated Markov axiom | Not adopted or derived | Cannot be used as an unmarked repair of F01 |

No diagnostic probability space or block variable is introduced as a real
substrate beneath the nadsoliton. These are mathematical countermodels to
an inference, not extra objects in FIN ontology. No claimed model-source
fact follows merely from constructing a path-space representation.

There **are** model evolutions in the repository. The failed step is their
independent fundamental selection, not the existence of differential or
jump equations after premises are supplied. The current work does not claim
to exclude every possible new source theorem or all possible ontological
formalizations. Its negative result is scoped to finite-order signature
providers outside a causally justified Markov/state-completeness class.
