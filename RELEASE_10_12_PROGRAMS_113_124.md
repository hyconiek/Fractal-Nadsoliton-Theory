## Abstract

Release 10.12 executes twelve constructive mathematical and operational
research programs. Rather than listing missing concepts, the release builds
explicit candidate objects and tests which of their premises are forced by
the current FIN structure.

The strict-lattice Schur obstruction is upgraded to a global theorem. With

\[
a=1-\widehat p(\pi),\qquad b=1-\widehat p(\pi/2),
\]

the exact ratio difference is

\[
R_{\rm S}-R_{\rm N}
=
\frac{m[(3a-2b)m+a(2a-b)]}
{(m+a)(2m+a)(m+b)}.
\]

Certified bounds make both numerator coefficients strictly positive.
Therefore the declared strict family is not closed under alternating-site
Schur reduction for any \(m>0\). The theorem also holds on a genuine
continuous neighborhood of the strict parameter tuple.

The strict random walk is placed in the normal domain of attraction of a
symmetric \(4/5\)-stable Lévy process under spatial scaling \(n^{-5/4}\).
Its limiting generator is

\[
C(-\Delta)^{2/5}.
\]

A complete dimensionless operational object is constructed, including state,
wave/diffusion dynamics, clock, preparation, instrument, environment,
apparatus, calibration, record, and decision rule. A hidden-Markov detector
channel supplies correlated apparatus memory. Physical length, clock, action
calibration, and external records remain absent.

The central new object is the \(Z_{12}\) homological-character fibre

\[
\mathcal F_p
=
\widetilde H_0(\ker[m_p:Z_{12}\to Z_{12}])
\oplus\mathcal X_p^-.
\]

For prime generators \(p=2,3,5,7,11\), its dimensions are exactly

\[
(1,2,2,2,2).
\]

The uniform normalized trace is \(9/5\), but the trace is not yet a strict
source. A general probability trace gives

\[
\eta(w)=2-w_2,
\]

so \(\eta=9/5\) precisely when \(w_2=1/5\).

Conditional on this trace and a nonzero multiplicative compression tail
\(T(d)=\beta d^\eta\), multiplicativity forces \(\beta=1\). The resulting
dyadic retention is

\[
2^{-4/5}
=\exp(-\alpha_{\rm geo}/5),
\]

and an exact damping-side legacy-to-strict completion multiplier follows.
This is not a full bridge: the trace source, compression-law source, phase,
frequency, amplitude, selector, physical units, and role transfer remain
open.
