## Mathematical review

- General claims use displayed arguments: Boolean event facets, finite
  differences, compact moment determinacy, contractions, Schur elimination,
  positive-clock monotonicity, weighted-measure recovery, Fourier channels
  and normalized resolvents. Finite samples do not replace these proofs.
- Mixed-law uniqueness assumes existence in the positive integrable event
  representation class; it is not a universal representation theorem.
- Quantum counterexamples concern supplied open channels. Exact unitary
  marginal constraints are explicitly treated as an exception.
- Product transition generators do not force product initial states.
  Idle attempts and common unit changes are separated from actual differences
  of dimensionless predictions.
- A1/A4/A8 were inspected as code, not executed as proof certificates. They
  serialize ansatz, symbolic RG and scoped gravity summaries with explicit
  foundational assumptions and open obligations. They do not derive the
  new event law or close the physical bridge.
