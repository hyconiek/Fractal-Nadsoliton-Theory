# Multiscale dynamics is not established fractality

Date: 2026-10-17. Stage E: exact limited obstructions and a typed future test.

## 1. The strict kernel's exponent is not a dynamic fractal dimension

For the declared continuous kernel extension, omega>0 and beta>0,
`K(d)=cos(omega d+phi)/(1+beta d^(9/5))` has a first positive zero d0.
If an exact dilation law K(a d)=a^-H K(d) held for every d>0 with fixed
a>1, evaluating it at d0/a would force a smaller positive zero. Therefore
no such global homogeneity holds. In particular the dyadic case fails
directly at d0/2. The envelope's asymptotic power is not exact self-similarity
of this oscillatory kernel or of its nonlinear trajectories.

This does not rule out stochastic, restricted-band, discrete or asymptotic
scaling after additional definitions and estimates. The actual finite
12-label restriction does not by itself provide arbitrarily fine spatial
scales. Existing Cantor/prism/refinement results retain their own topology/
stationary scopes; they are not discarded or promoted to a dynamical cascade.

## 2. The conditional Phi4 mechanism is an instructive nonfractal case

Its wall has width sqrt2/mu and its continuum has threshold sqrt2 mu.
For fixed positive mu,lambda, the coordinate/amplitude rescaling
Phi(t,x)=a u(bt,bx) changes the coefficients to mu²/b² and lambda a²/b².
Preserving both requires b=1 and a²=1. Scaling across different parameter
members is not self-similarity of one system. The example can have distinct
internal/continuum relaxation behaviours while lacking this exact homothety.

The N powers in the accepted q12 critical limits are genuine finite-size
scaling statements under their preparation/time contracts. No state-map
from the full field dynamics to those occupation processes is supplied;
they cannot yet be read as one physical spatial cascade or fractal world.

## 3. A precise coarse-dynamic compatibility obligation

For a full vector field F and a declared **linear** coarse map R, exact
deterministic closure requires RF(X) to depend only on RX. For a smooth
nonlinear map replace RF by DR(X)F(X). A sufficient smooth quotient
then gives R T_t=T_eff,t R by uniqueness. If two states have the same RX
but different RF(X), no universal autonomous coarse flow exists on that R.

This obstruction occurs already in the existing13-field template: for
nonzero y_i, fixing Phi,p_Phi while changing hidden psi_i changes its
acceleration by `-2y_i Phi Delta(psi_i²)`. A Phi-only evolution is therefore
not universal; the psi=0 invariant sector is a prepared restriction.
Linear hidden-mode elimination likewise produces frequency-dependent
Schur response, not the static Schur matrix as a universal time generator.
Momenta or a paid memory kernel cannot be omitted arbitrarily.

To study R_l(T_t X) approximately, freeze the state class, initial law or
energy envelope, retained variables/history, error norm, time interval,
scale parameter and composition of reductions. Then prove an actual bound.
An observed hierarchy of decay rates alone is not a fractal exponent or
energy flux between scales. No such full-action multiscale theorem has
been imported from the Markov/memory papers or inferred from eta=1.8.
