    Phi_ddot+m2_Phi Phi+lambda_Phi Phi³=0.

## 3. What isolated-object equations fail to specify

Even granting a fixed isolated Hamiltonian H_0, distinct candidate product
completions

    H_epsilon(A,B)=H_0(A)+H_0(B)+epsilon Phi_A² Phi_B²

have identical isolated restrictions, invariant isolated axes and identical
linearization at the zero pair. epsilon=0 gives no inter-object transfer;
epsilon>0 changes it. The extensions can be made permutation symmetric
and grouping-consistent for any finite number of copies by summing over
all pairs. Those consistency requirements alone do not select epsilon.

These are **logical completion countermodels**, not a proposed FIN tensor
product, graph or added interaction law. They show which extra source
must be present if objects are distinct carriers rather than regions of
an already supplied field. The current source map does not provide it.

If that functional state space and Cauchy law are accepted as conditional

profiles of the **same** field. There is no need to invent a separate
coupling for each phenomenon. The sum is initial data, not a claimed sum
of nonlinear solutions. In a quartic sector the force cross residual is

    F(a+b)-F(a)-F(b)=3lambda ab(a+b).

Thus overlap interactions are fixed by that same supplied potential.
For the double-well Phi sector an explicit smooth kink--antikink profile is

    Phi_R(x)=v[tanh(mu(x+R)/sqrt2)-tanh(mu(x-R)/sqrt2)-1].

`6mu²v T(1-T)²>0`, T=tanh(mu R/sqrt2), for finite R>0. It therefore evolves
nontrivially under the one existing PDE, without a newly fitted interaction.
This does not prove two-wall persistence or a particular scattering outcome.

The strict-core obstruction is **not** that field composition is impossible.
It is that the field/domain/Cauchy identification and the nonlinear source
needed to license this construction have not been derived from the strict
nadsoliton data. Internal labels alone do not provide relative position,
overlap geometry or identity of two separated objects.
