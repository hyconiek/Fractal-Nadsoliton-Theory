## Applied conditional theorem — Kowalczyk, Martel and Muñoz

[Kink dynamics in the phi4 model: asymptotic stability for odd perturbations
in the energy space](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.07420v2), revision25June2015;
published in JAMS, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/jams/870).

Read the introduction, **complete Theorem1.1**, Remark1.1, spectral setup
and Theorem1.2 statement, PDF pp1–3. The proof of the whole paper has not
been independently re-proved. The theorem concerns the classical1+1 phi4
equation on R, sufficiently small **odd** H1×L2 kink perturbations and local
energy-norm relaxation on bounded intervals. It gives no explicit decay rate
in that statement. FIN applicability is paid by the fixed positive mu,lambda
rescaling of the **existing legacy** Phi-only sector. Its required geometry,
signs and initial-data class are assumptions, not strict-derived results.
No application to the full coupled13-field system,3D, q12 or general data.

The independent exact energy/current and continuum-projection calculations
in this audit do not rely on a numerical Fermi-golden-rule coefficient.
They establish a bounded conditional mechanism, not a new stability theorem.
