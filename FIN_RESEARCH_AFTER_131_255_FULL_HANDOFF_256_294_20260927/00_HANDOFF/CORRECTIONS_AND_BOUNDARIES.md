# CORRECTIONS AND BOUNDARIES AFTER REPORT 255

The following corrections must be retained by future agents.

1. **"The cycle is uniquely selected by minimum capacity + maximal freshness" is too strong.**  
   Exhaustive q=2,m=2 enumeration (282) shows many reversible finite dilations attain those two objectives. A unique fixed cycle requires the stronger persistent-record-identity / content-independent-routing restriction.

2. **"Bijectivity or detailed balance protects diffusion" is false.**  
   Reversible content-rewriting reset gates open a finite density gap (257).

3. **"The one-unit Q3 clock fixes the multiunit transport rate" is too strong.**  
   The alpha family (288) has the same initial one-unit Q3 at fixed rho but continuously interpolates between pure reset and pure SWAP/reaction-diffusion.

4. **"Internal Z12 product directions can be promoted to spatial directions" is forbidden.**  
   They act on alternative internal state labels, not subsystem identity (273, 287, 292).

5. **"Short memory from the single-cell reduction persists after large-system closure" is false.**  
   The SWAP hydrodynamic carrier has R(t)~t^-1/2 and K(t)~t^-3/2; the finite-cycle first memory moment grows as n-1 (259).

6. **"Pure diffusion is a distinctive FIN prediction" is too strong.**  
   After rho calibration the leading SWAP hydrodynamic ratios are generic. FIN-specific discrimination should target resolved 12-state/pre-hydrodynamic response, memory, q_d structure and preparation dependence (262, 293).

7. **Natural-extension uniqueness is relative to the declared base process.**  
   It does not establish that the chosen coarse base observable is fundamental ontology, nor that history coordinates are spatial sites (281, 286, 290).
