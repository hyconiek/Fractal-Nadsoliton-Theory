## 1. Canonical rank supplied by natural extension

## 2. The centralizer is not a source law

The natural extension may possess other automorphisms C satisfying

    C T_hat
      =
    T_hat C.

But the universal property of the natural extension does not select one of them
as elementary.

A centralizer can be:
- trivial beyond powers of T_hat;
- finite;
- or very large.

Its mere existence is not a physical generator-selection principle.

## 3. Lifting criterion

Suppose the BASE process itself has another measure-preserving transformation R
that:
- commutes with T;
- preserves the declared process structure.

Then on the inverse limit it can lift coordinatewise to an automorphism R_hat that
commutes with T_hat.

Thus multiple commuting transformations can be sourced if they already exist as
independently meaningful transformations of the base process.

This gives a concrete acceptance gate for higher-rank locality.

## 5. Higher-dimensional acceptance criterion

To claim an operational d-dimensional product geometry, FIN must produce

    T_1,...,T_d

such that:

1. they are independently sourced, not arbitrary centralizer choices;
2. they commute;
3. none is generated by the others;
4. they act nontrivially on reconstructed subsystem identity;
5. their orbit of one local port algebra is free/transitive or otherwise gives a
   controlled incidence structure;

Until then, transformation rank must not be identified with spatial dimension.
