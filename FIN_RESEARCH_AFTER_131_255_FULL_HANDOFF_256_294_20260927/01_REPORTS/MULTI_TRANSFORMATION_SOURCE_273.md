## 1. Two different transformation types

Let a multiunit carrier contain slots indexed by V and an internal state alphabet S.

A global configuration is schematically

    Omega = S^V.

There are two fundamentally different actions.

### Internal/content transformations

These change state values while leaving slot identity fixed.

They belong to the "base" action on S^V.

Example:

    x_i -> r(x_i)

at one or many fixed slots.

Their induced action on V is the identity.

### Slot transformations

These permute which factor is which:

    i -> T(i).

They act on the factor index set V.

Their induced action on V is nontrivial and can generate incidence/geometry.

## 2. Projection-to-slot theorem

Let

    pi_slot

map a global transformation to its induced permutation of reconstructed operational factors.

Then every pure internal transformation g satisfies

    boxed:
    pi_slot(g)=identity.

Therefore any collection of commuting internal generators can have arbitrarily rich state-space factorization while generating ZERO spatial factor orbit.

In particular:

    +3,+4 in Z12

do not become two spatial translations merely because they commute and correspond to CRT factors.

In report 271 the transformations T_x,T_y act by conjugation on the LOCAL INTERVENTION ALGEBRAS themselves:

Thus their nontrivial action is on factor identity.

That is what makes them candidate spatial translations.
