# PAIR-RELATIONAL-COST-SOURCE-264
## Full slot-permutation symmetry forbids nonuniform pair costs unless a relational state or symmetry breaking is already present

Date: 2026-09-27

Status:
exact symmetry theorem plus operational response observation.

Assume task 256 has reconstructed m identical effective slots.

Let the current global preparation and the admissible law be invariant under the full slot permutation group S_m.

Suppose a candidate pair cost

    C_ij

is computed from any pair-local quantity in an equivariant way:
- reduced pair state;
- pair response;
- pair information flow;
- pair barrier;
- pair action;
- or another pair observable.

Equivariance means

    C_{sigma(i),sigma(j)}(sigma . state)
      =
    C_ij(state).

If the state itself is S_m-invariant, then for any two unordered pairs {i,j} and {k,l} there exists sigma in S_m mapping one pair to the other.

Therefore

    boxed:
    C_ij = C_kl

for all distinct pairs.

## Consequence

No pair-dependent incidence can emerge from:
- an iid minimum-capacity record environment;
- identical Z3 marginals;
- the unique pair SWAP gate;
- a completely permutation-symmetric preparation;

because all unordered pairs lie in one S_m orbit.

This is an exact symmetry no-go.

## How pair differentiation CAN arise

At least one of the following must be present:

1. a pair relational variable R_ij;
2. a nonuniform pair response/barrier generated by the microscopic state;
3. a symmetry-broken preparation with persistent pair correlations;
4. a restricted primitive pair-intervention algebra.

But then the new research question becomes:
why is that relational structure persistent and dynamically selected?

## Operational response reconstruction

Once separately addressable factors exist, actual adjacency does not require coordinates.

Perturb factor i and measure the infinitesimal one-point response at factor j.

For a Markov transport law:

    H(t)
      =
      exp(t L_response).

Hence

    boxed:
    L_response
      =
      dH/dt at t=0.

Off-diagonal nonzero entries directly identify pair influence.

For the swap graph:

    L_response
      =
      graph random-walk Laplacian.

Thus the interaction graph is operationally reconstructible from response kernels.

The unresolved issue is not graph reconstruction.

It is the source of a NONUNIFORM response matrix in the first place.

## Verdict

PAIR-RELATIONAL-COST-SOURCE-264 is negative under the present fully symmetric multi-slot input.

A new pair state or persistent symmetry breaking is mathematically necessary to escape complete-graph/equal-cost degeneracy.
