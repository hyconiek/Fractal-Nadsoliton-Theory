# FIN ST4047–ST4346 — Twenty-Round Tail-Selection Principle Audit

## Objective

Twenty adaptive rounds tested whether a standard mathematical principle can
select one positive local continuation of the six strict FIN conductances
without inserting the desired continuum by assumption.

## Results by principle

### Positive local cone

Every nonnegative finite-second-moment tail has

\[
\lambda(k)=Ck^2+o(k^2),
\qquad
C=C_0+\sum_{d\ge7}d^2t_d,
\]

with \(C_0=3.8153141154998296\). The cone is infinite-dimensional and
\(C\in[C_0,\infty)\).

### Minimum norm and minimum speed

Every strictly positive quadratic/Sobolev tail penalty and direct minimization
of \(C\) uniquely select the zero tail. This gives truncation and
\(\widehat c=1.9532829071846785\), but only after adding the principle that
nature minimizes tail size or propagation coefficient. Adding any source term
changes the minimizer.

### Fixed mass, entropy and smoothness

Fixed tail mass moves the minimum to an atom at \(d=7\), while its maximum
second moment remains unbounded. Maximum entropy produces a geometric tail only
after mass, mean and reference measure are supplied. Smoothness penalties and
positive exponential tails leave penalty orders, boundary conditions,
amplitudes and decay rates free.

### Exact fractal scaling

Exact dyadic homogeneity would require the constant ratio

\[
2^{-1.8}=0.2871745887.
\]

Observed strict ratios are

\[
(0.4086,0.4761,0.5144,0.5131,0.4588),

\]

so exact self-similarity fails already inside the frozen data.

### Shortest linear recurrence

The unique order-three recurrence fitting all six values has characteristic
roots including

\[
1.1715198406>1.

\]

Its continuation becomes negative and ultimately grows. Lower-order laws only
fit approximately; higher-order laws restore nonuniqueness. Shortest recurrence
is not a stable positive selector.

### Analytic continuation

Six interpolation values never determine an analytic function. Terms
proportional to

\[
\prod_{j=1}^6(d-j),g(d)

\]

preserve every strict value while changing the tail and its moments.
Analyticity without a strongly specified growth/function class is insufficient.

### Positive exponential mixtures

The shifted Hankel matrices remain indefinite. Exact positive
Hausdorff/Stieltjes/exponential-mixture representation is impossible without a
uniform deformation of at least \(3.6689\times10^{-4}\).

### Same-speed counterexample

Fix a tail contribution \(\Delta C=1\). Either

\[
t_7=\frac1{49}

\]

or

\[
t_8=\frac1{64}

\]

gives the same quadratic coefficient and low-frequency speed. Their fourth
moments are respectively

\[
49\quad\text{and}\quad64.

\]

Thus even an exact measurement of continuum wave speed does not identify the
tail or higher dispersion.

### RG, spectral action and algorithmic simplicity

Tail RG requires normalization and a metric; several fixed families survive.
Spectral actions depend on cutoff function, scale and counterterms. Minimum
description length depends on language, prior and noise model. None becomes a
strict physical law merely by being mathematically economical.

## Axiom-removal conclusion

Truncation follows from the small axiom package:

1. exact finite matching;
2. nonnegative tail;
3. finite second moment;
4. minimize a strictly positive tail functional.

The fourth axiom is essential. Removing it restores the complete positive tail
cone. Replacing it by entropy, smoothness, recurrence, analytic or RG criteria
selects different continuations or leaves new moduli.

\boxed{3/10\ \text{mathematical rows},\qquad0/10\ \text{physical rows}.}
\]

## Deepest surviving interpretation

No standard variational, entropic, analytic, recursive, renormalization or
algorithmic principle currently present in FIN selects one exact positive
continuum tail. Truncation is the unique minimum-tail model, but minimum-tail is
an added axiom rather than a consequence of the nadsoliton kernel.

The decisive next evidence is one strict coupling beyond \(d=6\), or an
independently derived physical functional whose Euler condition is precisely
the tail minimum. Until then the repository predicts a cone of local
continuations and only the class-conditional bound
\(\widehat c\ge1.9532829072\).

No unique continuum, physical speed, apparatus evidence, Standard Model,
gravity, \(L_{\rm total}\), or Theory of Everything closure follows.
