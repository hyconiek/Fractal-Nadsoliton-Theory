## Abstract

Release 11.04 reports six adaptive research rounds, ST1812–ST1901, attempting a
direct gauge-theoretic construction from the strict FIN Dirichlet form after
the APD/moment/Ltotal route was falsified.

The strict weighted incidence operator satisfies

\[
A=d_W^*d_W.
\]

After choosing complex matter and U(1) link phases, the action

\[
S[\psi,U]=\frac12\sum_{i<j}w_{ij}
|\psi_i-U_{ij}\psi_j|^2+\sum_iV(|\psi_i|^2)
\]

is globally regular, positive and exactly locally gauge invariant. Its literal
variation gives one coherent covariant EOM, and the trivial connection recovers
the strict operator. This is the strongest constructive source-action result
currently available.

It is not unique physics. The same weights admit U(1) with every integer
charge, discrete groups, non-Abelian groups and real non-gauge states.
Complexification, gauge group, representation, connection and potential
coefficients are not selected by A.

The strict support graph is the complete graph K12 with 66 edges, diameter one
and cycle rank 55. Without 2-cells there is no local curvature action. There
are 220 triangle cliques; their boundary map has rank 55, while the full clique
complex is the contractible 11-simplex. Keeping only the nearest-neighbour C12
produces one cycle only by discarding 54 nonzero strict edges. No canonical
face set, area or Wilson coefficient is generated.

The exact 12-to-24 refinement transports the fiber-constant gauge-matter
action, but increases cycle rank to 121, adds relative fiber gauge modes and
leaves q and all continuum scaling free.

A doubled incidence Dirac operator anticommutes with a natural grading and
squares to a block containing A. However, its 78-dimensional space has 56 zero
modes. No vertex-diagonal grading exists for a full-support vertex Dirac
operator because triangles make the grading equations inconsistent. Algebra
representation, real structure, first-order condition, cutoff scale, chiral
fermions and gravity remain additional data.

The direct construction therefore supplies a viable conditional lattice
gauge-matter model but no unique gauge physics. The highest-value next theorem
is to derive or refute an automorphism-natural, refinement-functorial weighted
2-complex and face measure from FIN data. Without it, a strict gauge kinetic
action and continuum Maxwell limit cannot be obtained without new assumptions.
