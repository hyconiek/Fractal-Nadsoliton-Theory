# FP-002 adverse proof controls

* A 3-vertex **rational synthetic network**, used solely to audit the dual-sign convention, has `c=(2,3)`, source `s=(2,1,-3)`, and rate load `(2,5,3)`. Capacity 5 is feasible; at capacity 4, the exact Farkas certificate is `phi=(-1,0,1)`, `beta=(0,1,0)`, giving edge inequalities `>=0` and total dual pairing `-1`. It is **not** an actual q12 MT state lattice.
* Phase-total source balance does NOT pay vertexwise feasibility; positivity of all continuum phase-level cut currents does NOT pay exact finite-N positivity.
* A theoretical necessary bound `C>1/2` does not refute existence because C is allowed to be arbitrarily large but finite.
* A q12 local spike violating pointwise continuity need not violate NC/IBA. A count-path reset lift does not introduce a closed phase on MT D_N.
