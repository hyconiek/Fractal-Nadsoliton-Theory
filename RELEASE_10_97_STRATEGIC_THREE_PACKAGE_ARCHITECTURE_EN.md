## Abstract

Release 10.97 reports six research rounds, ST1182–ST1271, updating FIN's
strategic architecture after the operator-to-state nonuniqueness theorem.

The strict informational layer is denoted by \(W0\). No new strict
scale-charged or translation-breaking source is found, and all previously
closed replay classes remain closed. On the finite carrier, the sampled strict
kernel matrix and \(A\) are interdefinable. The functional-calculus algebra
\(C^*(A)\) is commutative and seven-dimensional. Adding the vertex diagonal
algebra generates the full \(M_{12}\), but choosing that observable algebra is
additional structure and does not select classical versus quantum physics.

The conditional conversion package is

\[
CA=(\ell_*,\tau_*,\hbar_*),
\]

the unique rank-three subset spanning length, time and action. It derives the
consistent units

\[
c_*=\frac{\ell_*}{\tau_*},\qquad
E_*=\frac{\hbar_*}{\tau_*},\qquad
p_*=\frac{\hbar_*}{\ell_*},\qquad
m_*=\frac{\hbar_*\tau_*}{\ell_*^2},
\]

but its values remain a free positive three-torsor.

The conditional sector package

\[
SA=(A_{\rm origin},A_\lambda,A_{\rm coupling})
\]

acts on the 24-element frame torsor
\(\mathbb Z_{12}\times\{\pm1\}\). Origin and polarity select one frame only
axiomatically; coupling is separately necessary.

The main theorem proves that the earlier two-package architecture is
insufficient. Classical heat and quantum unitary models can share exactly the
same \(W0\), \(CA\), \(SA\), selected preparation and clock conversion, while
predicting different return probabilities at dimensionless time one:

\[
p_{\rm classical}=0.2628294052832485,
\qquad
p_{\rm quantum}=0.5843533247056469.
\]

An independent operational/state package is therefore necessary:

\[
OA=(\text{StateCategory},\text{PhysicalChannel},\text{ClockMap},
\text{Preparation},\text{Instrument},\text{RecordRule}).
\]

The updated architecture is

\[
\boxed{W0+(CA\times SA\times OA)\longrightarrow W3_{\rm conditional}.}
\]

Removing \(CA\) removes dimensional physics, removing \(SA\) restores the
24-fold sector ambiguity, and removing \(OA\) restores exact classical/quantum
operational ambiguity. The three packages are independent by explicit product
countermodels. All remain conditional; current strict FIN remains \(W0\).
