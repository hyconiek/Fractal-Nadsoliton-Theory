# TREE-BULK-02 — exact local refinement identity

Status: **PROOF_GRADE_CONDITIONAL**

## Main result
Replacing any coarse leaf of mass M by a new internal node and children of total mass M can preserve the old coarse boundary conductance exactly.  New fine structure can therefore be added locally without refitting older hierarchy levels.

## Core formulas
\[
g_{pw}=rac{M}{\kappa_p^{-1}-\kappa_w^{-1}},\qquad C_w=\kappa_wM,
\]
\[
(g_{pw}^{-1}+C_w^{-1})^{-1}=\kappa_pM.
\]

## Evidence / reproduction
Algebraic identity.

## Caveats
Static closure does not imply dynamic closure once the new node has storage.
