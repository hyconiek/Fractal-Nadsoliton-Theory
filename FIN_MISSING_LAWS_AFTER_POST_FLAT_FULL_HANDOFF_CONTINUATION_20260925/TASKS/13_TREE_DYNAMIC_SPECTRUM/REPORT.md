## Main result
The dynamic tree response has a positive-residue pole expansion.  In a regular b-ary self-similar hierarchy, each newly introduced deepest sibling-contrast family has pole ratio r^2, multiplicity ratio b, and total-residue ratio r^4.  This gives a dynamic estimator of r, b and D_H.
