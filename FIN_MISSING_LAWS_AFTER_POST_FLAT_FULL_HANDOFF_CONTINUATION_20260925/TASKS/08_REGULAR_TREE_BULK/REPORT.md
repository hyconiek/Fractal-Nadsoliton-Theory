# LOCALITY-BRIDGE-02 / TREE-BULK-01 — regular local bulk DtN

Status: **PROOF_GRADE_CONDITIONAL**

## Main result
For a regular b-ary hierarchy with boundary Haar eigenvalues `kappa_d=r^(2d)`, the dense boundary operator is the DtN map of a positive local weighted tree.  A branch effective resistance scales as `(b/r^2)^d`, and local edge resistances are positive for every r>1.

## Core formulas
\[
R_d=(b/r^2)^d,\qquad
a_d=b(b/r^2)^d(1-r^{-2}).
\]

## Evidence / reproduction
Algebraic series/parallel reduction; finite truncations were checked numerically.

## Caveats
Does not source the tree, r, or a physical dynamical storage law.

## Next question
Generalize to nonregular state-selected trees and unequal masses.
