# DUPLICATE-HIERARCHY-THEOREM-01 — exact replicated minimum scaling

Status: **PROOF_GRADE_CONDITIONAL**

## Main result
For `K=2^L` distinct phase vectors, each repeated `n=2^m` times, and the declared perfect-balanced hierarchy Ward energy with scale factor `s>=1`, the global minimum scales exactly linearly: `E*_(K,n)=n E*_(K,1)`.  The proof reduces the macro allocation of replicated phase mass to a doubly stochastic matrix and uses convex maximization on the Birkhoff polytope.

## Core formulas
\[
E^*_{K,n}=nE^*_{K,1}.
\]

## Evidence / reproduction
Algebraic proof sketch documented in the report; numerical duplication scouts are not needed for the equality.

## Caveats
Does not imply that the gap to the second hierarchy also scales exactly as n.
