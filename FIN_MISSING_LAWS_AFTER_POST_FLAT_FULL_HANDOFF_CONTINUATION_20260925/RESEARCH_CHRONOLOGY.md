# Research chronology after the predecessor FULL handoff

## Wave A — correcting hidden-memory order
- exact 7+4 stationary decomposition revisited at Gaussian level;
- residual variable `z = y - K x` found to be independent OU at equilibrium;
- genuine stationary reduced memory moved from `N^-1/2` to `N^-1`;
- basis-independent hidden diffusion-modulation strengths recomputed.

## Wave B — exact hierarchy combinatorics and replication
- exact balanced colored-tree recurrence derived;
- asymptotic entropy rates h_K computed;
- replicated-phase minimum theorem `E*_(K,n)=n E*_(K,1)` proved for dyadic
  equal replications within the declared balanced hierarchy energy;
- impurity-defect scaling `n^(2/D_H)` obtained and used to characterize
  extensive rigidity.

## Wave C — locality bridge
- hierarchy Haar/gradient representation made projectively consistent;
- dense boundary operator realized as DtN of a local regular tree;
- theorem generalized to arbitrary rooted trees, unequal masses and
  state-selected non-perfect hierarchies;
- exact refinement identity proved by local series reduction.

## Wave D — D_H=2 audit
- locality + positivity + exact refinement + self-similarity shown insufficient
  to select r;
- uniform unrescaled nondegeneracy selects `r^2=b` / `D_H=2` conditionally;
- natural mass storage gives z=2 scaling for every D_H, preventing silent
  promotion of D_H=2 to a strict FIN result.

## Wave E — dynamic tree memory
- internal storage added to local bulk;
- dynamic Schur complement shown to produce a matrix Stieltjes memory kernel;
- exact pole/multiplicity/residue scaling derived for regular hierarchies;
- boundary response proposed as an inverse geometry observable.

## Wave F — hidden-memory observable
- quartic selection rule derived from C12 harmonics;
- exact finite-N generator recursions identified pure k=5 as the leading
  quartic closure-defect channel;
- closed formula in lambda5 and g obtained for the leading large-N coefficient.

## Wave G — hierarchy free energy
- exact partition recursion for unordered colored perfect trees derived;
- 16-leaf near-equal 12-phase mixtures studied numerically;
- four-phase equal-replication finite-size scaling developed;
- eight-phase extension started and remains open.

## Wave H — inverse/noise frontier
- static resistance metric + dynamic high-frequency coefficients give a
  constructive layer-stripping inverse scheme;
- pole positions alone shown insufficient; residues are necessary;
- explicit perturbation bound gives a sufficient noise threshold preserving
  the reconstructed tree topology.
