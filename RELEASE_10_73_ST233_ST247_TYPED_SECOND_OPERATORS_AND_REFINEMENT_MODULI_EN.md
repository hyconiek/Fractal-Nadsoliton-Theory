## Abstract

Release 10.73 executes programs ST233–ST247. It constructs a mathematically genuine noncommuting second operator from a vertex state, proves interval-separated likelihood-ratio classes for adjacent strict heat preparations, locates the selector information in the stabilizer of the conditioning state, and certifies a bounded continuation graph for all sixty signed fold seeds.

The release also proves a rational global CPTP recovery optimum for a nonunital negative-orientation qubit channel, a verified continuous selector-control optimum in a declared two-state thermodynamic model, a nonlinear carrier-chart obstruction requiring a connection or jet splitting, and the complete self-adjoint classification of exact finite-fiber refinements.

3. **ST235 — selector-resource theorem.** Uniform, localized-even, and chiral conditions have stabilizer orders 24, 2, and 1. Conditioning can select only by carrying the missing orbit information.
4. **ST236 — bounded branch graph.** All 120 declared pseudo-arclength steps are Krawczyk-certified. The finite graph has thirty components, without a claim about untraced global branches.
5. **ST237 — nonunital recovery.** A rational primal–dual certificate proves the global CPTP optimum \(189/500\) for the declared Pauli-plus-replacer channel.
6. **ST238 — continuous selector cost.** The unique optimum satisfies \(C\in[0.0744,0.0747]\) and dimensionless entropy production in \([0.3176910887,0.3191979592]\).
7. **ST239 — nonlinear carrier obstruction.** A concrete nonlinear chart changes the ordinary twelfth-response ratio. A connection or equivalent jet splitting is necessary for a covariant completion.
8. **ST240 — certificate diagnostics.** Sixty-four predeclared coarse cells fail, while all 512 children pass. This distinguishes method resolution from root loss in those boxes; it is not a full-cube cover.
9. **ST241–ST242.** Conditional sharp LAN minimax constants and a general fixed-factor higher-dimensional Clifford polar theorem are obtained.
10. **ST243 — HMM Chernoff optimization.** A unique length-16 optimizer near 0.502259303 is supported by strong numerical evidence. It is deliberately not labeled proven because the endpoint sums were not evaluated with outward interval arithmetic.
11. **ST244 — reset bookkeeping.** Reversible SWAP transfers information; blank recycling pays the free-energy/Landauer cost.
12. **ST245–ST246 — refinement moduli and spectral dimension.** The full self-adjoint block classification and exact additive spectral-dimension formula are proved. Neither selects physical scale.
13. **ST247 — evidence boundary.** External validation remains blocked because no independently registered laboratory record exists.
