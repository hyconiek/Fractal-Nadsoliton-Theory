\begin{abstract}
Release 10.95 showed conditionally that a complete invariant normalized state
of the whole nadsoliton cannot reach a nonexistence state outside its state
space.  The unresolved question was whether the frozen strict operator $A$
itself canonically determines that total-state space and dynamics.

ST1092--ST1181 answer this question negatively for the $A$-only route.  First,
the positive result is strengthened: $Q=-A$ satisfies the exact finite Markov
generator criterion.  All off-diagonal rates are positive, row sums vanish,
and $e^{-tA}$ is a positive mass-preserving irreducible reversible Markov
semigroup for every $t\ge0$.  Its unique stationary distribution is uniform
and it has nonnegative Shannon entropy production.

The main theorem is an operator-to-state nonuniqueness no-go.  The same $A$
canonically supports at least three pairwise inequivalent normalized models:
the classical simplex $\Dclass$ with heat flow, pure quantum rays
$\mathbb{CP}^{11}$ with unitary flow, and density operators $\Drho$ with
unitary conjugation.  Their real dimensions are respectively $11$, $22$ and
$143$; their boundary/convex structures and stationary sets differ, and the
heat and unitary dynamics are inequivalent by invertibility and attractor
structure.  The inherited $\mathbb Z_{12}$ symmetry acts on every candidate
and therefore does not select one.  Exact $12\to24$ refinements repeat the
ambiguity and introduce a free fiber rate.

The heat interpretation also has a precise microscopic boundary.  A fixed-
time CPTP measure-and-prepare dilation exists, but no single finite closed
environment with a time-independent Hamiltonian and fixed initial state can
realize the convergent strict heat semigroup exactly for all times: finite
closed unitary dynamics is almost periodic.  Hence an infinite internal bath,
fresh ancillas or a limit procedure is additional structure.  Strict heat
provides detailed-balance gradient currents, not a sustained internal pump.

Consequently, what blocks annihilation remains model-relative: mass in the
classical model, norm/trace in the quantum models, and state-space closure in
the ontological theorem.  The operator does not decide which blocker is
physical.  At least two extra axiom classes are unavoidable: a state/observable
category and a physical channel with clock/instrument semantics.  Repeating
$A$-only state searches is now closed; progress requires genuinely new strict
data or an explicitly labelled axiom.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive result}

The six rounds establish both a positive theorem and a terminal obstruction.

\paragraph{Positive theorem.}
The strict matrix is not merely mass preserving on sampled vectors.  It is a
weighted complete-graph Laplacian.  Therefore $-A$ is a genuine conservative

 (\mathcal M_{\rm tot},\Phi,\text{operational meaning})
\]
as a unique derivation.  It does not terminate FIN if an additional object can
be independently derived.

 \min_{i\ne j}Q_{ij}=0.011070817321442113>0,

\section{The operator-to-state nonuniqueness theorem}

\begin{theorem}[$A$-only state-selection no-go]
Let $A$ be the frozen $12\times12$ real symmetric strict Laplacian.  The datum
consisting of $A$, its inherited vertex basis, its symmetry group and its
functional calculus does not determine a unique normalized total-state space
and physical channel.  In particular, the three constructions
$(\Dclass,e^{-tA})$, $(\mathbb{CP}^{11},e^{-itA})$ and
$(\Drho,\rho\mapsto e^{-itA}\rho e^{itA})$ are valid, pairwise inequivalent
realisations of the same operator datum.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Markov criterion proves that the first construction is a normalized
positive semigroup.  Self-adjointness proves that the second and third are a
unitary ray flow and a trace-preserving positive conjugation group.  The state
spaces are pairwise nonhomeomorphic by their distinct covering dimensions and
boundary/convex structures.  The heat dynamics has a unique global stationary
probability attractor and its inverse is not a stochastic channel, whereas the
unitary dynamics is a bijective recurrent group and has a higher-dimensional
stationary commutant.  Hence no state-space/dynamical equivalence identifies
the constructions.  All inherit the cyclic permutation action, so symmetry
does not remove the alternatives.  Therefore $A$ and the declared associated
data do not select a unique one.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
Any unique operational FIN theory requires at least one independently justified
state/observable-category premise and one physical-channel/instrument premise,
unless richer strict data proves an equivalent selection theorem.
\end{corollary}

The theorem is not a claim that the three candidates exhaust all models.
Additional candidates strengthen rather than weaken nonuniqueness.

\section{Round II: ST1107--ST1121 --- completeness and killing}

Finite matrix exponentials exist globally.  The declared unitary and heat
channels therefore have no finite-time blow-up.  For linear channels,
no-killing is a generator condition:
\[
 Q\mathbf1=0
 \quad\text{(classical)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal L^*(I)=0
 \quad\text{(quantum)}.
\]
It is not a consequence of the word ``self-reference.''

The same $A$ supports infinitely many additional complete quantum channels,
for example
\[
 \mathcal L_\gamma(\rho)
 =-i[A,\rho]
 +\gamma\left(A\rho A-\frac12\{A^2,\rho\}\right),
 \qquad \gamma\ge0.
\]
Every $\gamma$ is trace preserving, but $A$ does not select $\gamma$.  Uniform
killing supplies the contrasting classical family
\[
 Q_\gamma=-A-\gamma I,
 \qquad S(t)=e^{-\gamma t}.
\]
Again, $\gamma$ is new input.

The full nonlinear nadsoliton law remains absent.  Compactness of a candidate
state space is insufficient unless the vector field is tangent/inward,
regular and globally solvable.  Therefore no universal no-killing theorem for
future nonlinear or open extensions follows from the linear certificate.

\section{Round III: ST1122--ST1136 --- internal heat and dilation}

For a classical probability state define the edge current into $i$ from $j$:
\[
 J_{ij}=W_{ij}(p_j-p_i)=-J_{ji}.
\]
It obeys the exact continuity equation
\[
 \dot p_i=\sum_jJ_{ij}=-(Ap)_i,
 \qquad \sum_i\dot p_i=0.
\]
Shannon entropy satisfies
\[
 \frac{dH}{dt}
 =\frac12\sum_{i,j}W_{ij}(p_i-p_j)(\log p_i-\log p_j)\ge0.
\]
Because the chain is irreducible and detailed-balanced, a stationary state is
uniform and every stationary edge current vanishes.  Strict heat is therefore
a relaxational gradient flow, not a sustained internal pump or circulation.

\subsection{Fixed-time quantum embedding}

For $P=e^{-A}$, one CPTP embedding is the measure-and-prepare channel with
Kraus operators
\[
 K_{ij}=\sqrt{P_{ij}}\,|i\rangle\langle j|,
 \qquad
 \sum_{i,j}K_{ij}^*K_{ij}=I,
\]
so
\[
 \mathcal E(\rho)=\operatorname{diag}(P\operatorname{diag}\rho).
\]
All $144$ entries of $P$ are positive, and this declared channel has full Choi
rank $144$.  It destroys input coherence.  Other quantum extensions of the
same classical transition matrix exist, so this is not a state-category
selector.

\begin{proposition}[finite closed dilation obstruction]
No single finite-dimensional environment in a fixed initial state, coupled by
one time-independent total Hamiltonian, realizes the nontrivial strict mixing
heat semigroup exactly for every $t\ge0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For finite system and environment, every matrix element of the reduced
dynamics is a finite sum of exponentials of energy differences and is almost
periodic.  A nonstationary strict heat trajectory converges exponentially to
the unique uniform state.  A nonconstant almost-periodic function cannot have
a one-sided limit.  The two behaviours are incompatible.
\end{proof}

A fixed-time Stinespring dilation remains valid.  Exact all-time irreversible
heat requires an infinite internal bath, fresh internal ancillas, or a
continuum/singular limit.  Such structure may remain internal to the one total
nadsoliton, but it is not supplied by $A$ alone.

\section{Round IV: ST1137--ST1151 --- refinement and duality}

An exact two-point-fiber family is
\[
 A_{24}(q)=A_{12}\otimes I_2+I_{12}\otimes
 \begin{pmatrix}q&-q\\-q&q\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad q\ge0.
\]
Let $C$ sum each two-point fiber.  Then
\[
 CA_{24}(q)=A_{12}C,
\]
and consequently
\[
 Ce^{-tA_{24}(q)}=e^{-tA_{12}}C,
 \qquad
 Ce^{-itA_{24}(q)}=e^{-itA_{12}}C.
\]
The dual functional calculi are exactly compatible with this refinement.
However, every $q\ge0$ gives the same coarse dynamics.  At $q=0$ the refined
kernel dimension is two; for $q>0$ it is one.  Fine topology/connectivity can
therefore change while the coarse observer is exactly blind.

The spectrum is
\[
 \operatorname{spec}A_{24}(q)
 =\operatorname{spec}A_{12}
 \cup\bigl(\operatorname{spec}A_{12}+2q\bigr).
\]
For a fine classical state, entropy decomposes as
\[
 H(p_{24})=H(Cp_{24})+
 \sum_i(Cp_{24})_iH(p_{\mathrm{fiber}|i}).
\]
This makes hidden information precise.  Yet each $A_{24}(q)$ again admits
simplex, ray and density-state models.  Refinement repeats rather than resolves
the state-category ambiguity.  The parameter $q$ is only a relative inverse
time scale until a dimensional anchor is supplied.

\section{Round V: ST1152--ST1166 --- observer, clock and record}

A single perfectly distinguishable $12$-state carrier stores at most
\[
 \log_2 12=3.5849625007\ldots
\]
classical bits.  The Holevo bound gives the same single-use upper bound for
classical information extracted from a $12$-dimensional quantum system.  A
literal single $12$-level model cannot contain an unbounded independent record
history.  A complete record-bearing model therefore needs composition,
repeated carriers, unbounded refinement or an infinite algebra.  This does not
exclude such a FIN completion; it excludes identifying the lone finite
$A_{12}$ model with the whole operational universe.

Two natural measurements illustrate further ambiguity.  The vertex POVM
$E_x=|x\rangle\langle x|$ has twelve outcomes but is not informationally
complete for density matrices.  The spectral PVM has seven effects because of
degeneracies and does not resolve vertices.  Even a fixed POVM does not fix an
instrument:
\[
 \mathcal I_x^{\rm L}(\rho)=E_x\rho E_x,
 \qquad
 \mathcal I_x^{\rm MP}(\rho)=\operatorname{Tr}(E_x\rho)\sigma_x
\]
have the same outcome probabilities and different postmeasurement states.
$A$ does not select the states $\sigma_x$, apparatus, ancilla or record.

The spectral gap gives two dimensionless clock candidates:
\[
 \text{unitary phase: }e^{-it\lambda_1},
 \qquad T_1=\frac{2\pi}{\lambda_1}=8.331\ldots,
\]
\[
 \text{heat contrast: }a(t)=a_0e^{-\lambda_1t}.
\]
The first is periodic and phase-valued; the second is monotone but saturates
and is irreversible.  They share a number, not an operational clock.  Neither
produces SI seconds.

An internal observer also requires a subalgebra or factorization, memory
states, an interaction/instrument and a record channel.  There is no canonical
system--observer split in $A_{12}$.

\section{Minimal unavoidable extra axioms}

At least two axiom classes are necessary for a unique operational theory:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{State/observable category}: classical probability space,
      complex Hilbert rays, a density-operator algebra, or another explicitly
      defined category.
\item \textbf{Physical channel and operational semantics}: which semigroup or
      group represents time, together with clock, preparation, instrument and
      record rules.
\end{enumerate}
Removing the first leaves the three nonhomeomorphic state spaces.  Removing
the second leaves heat, unitary and infinitely many dephasing/open channels on
compatible carriers.  Hence both classes are necessary in the declared
problem.  They need not be arbitrary assumptions if future FIN mathematics
derives them from genuinely new strict data.
