# NOC-001 — Scoped result

**CONDITIONAL_NATIVE_UNIFORM_L1_GENERATOR_ESTIMATE.** With the exact q12 labelled LOO HB, either frozen matrix, `1<=k<=N/2`, and the inherited reflected local Gibbs moments/domain, the projected chart `Phi=(F,R)` satisfies

`pi_D |L_D(v o Phi) - (G_gamma,alpha v) o Phi| <= C ||v||_{C^3_1} epsilon_N,k`,

`epsilon_N,k=N^-1/4 + k^-1/2 + (N/sqrt(k))*pi_D(B_D)`.

All constants are independent of `N,k`, with dependence allowed on the two **fixed** matrices and inherited local-domain bounds. This is proved by exact jump drifts/brackets, hypergeometric conditional concentration, stationary MT soft/fast moments, and third-jump Taylor. It is not uniform over a broad epsilon family. A fully unconditional theorem requires independent confirmation of the inherited MT domain/path/trapping proofs for the control, not a new finite-N Gaussian CLT.
