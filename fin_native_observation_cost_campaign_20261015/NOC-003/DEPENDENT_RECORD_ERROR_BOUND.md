# NOC-003 — Effective bound for a gap of order alpha

Choose `a=c alpha` with any `c>0` fixed by NOC-002's strict mean separation. Under reflected stationary comparison:

`P_piD(|A_M-mu_gamma,alpha|>c alpha) <= C[1/(M alpha^2) + (N^-1/4+k^-1/2+(N/sqrt(k))*pi_D(B_D))/alpha]`.

Constants are uniform `1<=k<=N/2` for the two frozen operators and the declared small-alpha range, conditional on MT local bounds. This error is **not** obtained from a growing-time OU weak limit: Duhamel is fixed to one unit and the martingale sum treats all temporal dependence natively. Unreflected iid-start error requires an **additive path TV/exit payment** in NOC-004, not an unlicensed stationary mean comparison.
