**Scope:** All five user-requested research campaigns from **“FIN — wyprowadzanie brakujących obiektów teoretycznych”** through the most recent anonymous four-event-history campaign, without the already-downloaded RP001–005 and RP004-A/B predecessor packages; retaining only the user-provided post-RP004 claim/status context. Date of source research: 2026-10-19.

**Start with** `HANDOFF_MASTER.md`, then `CLAIM_AND_DEPENDENCY_MAP.md`, `POST_RP004_USER_PROVIDED_CONTEXT.md`, and `PROVENANCE_AND_LIMITS.md`. There are no duplicate nested ZIPs; full source study directories are extracted unmodified under the names in their original ZIPs.

This is an archival **consolidation** of original research packages. It is not a fresh independent scientific audit, not an acceptance of unverified proofs, and not a new fundamental FIN model.

### Integrity

Run `python3 00_MASTER_HANDOFF/VERIFY_ALL.py` from the extracted package root (or `python3 <path>/VERIFY_ALL.py` from any directory). This verifies the SHA256 manifest of **all supplied files except the verifier and the manifest themselves**; those are included in the ZIP CRC integrity check. It is a file-content check, not a proof checker.

**Explicit exclusions:** original RP001–005 package, RP004-A/B continuation package and the old RP004 continuation snapshot are NOT in this archive. Brief mentions of RP004 remain only as context of the new studies.
