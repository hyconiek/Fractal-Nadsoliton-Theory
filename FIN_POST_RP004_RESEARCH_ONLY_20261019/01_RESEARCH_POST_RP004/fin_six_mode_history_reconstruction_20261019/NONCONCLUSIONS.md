# Nonconclusions

- No derivation of the intrinsic evolution law from the nadsoliton; no closure of F01, F03, F06.
- No identification of natural strict weights, no proof strict/legacy=A7, no physical space/time/units/selector.
- No emergent Lorentzian causal cone, QM, GR, SM, particle spectrum or Teoria Wszystkiego.
- No novel universal inverse Markov theorem or novelty priority over Fourier/spectral identification literature.
- The inverse theorem assumes reversible radial homogeneous single-label Markov dynamics and a fully labelled source of six modal two-time correlations at one common model-time lag.
- Five-mode nonidentifiability does not exclude additional mixed or non-modal readouts and does not assert equality of *all* process histories.
- A stationary correlation function reconstructed from *assumed* Q is not an independent historical/source law.
- A test error bound from deterministic corrupted autocorrelations does not provide a statistical sample complexity or measurement protocol.
