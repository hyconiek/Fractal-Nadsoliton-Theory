## 2. Protocol

For N=7,8,9,10:

1. construct the exact pinning preparation inside the declared J=0 basin;

## 7. Scientific interpretation

The earlier 1–2% preparation residual was not an irreducible memory effect. Most of it came from predicting the finite-N preparation state by an empirical cross-N map.

The exact defect representation explains and removes that error without another fitted PCA correction.

The central caveat is important: for N=7..10 the present 331 predictor still uses the exact microscopic burn propagator. Therefore task 331 establishes a **mechanistic and controlled preparation theory**, not yet a cheap autonomous cross-N formula for the post-burn preparation prior.
