## Abstract

Release 10.60 executes FIN research programs ST70–ST81. It independently audits the exported ST58 proof inequalities, classifies the mathematical premise needed for positive feedback, constructs and obstructs a spin cover, tests nonstationary fine-data refinement, adds nuisance-robust synthetic count bounds, characterizes the complete CPTP intertwiner cone, proves relative thermodynamic-resource minimality, crosses the ST65 localized fold, analyzes strict-doublet backreaction, proves a Bargmann-chirality source obstruction, and exports a signed custody validator.

ST70 parses every serialized ST58 interval endpoint as an exact rational number and replays every decisive acceptance inequality without calling the ST58 implementation or its interval library. The collision polynomial is positive at the left endpoint, negative at the right endpoint, and strictly decreasing throughout the bracket

```text
1.278014751813487 ≤ s* ≤ 1.2780147518274094.
```

The replay validates the exported proof ledger exactly. It does not independently regenerate the upstream cosine, Fourier, stationary-box, inverse, or Krawczyk enclosures.

This precisely identifies the additional time/order premise that evades the ST59 sign-flip obstruction; strict symmetry does not supply its sign.

ST72 constructs the nontrivial central extension `C24 → C12` using the carry cocycle

```text

```

All 1,728 cocycle equations pass. The coboundary coefficient matrix has rank 10 and its augmented matrix rank 11, proving that the cocycle is nontrivial. The spin cover exists, but scalar strict data project to the same `C12` action for both `C12 × Z2` and `C24`; they cannot select the lift.

ST73 proves that a supplied fine-only statistic and a quadratic rate penalty uniquely select a dyadic refinement parameter. The converse constructs a fine record selecting every admissible parameter. Nonstationary fine data can therefore remove lift degeneracy, but no canonical refinement follows from coarse strict `A`.

The conservative bracket becomes 32 necessary and 14,301 sufficient uniformly sampled joint events, corresponding to expected means of 3 and 1,192 events per preparation. These are not laboratory counts or fixed-stratum guarantees.

Its deterministic test keys and local events do not establish external identity or custody.
