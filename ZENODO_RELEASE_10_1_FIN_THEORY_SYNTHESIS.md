## Abstract

Release 10.1 is a self-contained mathematical synthesis of the present Fractal-Nadsoliton-Theory (FIN) framework. It does not treat FIN as an established physical theory and does not rely on cosmological, metaphysical, or Theory-of-Everything interpretations. Instead, it reconstructs the theory from its finite operators, executes twenty independent research programs, subjects the resulting structures to explicit falsification tests, and identifies the smallest additional structures required before experimentally predictive physics is possible.

The monograph proves that the strict and legacy kernels are mutually polynomial functions on the fixed twelve-cycle, while also demonstrating that this finite interpolation is ill-conditioned and fails dramatically under natural out-of-size extensions. It establishes a complete relational system–apparatus invariant despite the impossibility of deriving an absolute orientation from the reflection-even operator algebra. It proves that dimensionless invariant data cannot generate dimensional action, length, time, mass, or energy without independent calibration scales, and that a single finite operator does not select a unique continuum or causal limit.

A central result is the exact dual role of the positive Dirichlet generator

\[
A=sI-K_s.
\]

The same spectral operator generates the unitary group \(e^{-itA}\) and the reversible Markov semigroup \(e^{-tA}\). These evolutions share spectral data but have different short-time laws, composition rules, entropy behavior, intervention dependence, and physical meanings. The monograph proves that the operator alone does not select the temporal ray, state space, preparation, clock, instrument, environment, apparatus, or measurement record. Unitary dilation, decoherence, quantum-Zeno, entropy-production, variational, spectral-triple, continuum-scaling, and Bayesian-discrimination programs are carried out to determine exactly what follows and what remains underdetermined.

The resulting synthesis classifies FIN as a finite spectral kinematics with several mathematically admissible temporal semantics. Its most promising route to genuine physics is not another receiver formula but a calibrated operational-relational continuum completion: a convergent and positivity-controlled operator family, an explicit state and process category, clock and dimensional calibration, preparation and instrument maps, environment and record structure, and blinded held-out experimental predictions.

The release contains the 111-page preprint, editable Markdown and LaTeX sources, the deterministic converter, two independent NumPy research suites, their machine-readable reference outputs, and a SHA-256 manifest.

## Principal mathematical conclusions

### Proven in the stated finite or conditional scope

- The strict and legacy kernels are exact two-way polynomial functions on \(C_{12}\).
- The finite polynomial bridge is not a stable continuum or role-transfer theorem.
- A reflection-even scalar operator cannot generate an absolute orientation.
- A complete relative system–apparatus invariant exists on the finite frame space.
- Dimensionless invariant data cannot naturally produce a nonzero dimensionful observable.
- Three independent calibration scales are generically required to span action, length, and time dimensions.
- A single twelve-point matrix does not determine its continuum scaling, locality, causal cone, or Lorentzian limit.
- The Perron-shifted strict operator is simultaneously a positive Dirichlet generator for unitary and reversible Markov dynamics.
- The corresponding Born population matrices fail Chapman–Kolmogorov composition, while the Markov matrices satisfy it.
- No nontrivial continuous finite evolution is both unitary and entrywise stochastic.
- FIN diffusion admits an exact infinite-dimensional unitary dilation, but no finite closed bath reproduces nonconstant exponential relaxation for all times.
- Frequent projective observation produces a Zeno limit rather than automatically producing the FIN heat semigroup.
- Shannon information does not determine thermodynamic energy, temperature, or work without a Hamiltonian, bath, state, and allowed operations.
- The naive finite spectral-triple proposal fails the first-order and even-grading tests.
- The unshifted strict kernel is not a stable Euclidean Gaussian action because it contains negative modes.
- The finite generator alone cannot select state, time category, preparation, clock, instrument, environment, apparatus, or record.

### Strongly supported synthesis

- The smallest robust mathematical object is the positive Dirichlet generator \(A=sI-K_s\) together with its spectral measure.
- Green, unitary, heat, wave, Poisson, filtering, and variational constructions are different functional images of the same finite spectral core.
- These images are not automatically the same physics.
- A calibrated operational-relational continuum theory is the shortest coherent bridge from the present mathematics to experiment.

### Not established

- A unique physical temporal law selected internally by FIN.
- A FIN-native dimensionful clock, length, action, energy, or mass scale.
- A unique positivity-preserving and local continuum refinement.
- Lorentz symmetry, reflection positivity, a causal spacetime limit, or field content.
- A unique Standard-Model-like spectral triple or gauge sector.
- An independently motivated natural physical system realizing the operator.
- Empirical superiority over generic circulant, quantum-walk, Markov, or open-system alternatives.
