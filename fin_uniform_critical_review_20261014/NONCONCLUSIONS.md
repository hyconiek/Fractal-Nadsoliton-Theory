# Nonconclusions and scope boundaries

- UF is an analytic conditional model theorem, not mechanized proof, peer
  review or empirical evidence. Input spectral/backend assumptions persist.
- Source/replay44 flags and intake40 checks do not prove stochastic convergence
  by numerical sampling. Input hashes do not attest an executor's Git checkout.
- Iid-uniform initialization and the exact critical gain family are supplied
  statistical/control choices, not cosmological initial-state or tuning sources.
- Independence of initial labels is not independence of interacting trajectories.
- Critical closure is asymptotic; existing exact finite-N parity nonlumpability
  and rejected recursive binary/autonomous-bit routes remain valid.
- Quartic limiting stationarity is not full finite-N Gibbs equilibrium or
  a uniform-in-time approximation. No scalar global-parity discrepancy is assumed.
- The new finite-N bound is an equilibrium mass bound. It does not give a
  dynamical approximation error, mixing rate, residence time or escape prefactor.
- The MT trapping/conditional-equilibrium/relaxation chain is unexecuted.
  A long-lived sector, a conserved sign and a propagating particle are distinct.
- A reflected comparison is not a physical FIN wall or an admissible unreported
  controller. Connectivity and initial coverage cannot be guessed from a picture.
- No independent A7/kinetics/gain/clock/units/locality/conservation source,
  apparatus/EV, QW-2191/legacy-role discharge, FIN-M0/B4/task335 or QM/GR/SM/
  L_total/ToE closure is supplied. No external experiment, purchase or message.
