# Architect decision after UF

Date: 2026-10-14.
Decision: **CRITICAL_LOCAL_EQUILIBRIUM_AND_TRAPPING_WINDOW**.
Status: next research programme; MT has not been executed.

UF now gives an actual finite-horizon collective process from an unbiased
reference start. The global Gibbs exclusion certificate gives the opposite
late-equilibrium occupation behaviour. Neither result alone says how long a
finite-N run remains in the local critical sector or how it relaxes there.

That is the next useful bridge. Do not replace it by another normal-form,
variance-digit or generic source-inventory campaign.

## Precise scientific question

> Is there a proved finite-N window in which the actual symmetric-start
> process has locally equilibrated near u yet has overwhelmingly not escaped
> to the globally favoured part of the same supplied model?

An affirmative answer would make the effective quartic law a controlled
finite-N local statistical description, not only an iterated limit. A negative
or incomplete answer must name the missing trapping/relaxation estimate.

## A route using no extra microscopic law

1. Use the known parity free energy and positive ten-mode Hessian to prove
   local quartic/fast coercivity, allowing the N-dependent quadratic perturbation.
2. Define a small local **analysis** region with an actual count-state boundary
   and positive free-energy height. A connected component must be specified;
   a disconnected reflected chain does not prepare the whole conditional law.
3. Combine stationary unnormalized Gibbs weights, PSD interaction and an
   actual-rate bound to control outward boundary flux from the iid-uniform
   start. This can give a one-sided trapping bound without a global saddle census.
4. Derive the conditional local Gibbs scaling law and separately prove or
   isolate a reflected-chain relaxation bound. Reflection is an auxiliary
   proof coupling, not a physical wall inserted into FIN.
5. Transfer to the original process only before its controlled exit, then
   state the finite-N time window or return its unpaid mixing lemma.

## Scope safeguards

The probability lower bound for sector lifetime is not an exact escape
exponent, matching mean time or prefactor. A sector can persist while its
internal sign relaxes rapidly. Long trapping is not conserved information,
a propagating particle, emergent space or a physical clock.

An auxiliary conditional equilibrium is not automatically reached by the
actual process. UF weak convergence alone cannot prove reflected mixing or
interchange stationary and N limits. If relaxation remains open, retain
trapping/static results and do not claim the complete time-window theorem.
