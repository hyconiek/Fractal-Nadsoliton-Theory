## Abstract

Release 10.24 executes FIN research Programs 255–266.  The central result is
that every nontrivial observation context of the finite strict twelve-state
generator carries an exact positive operator-valued Stieltjes memory measure.
All 4,094 contexts are audited.  The hidden even/odd realization has six
declared states but only five observable-controllable dimensions; one hidden
spectral mode is invisible to the self-energy, and arbitrary decoupled modes
can be added without changing the observed memory function.

The release also proves Schur-context composition, derives a closed chiral
memory-susceptibility formula and norm bound, constructs a multi-time process
information ledger, and identifies an exact generator-scale versus clock-scale
degeneracy.  A frozen amplitude-only legacy-to-strict completion map is
falsified: it produces an indefinite generator and a positive-resolvent-axis
pole, so amplitude absorption alone cannot enter the positive strict
Stieltjes class.

Methodological studies show that a vertex POVM alone cannot identify graph
currents, generic positive Laplacians satisfy most Stieltjes and Schur tests,
and a strict spectral fingerprint supplies the main specificity.  A matched
twelve-state reservoir benchmark yields a negative result for automatic
biological or computational advantage: the declared FIN heat reservoir has
lower linear memory capacity than all 120 controls and the slowest recovery
for the frozen perturbation.

The report constructs theoretical objects `O16`–`O25` and recommends fourteen
next programs, `P267`–`P280`.  It does not claim a strict selector, physical
scale, completed kernel bridge, unique microscopic environment, unique
adaptive law, laboratory validation, Standard Model or gravitational
derivation, or Theory-of-Everything closure.
