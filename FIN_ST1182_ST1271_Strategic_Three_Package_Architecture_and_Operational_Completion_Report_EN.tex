\begin{abstract}
The earlier strategic verdict proposed a two-package architecture: a strict
dimensionless informational core $W0$, conversion axioms $CA$ for units, and
sector axioms $SA$ for origin/polarity selection, producing conditional
effective physics $W3$.  Release 10.96 subsequently proved that the same strict
operator $A$ admits exact classical and quantum state models with incompatible
dynamics.  ST1182--ST1271 test whether $CA+SA$ removes that ambiguity.

First, the strict-source intake is closed without replay: no new scale-charged
$S_+$ or translation-breaking source has appeared.  On the finite carrier the
sampled strict kernel and $A$ are algebraically interdefinable.  The functional
calculus algebra $C^*(A)$ is commutative and seven-dimensional.  Adding the
vertex diagonal algebra generates the full $M_{12}$ because every off-diagonal
entry of $A$ is nonzero, but choosing that diagonal algebra as physical input
is itself extra structure and still permits both classical and quantum uses.

The conversion package is reconstructed as the positive triple
$(\ell_*,\tau_*,\hbar_*)$.  It is the unique rank-three subset spanning length,
time and action, and yields
\[
c_*=\ell_*/\tau_*,\quad E_*=\hbar_*/\tau_*,\quad
p_*=\hbar_*/\ell_*,\quad m_*=\hbar_*\tau_*/\ell_*^2.
\]
Its numerical values remain a free $(\mathbb R_+)^3$ torsor.  It does not
select a sector, state category, temperature scale or instrument.

The sector package is reconstructed on the frame torsor
$\mathbb Z_{12}\times\{\pm1\}$.  The dihedral action is free and transitive.
Origin plus polarity select one frame, and a coupling axiom makes that frame
dynamically relevant, but all are explicitly non-strict.  The unique invariant
probability measure is uniform and cannot select a frame.

The main theorem falsifies the sufficiency of the earlier two-package
architecture.  Three pairwise inequivalent models share exactly the same
$W0$, $CA$ and $SA$.  For the same selected-vertex preparation at dimensionless
time one, classical heat predicts return probability $0.2628294053$, whereas
unitary evolution predicts $0.5843533247$.  Thus units and sector choice do not
choose state or dynamics.

An independent operational/state package $OA$ is therefore unavoidable.  Its
minimal interface contains StateCategory, PhysicalChannel, ClockMap,
Preparation, Instrument and RecordRule.  Removing any one leaves a concrete
ambiguity or destroys falsifiability.  The strategic architecture must be
upgraded to
\[
  W0+(CA\times SA\times OA)\longrightarrow W3_{\rm conditional}.
\]
This is a conditional model-building framework, not strict ToE closure.
\end{abstract}

\section{Strategic input and nonreplay rule}

The current repository verdict separates three previously known cuts:
dimension, sector selection and the information-to-physics bridge.  It also
closes repeated searches in invariant/weight-zero receiver classes.  Release
10.96 adds a fourth fact: the operator datum does not select classical versus
quantum state semantics.

Accordingly, this cycle did not repeat generic legacy-to-strict fitting,
invariant selector searches, dimensionless $S_+$ monomials, phase locking or
boundary fitting to $c$.  No new hard source formula entered the repository.
The only admissible local continuation was to formalize and falsify the
explicit conditional architecture.

\section{Round I: ST1182--ST1196 --- strict core $W0$ and algebra}

The strict informational ledger is
\[
 W0=\{K_{\rm strict},A,\alpha_{\rm geo},A_\phi,
      \text{finite spectral/graph theorems and no-go certificates}\},
\]
where
\[
 \alpha_{\rm geo}=4\ln2,qquad
 A_\phi=\frac{2\pi}{\alpha_{\rm geo}},qquad
 \alpha_{\rm geo}A_\phi=2\pi.
\]
These identities remain dimensionless and do not define $\hbar$.

On the finite carrier,
\[
 W=sI-A,qquad A=sI-W,qquad s=1.660307278766099.
\]
Therefore the sampled strict kernel matrix contains no additional state-
category selector beyond $(A,s)$.  This statement does not collapse the full
continuous kernel function to its twelve-point sample.

The spectrum has seven distinct values, so
\[
 C^*(A)\cong\mathbb C^7
\]
is commutative.  It cannot by itself be the full noncommutative observable
algebra $M_{12}$.

There is nevertheless a useful algebraic result.  Let
$\mathcal D=\operatorname{span}\{E_{ii}\}$ be the declared vertex diagonal
algebra.  Since $A_{ij}\ne0$ for every $i\ne j$,
\[
 E_{ii}AE_{jj}=A_{ij}E_{ij}.
\]
Hence $\mathcal D$ and $A$ generate every matrix unit and therefore $M_{12}$.
This proves algebraic reachability, not physical quantum semantics: the same
vertex data also defines the classical Markov model.

\section{Round II: ST1197--ST1211 --- conversion package $CA$}

Define the explicitly conditional positive scales
\[
 CA=(\ell_*,\tau_*,\hbar_*)\in(\mathbb R_+)^3
\]
with dimensions length, time and action.  The exhaustive audit of all eight
subsets gives rank three only for the full triple; every proper subset has rank
at most two.

The package consistently dimensionalizes both main channels:
\[
 H_{\rm phys}=\frac{\hbar_*}{\tau_*}A,qquad
 U(t_{\rm phys})=exp\!\left[-i\frac{t_{\rm phys}}{\tau_*}A\right],
\]
\[
 L_{\rm heat}=-\frac1{\tau_*}A,qquad
 P(t_{\rm phys})=exp\!\left[-\frac{t_{\rm phys}}{\tau_*}A\right].
\]
It also gives
\[
 c_*=\frac{\ell_*}{\tau_*},\quad
 E_*=\frac{\hbar_*}{\tau_*},\quad
 p_*=\frac{\hbar_*}{\ell_*},\quad
 m_*=\frac{\hbar_*\tau_*}{\ell_*^2},\quad E_*=m_*c_*^2.
\]
These are dimensional identities, not derivations of observed constants,
Lorentz invariance or particle mass.

The free action of $(\mathbb R_+)^3$ on the scale triple leaves all
dimensionless $W0$ relations invariant.  Thus $CA$ installs units without
choosing values.  It is selector-blind and state-category-blind.  Moreover,
$k_B$ or an equivalent temperature/entropy conversion and a bath/process law
remain necessary before Shannon entropy becomes thermodynamic entropy.

\section{Round III: ST1212--ST1226 --- sector package $SA$}

Let
\[
 X=\mathbb Z_{12}\times\{\pm1\}
\]
encode an origin and orientation/polarity.  The dihedral group $D_{12}$ of
order $24$ acts by rotations
$(r,\lambda)\mapsto(r+k,\lambda)$ and reflections
$(r,\lambda)\mapsto(k-r,-\lambda)$.  Direct enumeration proves that the action
on $X$ is free and transitive.  Thus $X$ is a $D_{12}$ torsor: symmetry alone
cannot choose a point.

The minimal conditional sector package is
\[
 SA=(A_{\rm origin},A_\lambda,A_{\rm coupling}).
\]
Origin alone leaves two polarities; polarity alone leaves twelve origins;
together they select one labelled frame.  Without a coupling axiom the label
has no dynamical effect.

The finite selector potential
\[
 V_{\rm ax}(r,\lambda)=\mu\left[
 w_o d_{\mathbb Z_{12}}(r,r_0)^2+
 w_\lambda\frac{1-\lambda\lambda_0}{2}\right]
\]
has a unique minimum at $(r_0,\lambda_0)$ for every audited positive
$w_o,w_\lambda\in\{1,2,3\}$.  Its weights and scale are assumed.  Conversely,
the unique invariant probability measure on the transitive torsor is uniform
with mass $1/24$ per branch and is selector-blind.

$CA$ and $SA$ solve independent group actions:
\[
 (\mathbb R_+)^3\times X.
\]
Neither supplies the other, and neither selects classical versus quantum
states.

\section{Round IV: ST1227--ST1241 --- two-package insufficiency}

Fix one identical data set
\[
 W0,qquad CA=(\ell_*,\tau_*,\hbar_*),qquad
 SA=(r_0,\lambda_0,\text{coupling}).
\]
It admits at least the following normalized models:
\begin{enumerate}
\item classical probabilities on $\Delta_{11}$ with $e^{-tA}$;
\item pure rays in $\mathbb{CP}^{11}$ with $e^{-itA}$;
\item density states in $\mathcal D_{12}$ with unitary conjugation.
\end{enumerate}
Their dimensions $11,22,143$ and topologies remain inequivalent.  $CA$ only
replaces $t$ by $t_{\rm phys}/\tau_*$.  $SA$ can choose the same preparation
label $r_0$ in every model.

For $r_0=0$ and dimensionless time $t=1$, the return experiment gives
\[
 p_{\rm C}(0|0;1)=(e^{-A})_{00}=0.2628294052832485,
\]
\[
 p_{\rm Q}(0|0;1)=|(e^{-iA})_{00}|^2=0.5843533247056469.
\]
The difference is $0.3215239194223984$.  Hence the same $W0+CA+SA$ supplies
distinct testable predictions after a state/channel interpretation is chosen.

\begin{theorem}[two-package insufficiency]
$W0+CA+SA$ does not determine a unique operational theory.  Units and a
selected sector frame do not select the state category, physical channel,
clock implementation, preparation, instrument or record.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The three models above share identical $W0$, $CA$ and $SA$, but their state
spaces are pairwise inequivalent and the displayed common preparation/readout
experiment has unequal predictions.  Therefore those data cannot determine a
unique operational model.
\end{proof}

The documented adaptive form
\[
 \dot K=-\Pi\bigl(V'(K)-C(K)\bigr)
\]
does not repair the theorem.  It is typed on an operator manifold and requires
$\Pi,V,C$.  The counterflow $\dot A=-\gamma A$ preserves both the Markov-
Laplacian and self-adjoint quantum-generator interpretations.  An adaptive law
selects a category only if it uniquely preserves a category-specific cone or
algebra and supplies an operational readout; no such strict theorem exists.

\section{Round VI: ST1257--ST1271 --- three-package theorem}

\begin{theorem}[strategic three-package architecture]
Within the declared FIN conditional construction, the three obligation classes
conversion, sector selection and operational meaning are independent.  No two
of $CA$, $SA$ and $OA$ cover all three.  A conditionally dimensionful,
sector-selected and testable theory therefore requires
\[
 \boxed{W0+(CA\times SA\times OA)\longrightarrow W3_{\rm conditional}.}
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Removing $CA$ leaves every prediction dimensionless, and every proper subset
of its three scale vectors has rank below three.  Removing $SA$ leaves the
free transitive 24-frame torsor.  Removing $OA$ leaves the exact classical and
quantum countermodels with unequal return predictions and no unique record
semantics.  The explicit product
$(\mathbb R_+)^3\times X\times\{OA\text{ choices}\}$ permits each factor to
vary while the other two are fixed, proving independence.
\end{proof}

The minimality is by obligation class, not by scalar count.  A future theorem
may derive several fields from one deeper object, but no such object is present.

\]

\section{Updated strategic verdict}

The previous two-package proposal was not wrong; it was incomplete.  $CA$ and
$SA$ remain the correct conditional answers to independent unit and sector
cuts.  Release 10.96 shows that neither addresses the state/operation cut.

$CA$ & length/time/action conversion & conditional & scale-charged strict values \\
$SA$ & origin/polarity/coupling & conditional & non-premise selector source \\

adaptive terminology without a typed law, or receiver fits as sources.

\section{Final verdict}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{The rigorous conditional FIN architecture has three, not two,
independent missing bridges.}  Conversion axioms can supply units.  Sector
axioms can choose an oriented branch.  Neither determines what counts as a
state, which dynamics is physical, how time is read, how the state is prepared,
what instrument acts, or what record falsifies the prediction.  The operational
package $OA$ is mathematically unavoidable after the $A$-only nonuniqueness
theorem.  Current strict FIN remains $W0$; $CA$, $SA$, $OA$ and every resulting
$W3$ statement are conditional.
\end{quote}

This is an important refinement of the strategic verdict, not physical
confirmation of FIN.
