and a higher-order remainder. The latter has global source size O(epsilon^4)
and integrated squared compact trace O(epsilon^3) on critical time. The
whole local-continuum estimate therefore depends only on the explicitly

A separate frozen far-field calculation tests a possible next shortcut.

    A_self = 9/2 - 9/[4(w+3)] - 3g4/8.

homogeneous envelope equation. The calculation does not prove an actual
nonlinear threshold coefficient, altered scattering or instability for
the prepared inhomogeneous solution. It identifies why blindly dividing
by a cubic phase is not a justified general strategy.
