# Next open atom — quantitative native cut inequality on actual q12 H_N

The exact finite cut theorem is now available, but the model-specific all-N condition is not:

`sup_(N sufficiently large, N in 12*N) sup_(S subset D_N) [sum_(x in S) s_x]_+ / C_H^+(S) < infinity`, with the standard positive/zero=+infinity convention.

Here `s_x=(N/2) W_x L0_D m(x)`, `C_H^+(S)=sum_(x->y in H, x in S, y outside) W_x q0(x,y)`, and H uses the unchanged MT departure-layer leak rule and original A/B threshold. If proved, it yields an **explicit** max-flow construction with `c_e<=K c0_e`, exact drift and total rate `<=KN`. It is a *sufficient* stronger route to the old vertex-cap problem, not a redefinition of the allowed diagnostic class.

A feasible H count flow not bounded relative to each native edge could still meet a uniform `CN` cap. An exact lower ideal with negative source for the actual unbounded q12 family would kill the fixed H support route. Neither statement is proved here.

The shortest new proof target is a native-weight flow pairing for irregular shell-tracing cuts. It may redistribute **both ways relative to retained native edges**: do not impose delta>=0, since that can falsely reject feasible repairs.
