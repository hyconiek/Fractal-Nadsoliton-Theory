# Nonconclusions and scope

- The new uniform lower bound on native LOO transition rates is an inequality *inside the local MT cylinder*, not an assertion about the full Gibbs simplex or a new natural kinetic law.
- No bound `sup_N Q_N<infinity` was proved; a uniform-rate leaky generator was NOT constructed.
- There is no true q12 asymptotic countercut/certificate, and no proof or refutation of universal bracket-averaging IBA.
- The old artificial family with unbounded K and bounded C remains valid for reference networks without uniform positive `q0/N`; its extrapolation to the MT-local q12 family was the incorrect step.
- The synthetic rational fixture tests graph algebra only; it does not certify the actual A7 entries, spectral arithmetic or MT domain membership.
- No iid-start/late-iid transfer, physical time/units/locality, conserved physical energy, detector/EV/calibration or finite-N empirical power.
- No independent natural source for A7 or kinetics; no QM, QFT, GR, SM, L_total, ToE, ontology/selector or role transfer; no FIN-M0/B4/task337/task335 activation.
- Existing source packages, user plans, reviews and AGENTS have not been modified.
