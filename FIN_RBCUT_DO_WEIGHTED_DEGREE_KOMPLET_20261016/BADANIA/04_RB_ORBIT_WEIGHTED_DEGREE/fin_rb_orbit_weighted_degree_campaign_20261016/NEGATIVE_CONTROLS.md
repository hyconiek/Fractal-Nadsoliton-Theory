# Controls and invalid inferences

1. **Generic bounded activity vs reference domination.** The previously accepted artificial four-state graded family has H native edges `1/N`, removed F edges `1`, reference vertex weights one, K*=N+1 and a feasible final H flow of magnitude `1+1/N` (so an absolute vertex clock bound). Its native individual edge rate divided by N is `1/N^2`, hence there is no strictly positive uniform `a_*`. It cannot refute the new FIN-local theorem; it explains its essential hypothesis.
2. **Global full simplex vs MT local domain.** On the full count simplex, an occupied label can have `n_a=1` and `q0(x,y)` of order one, so `q0(x,y)/N` can vanish. The new step is NOT licensed there. It is licensed by the exact cylinder `|m|<1/100`, `||w||<1/10000`, and the inherited positive-semidefinite A7/top-spectrum contract.
3. **Shell rarity is not an ideal-sign proof.** `pi_D(Shell_N)->0` and `sum_L s>=-o(N Z_N^W)` do not imply `sum_L s>=0`, nor bounded Q_N. The new equivalence leaves this mathematical gate open.
4. **No q12 synthetic test disguised as a theorem.** `verify_weighted_degree.py` checks a 36-state D6-symmetric rational graph and a degenerating four-state family; neither is genuine MT `D_N`. Ten bounded flags are not ten independent proofs or empirical records.
5. **Same-parity rates.** These may be zero in the constructed parity-drift feasibility class or retained native with one additional N clock allowance. Their removal cannot be identified with the original native generator, even if a new diagnostic feasible flow is eventually found.
6. **Native q0 bound is not a physical source.** It uses the native heat-bath rule as an explicitly supplied mathematical comparator. Nothing derives that rule from autonomous physical dynamics.
7. **No trajectory theorem without conductances.** The old rare-leak IBA implication is conditional on an actual exact-drift uniformly capped sequence. Merely defining Q_N or bounding toy scores does not activate it.
