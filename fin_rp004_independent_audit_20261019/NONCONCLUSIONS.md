# Nonconclusions and rejection boundaries

- This is not a line-by-line audit of the reported 2026-10-19 MOVING_CONE/BOOTSTRAP/OUTWARD_FLUX proofs; those files were not accessible.
- Numerical algebra checks, energy identities and even the **correct conditional gluing proof** do not establish the missing uniform-in-a cone residue/stationary-phase estimate.
- Spectral Γ positivity does not itself prove finite-window outward flux; E_global growth does not force E_ext growth.
- The older B package explicitly required repair of its triangular constant closure; its original PASS-like descriptions cannot replace the repaired proof.
- The reported positive coefficient is **compatible** with safe conditional bounds, not independently certified.
- No new model, fit, geometry, source/kinetic law, QM/GR/SM/ToE inference, experiment, detector, photos, absorption, full scattering or second boundary.
- An absent public `main` file is not evidence of its absence in the user's unpushed local repository.
