## Abstract

Release 11.05 reports six adaptive research rounds, ST1902–ST1991, testing
whether strict FIN support, automorphism symmetry and exact refinement uniquely
generate a weighted 2-complex and gauge kinetic action.

The weighted graph automorphism group is exactly \(D_{12}\). Its 220 triangles
split into 12 orbits, so automorphism-natural face sets form 4096 orbit unions.
Exact GF(2) elimination proves that at least 2542 have full boundary rank 55;
at least 338 are inclusion-minimal. Two distinct five-orbit complexes with 76
faces are independently verified to have exact rational rank 55. Naturality,
\(H^1=0\), minimal orbit count and equal minimum face count therefore do not
select a complex.

Positive natural face measures form an \((\mathbb R_+)^{12}\) orbit cone, or
\((\mathbb R_+)^5\) on a five-orbit complex. Product, geometric mean,
arithmetic mean, minimum and maximum edge-derived rules span rank five and are
not proportional. Positivity and homogeneity do not select a Hodge star or
Wilson normalization.

The exact refined support \(K_{12}\square K_2\) has 144 edges and cycle rank
121. Its flag complex contains 440 triangles of boundary rank 110, leaving
\(H^1\) dimension 11. The typed product refinement supplies 66 non-clique
squares; triangles plus squares have rank 121 and restore \(H^1=0\). Thus the
support-only flag functor is not product-refinement functorial. The product
repair requires refinement metadata and retains free square-orbit weights.

Finite heat traces have spectral dimension zero at both asymptotic time
extremes. Under iterated refinement,

\[
Z_k(t)=Z_{12}(t)\prod_\ell(1+e^{-2q_\ell t}),
\]

so every intermediate spectral-dimension plateau depends on the unsourced
sequence \(q_\ell\). A fitted four-dimensional plateau is not a derivation.

The most decisive next theorem is to classify all positive face inner products
for which gauge curvature energy commutes with FIN coarse/refinement maps over
12-to-24-to-48, and determine whether they are unique up to one overall scale.
