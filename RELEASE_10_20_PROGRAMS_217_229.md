## Abstract

Release 10.20 reports thirteen executed mathematical, formal, statistical,
and operational research programs, numbered 217 through 229. The studies
construct missing objects required to connect the dimensionless FIN spectral
generator to a reproducible observation protocol, while preserving the
distinction between strict mathematics, conditional instruments, synthetic
method evidence, and external physical validation.

The release proves that the uniform central state is uniquely fixed in the
category of bistochastic sector channels: invariance under the permutation
subcategory already forces every central coordinate to equal `1/m`. For FIN
sector dimensions `(1,2,2,2,2)`, the corresponding expectation is exactly
`9/5`. A nonuniform state can instead be selected operationally by a
preparation record. A simultaneous Hoeffding bound distinguishes this
empirical frequency section from an internally generated strict state.

The strict exponent problem receives a stronger no-go theorem. In the
coordinate `x=eta-1`, every continuous, reflection-compatible, positively
homogeneous vector field satisfying

```text
F(a*x) = a*F(x),  a>0
```

is `F(x)=kappa*x`. A nonzero slope has only the fixed point `x=0`, while a
zero slope fixes every point. Consequently the strict increment `x=4/5`
cannot be uniquely selected without a new non-affine or scale-bearing datum.
The exact degree-eight polynomial constraint has rank eight and nullity one.

A new finite-reference theorem quantifies the selector problem. For the
imperfect reflection reference

```text

```

exact production of any nonzero target asymmetry followed by exact,
uncorrelated return of the reference would decrease squared fidelity from
`1-r^2` to `(1-r^2)(1-t^2)`, contradicting channel monotonicity. Exact
product catalysis is therefore impossible when no second asymmetric source
is consumed. Exact marginal return remains possible by storing
target-reference correlations. At `r=0.8` and target `t=0.6`, the constructed
channel requires `0.178364` bits of mutual information. This quantifies a
supplied selector resource but does not source its initial sign or discharge
`QW-2191`.

The statistical layer is strengthened in three ways:

  reduces the calibrated hidden-mixing DKW radius from `0.203960` to

The efficient region remains explicitly asymptotic. It is not substituted
