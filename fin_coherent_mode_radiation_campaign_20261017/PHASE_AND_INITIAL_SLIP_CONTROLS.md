# Phase and preparation: exact second-order controls

For the normalized real eigenfunction f and complex preparation Psi^(1)=epsilon f[A cos(omega t)+ i B sin(omega t)],

    |Psi^(1)|^2=epsilon^2 |f|^2 [(A^2+B^2)/2 +(A^2-B^2)cos(2omega t)/2],
    -V_phi^(2)=-epsilon^2 S [(A^2+B^2)+(A^2-B^2)cos(2omega t)].

A=1,B=0 (neutral real): DC and second harmonic both -epsilon^2 S, as in RP-003. An ellipse A!=B has oscillatory strength -(A^2-B^2)epsilon^2 S; its second-harmonic spectral density scales with (A^2-B^2)^2 Gamma. A=B=1 (circular): **zero second harmonic at this order**, but nonzero static force -2epsilon^2 S; the modeled conserved global phase charge at leading order is Q=epsilon^2 A B omega if ||f||=1, with the sign convention adopted in PHASE_AND_RADIATION_SEED. Q is not electromagnetic charge.

The circular rotating datum Psi_t(0)=i epsilon omega f is DIFFERENT from the neutral contract Psi_t(0)=0. With the same undressed phi(0)=K, phi_t(0)=0, even the circular case has phi_tt(0)=-2epsilon^2 S+O(epsilon^4) and therefore excites a transient in both shape and continuum projections. The absence of a 2omega Fourier forcing is NOT a no-radiation theorem. A genuine exactly rotating nonlinear coherent object requires solving the same coupled stationary profile system, with dressed phi and Psi and fixed conserved Q; no new potential is admitted.

Under the neutral datum, the forced scalar second-order correction u solves u_tt+L_phi u=-S(1+cos Omega t), u(0)=u_t(0)=0. This *causal* solution cannot be replaced by the eternal outgoing steady response without paying turn-on and local energy terms. P_c S has a genuine continuum part but the odd discrete shape-mode component of S must also be retained in u. Since S is odd, translation zero mode is absent at leading order; this does not pin the kink for the exact nonlinear evolution.
