# Time and spatial scopes

* The certified mode and Gamma theorem live on the full line x in R; the dimensionless legacy 1+1 geometry is supplied. The mode decays exponentially, rather than being compactly supported.
* Phi(0)=tanh x, Phi_t(0)=0, Psi(0)=epsilon f, Psi_t(0)=0; no independent free radiation field or packet.
* Smooth coupled PDE perturbation comparisons are valid on each **fixed** finite time interval [0,T] with a constant depending on T. This is not a uniform growing-time certificate.
* A candidate R_epsilon needs logarithmic growth to suppress kink/background cross current; T_epsilon must then exceed causal travel time and account for massive group delay. Neither a valid specific pair nor a positive bound is certified, and no ad hoc pair is represented as a completed theorem.
* Whole-field orbital stability holds globally for sufficiently small data **conditional on** the inherited NS stability and wellposedness premises, on the same solution; it does not imply dispersive estimates.
