The smaller-gap case amplifies more strongly for both sampled phases. Phase dependence is present but does not remove amplification. At w=1.25 the sampled maximum is at T=100 for both phases; saturation is not established. The energy norm differs with w, so this is a relative-gain comparison, not a comparison of equal raw displacements.
