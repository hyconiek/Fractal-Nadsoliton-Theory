# MP7-013 — exact classification of self-consistent boundary supports

Let `K={3,4,5,6}` denote the four aligned cosine/alternating frequencies.  For a field with strictly positive amplitudes on an active subset `S subset K`, expand

`w_j=exp(sum_{a in S} J_a cos(2*pi*a*j/12))`

(with frequency six interpreted as `cos(pi j)=(-1)^j`).  The Fourier coefficient of `w` at frequency `k` is a sum of products of modified-Bessel coefficients, all nonnegative.  Because each active `J_a>0` gives strictly positive coefficients at every integer harmonic, the coefficient at `k` is strictly positive iff

`k in <S> subset Z12`,

the subgroup generated by the active frequencies.  Reflection symmetry makes the corresponding sine mean zero, so the aligned cosine mean satisfies

`E[C_k]>0` iff `k in <S>`, and `E[C_k]=0` otherwise,

up to the positive supplied feature normalization.

At a stationary point `s=g E[C4]`, an inactive coordinate `s_k=0` therefore requires `E[C_k]=0`.  The same condition is necessary for a constrained minimum in the nonnegative orthant, because the inward directional derivative is

`partial_{s_k} Phi = -E[C_k] <=0`

at `s_k=0`, and a boundary minimum needs it to be nonnegative.  Hence a support is admissible only when

`S = K intersect <S>`.

Enumerating subgroups of `Z12` gives exactly

`empty`, `{6}`, `{4}`, `{4,6}`, `{3,6}`, `{3,4,5,6}`.

All other nonempty proper subsets are excluded structurally: at least one absent retained mode belongs to the generated subgroup and has a strictly positive inward mean.

This classifies possible supports only.  It does not assert that every listed nonempty support actually has a stationary branch at a given gain.
