# MP7 scientific nonconclusions and guardrails

The MP7 campaign establishes finite-model mathematical results only within the exact domains stated in the claim register. It does **not** establish any of the following.

- **Target S is not proved.** Target P and the new positive normalized margin remain weaker than the sharper sigma-threshold problem.
- **There is no unrestricted all-stationary full-X7 index-one theorem.** The earlier stationary counterexample remains valid; MP7-038 is restricted to interior aligned stationary points in the stated gain window.
- **Phase alignment is a global-minimizer reduction, not a stationary-point alignment theorem.** Nonaligned saddles/stationary families are not removed by MP7-011.
- **The fixed-amplitude 60 phase roots are not 60 full equilibria or physical states.** MP7-040 explicitly excludes full positive-g stationarity at those frozen amplitudes.
- **No physical selector is derived.** A D12 orbit of equivalent localized minima does not choose one label, orientation, spatial direction, particle type or observed vacuum.
- **The model does not source the gain g.** The numerical transition and fold gains are parameters of the supplied finite model, not derived laboratory couplings or universal constants.
- **No physical time or unique dynamics is derived.** MP7-028/029 add explicit mobilities/noise laws and demonstrate rate nonuniqueness.
- **The finite-N copy count N is supplied.** It is not identified with a bit count, number of particles, universe size or thermodynamic system size.
- **No temperature or latent heat is derived.** The certified jump in dV/dg is a dimensionless slope discontinuity with respect to g.
- **The auxiliary Gaussian field is an exact equilibrium representation, not a newly derived physical field.**
- **The current explicit finite-N error is local to fixed caps at the coexistence gain.** A global quantitative outside-cap remainder uniform in g=g_eq+c/N is still open.
- **The D3 transverse crossing is not yet a classified nonlinear bifurcation.** A two-dimensional critical representation and allowed cubic invariant have been proved, but daughter branches/normal-form coefficients have not.
- **The robustness radius from MP7-021 is conservative.** It is a sufficient perturbative neighborhood, not evidence that the model fails outside it.
- **No Standard Model, GR, legacy-role transfer, L_total closure, QW-2191 selector closure, laboratory realization or theory-of-everything claim follows.**
