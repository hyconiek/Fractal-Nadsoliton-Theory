Question: can malformed input or a floating boundary produce an unsupported

Inputs: A01–A08. Dependency: the F intake findings.
Output directory: fin_evidence_intake_tools_20261014/.

Method:
1. Create separately versioned wrappers/implementations; do not edit the
   archived F producer or silently replace its scientific outputs.
2. Validate finite positive integer n, 0<alpha<1, exact nonnegative record
   counts, all13 outcomes and consistency between counts and n. Do not
   renormalize malformed empirical data into a passing histogram.
3. Reject empty evidence input, nonfinite/nondecimal numerical fields and
   missing required identities. Parse UTC/monotonic timing under an explicit
   schema; distinguish missing measurements from zero. Detect calibration/
   validation role collisions and repeated sample/reset identities according
   to the declared protocol. Do not infer iid from unique IDs.
4. Preserve raw records and noisy absolute-label/output mismatches; quarantine
   malformed rows with reason codes rather than silently dropping them.

   an exact quotient by passing only a shape assertion.

   ambiguous threshold cases return NUMERICAL_BOUND_OPEN/INCONCLUSIVE.
7. Unit-test the intake's concrete malformed fixtures plus omitted invalids,
   role collisions, incompatible model inputs and boundary decisions.

Deliverables: INPUT_GATE_CONTRACT.json, strict intake/decision helpers,
UNIT_TESTS.json, NUMERICAL_SCOPE.md, REPLAY.md and hashes.
