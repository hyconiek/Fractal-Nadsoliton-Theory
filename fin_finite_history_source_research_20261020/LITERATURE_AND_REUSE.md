No full copyrighted PDF is copied into this research directory.

## Primary works actually inspected

- Csiszar, Cover and Choi, *Conditional Limit Theorems under Markov
  Conditioning*, IEEE Transactions on Information Theory 33(6), 788--801,
  November 1987, DOI 10.1109/TIT.1987.1057385.
  [Official author-hosted PDF](https://www-isl.stanford.edu/~cover/papers/transIT/0788csis.pdf).
  Inspected definitions, Theorems 2--4, Proposition W, Lemmas 3--4 and
  surrounding proof text. Conditional Markov limits and polynomial type
  bounds are established methods. Endpoint/circular conventions and
  approximation/support hypotheses matter. We give our own restricted
  last-exit counting and whole-block proof rather than asserting automatic
  applicability to a growing FIN carrier or rare preparation.
- Jacquet, Knessl and Szpankowski, *Counting Markov Types*, DMTCS conference
  paper, published 31 December 2009.
  [Publisher page](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/articles/2768),
  [publisher PDF](https://dmtcs.episciences.org/en/articles/2768/download).
  Inspected balanced-frequency definitions and Theorem 1. Counting types
  and counting words within one type are distinct questions. Its stated
  growing-alphabet asymptotic is not imported into FIN or used in our bound.

These were read as extracted text through the web tool, not a line-by-line
verification of every proof or a hash-attested original download. Some PDF
formulas are text-mangled; no extracted formula is treated as our certificate.
URLs/metadata are recorded, but original PDF-byte hashes are **not claimed**.
