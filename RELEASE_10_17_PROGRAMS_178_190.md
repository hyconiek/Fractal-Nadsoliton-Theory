## Abstract

Release 10.17 reports thirteen executed mathematical, computational and
operational studies of the FIN informational-spectral framework. The release
continues the falsification-first methodology of Release 10.16 and does not
attempt to confirm cosmological or Theory-of-Everything interpretations.

The first principal result is a classification theorem for intensive state
laws. Under extensive replication, a continuous degree-zero law based only
on additive information $\alpha$ and multiplicative sector count $h$ has the
form

$$
\beta=F\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{\log h}\right).
$$

Composition leaves the function $F$ arbitrary. Requiring invariance under
arbitrary label-splitting coarse graining reduces this family to constants,
but does not select the value $\log2$. The former one-copy relation
$\beta_F=\alpha_{\rm geo}/4=\log2$ therefore remains a conditional premise,
not a derived physical law.

The second principal result is the construction of a
`CompressionScaleTorsor`. For fixed exponent $\eta$, every positive
compression coefficient $\beta$ is related by coordinate scaling, and

$$
\frac1{1+\beta d^\eta}
=
\frac1{1+x^\eta},
\qquad x=\beta^{1/\eta}d.
$$

Positivity selects a positive orbit but not the representative $\beta=1$.
Six audited candidate source classes produce no target-independent strict
source.

The release also constructs a complete Choi/LMI criterion for deterministic
conversion under qubit reflection covariance. It proves that the previously
used transverse monotone is incomplete on the full state space and replaces
it with a necessary-and-sufficient finite criterion.

Operational advances include an exact DKW theorem for repeated independent
blocks, a nonlinear multi-control calibration frame, a frozen open-set
challenge, and a two-step process instrument for double-slit dynamics. The
process instrument contains an echo intervention and a nonfactorization
memory witness separating quasistatic phase noise, Markov dephasing, detector
blur and classical path diffusion in the declared model.

Two further impossibility theorems sharpen the physical boundary. A fixed
system generator admits a continuum of CPTP environment channels, so the
environment is not a function of the operator alone. The legacy phase
generator is algebraic and cyclotomic, while the strict generator
$e^{i743/4000}$ is transcendental; finite algebraic legacy operations cannot
produce the strict character or its correction cocycle.

Finally, wave, heat and normalized resolvent predictions are shown to be
invariant under

$$
(A,t,z)\mapsto(cA,t/c,cz).
$$

An intrinsic spectral rule cannot select a clock representative. A
`SpectralClockFrame` is constructed as a conditional calibration object, not
as a strict unit source.

Three repository-local external-lineage bundles are audited against eleven
operational requirements. None passes the complete preparation, raw-order,
unit, detector, memory and reference-control gate. No external experiment is
executed.

## Main scientific conclusions

1. Tensor composition does not uniquely select a FIN sector state.
2. Coarse-graining invariance selects at most an unspecified constant state
   parameter.
3. Positive strict compression is a scale orbit until a length frame is
   supplied.
4. The full sub-three-percent ball certificate remains open.
5. The exact finite Dirichlet core is independent of the AFIS Boolean model.
6. Reflection-covariant qubit conversion has a complete Choi/LMI criterion.
7. Dependence correction must use the number of independent blocks, not the
   raw record count.
8. A second calibration control makes the shared-apparatus premise
   falsifiable.
9. Marginal quantile features cannot identify temporal memory.
10. A two-step intervention process separates declared mechanisms of
    visibility loss.
11. The environment and decoherence channel are not uniquely determined by
    the system generator.
12. An algebraic legacy-only phase completion is impossible.
13. A physical clock is a section of a positive scale orbit, not an invariant
    spectral number.
14. No local bundle is admissible for the new external protocol.
