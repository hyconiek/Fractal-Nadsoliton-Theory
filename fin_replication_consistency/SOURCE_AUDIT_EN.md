## Non-equivalent operations

- Refining vertices of one graph is not replicating constituents into X^N.
- Copying a known classical preparation is not cloning an unknown quantum state.
- A kernel self-loop W_ii is not the event rate b_ii of two coordinates
  initially prepared at the same vertex.
- A symmetric or mirror-odd operator does not specify an update clock.
- An energy function does not select asynchronous versus synchronous updates.
- Matrix complete positivity here means nonnegative Gram factorization,
  not a quantum-channel statement.
