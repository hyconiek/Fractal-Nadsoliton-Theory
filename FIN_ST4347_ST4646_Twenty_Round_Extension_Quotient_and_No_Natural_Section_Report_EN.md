# FIN ST4347–ST4646 — Extension Quotient and No-Natural-Section Theorem

## Setup

Let \(X\) be a space of infinite kernel continuations and let

\[
R:X\to Y

\]

## No-natural-section theorem

The tail group acts freely by

\[
x\mapsto x+v,qquad v\in V,

\]

while acting trivially on \(Y\). Suppose a section \(s:Y\to X\) were natural
with respect to this action. Equivariance would require

\[
s(y)=s(y)+v

\]

for every \(v\in V\), which is impossible when \(v\neq0\).

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{there is no tail-translation-natural selector of a continuum extension}.}

\]

The zero-tail, smoothest-tail, maximum-entropy, Bayesian, recursive and RG
sections are legitimate only after adding structure that breaks this symmetry.

## Invariant-factorization theorem

An observable \(F:X\to Z\) is determined by the finite strict data exactly when
it is constant on every tail fibre. In that case there is a unique
\(\bar F:Y\to Z\) such that

\[
F=\bar F\circ R.

\]

This gives a precise criterion for honest strict claims.

Finite matrix spectrum, \(D_{12}\) symmetry, row-sum identities, dual finite
dynamics and the finite loop tensor factor through \(R\). Continuum speed,
small-wave-number order, causal class, spectral dimension and higher dispersion
do not.

## Maximal canonical object

The canonical quotient is

\[
X/V\cong Y.

\]

Thus the maximal continuation-independent object is the finite strict
operational class itself, not one preferred infinite representative. Selecting
a representative resembles gauge fixing mathematically, but different choices
make different physical predictions, so this is not a harmless physical gauge.

## Why more finite data do not solve the problem

Adding \(K(7)\), or any finite number of tail coordinates, leaves an
infinite-dimensional residual fibre. Full infinite recovery becomes possible
only after:

- supplying the complete tail;
- restricting to a finite-dimensional model class;
- or deriving an independent functional/recurrence that selects a section.

The model-class or functional choice is itself the missing information.

The same obstruction applies separately to legacy and strict fibres. It does
not complete the kernel bridge or transfer legacy roles.
