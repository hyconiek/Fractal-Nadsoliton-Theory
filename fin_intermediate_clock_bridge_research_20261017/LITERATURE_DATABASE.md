## IC-L01 — Cappelletti and Wiuf, 2016; arXiv v3, 2015

[Elimination of Intermediate Species in Multiscale Stochastic Reaction Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.2474).
Actual browser search and primary metadata reading; PDF read through the
web fallback after Firecrawl HTTP402. Inspected intermediate definitions,
reduced-rate construction and Section4.3's complete statement of Theorem4.3
and Corollary4.4. Rates, initial data and scaling hypotheses are inputs.
The general result does not give unrestricted path convergence. No such
theorem is imported to FIN: our finite trace and TV bound are proved
directly, with both N and kappa and the observation map explicit.

## IC-L02 — Esposito, 2012, with the published erratum

[Stochastic thermodynamics under coarse graining](https://arxiv.org/abs/1112.5410),
[erratum](https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.86.049904).
Read PDF SectionIII, equations28--41, and the full one-page erratum. The
coarse master equation depends on hidden conditional distributions unless
closure/time-scale conditions are paid. The erratum restricts part of
SectionIV.B to transitions generated by one common reservoir; those entropy
identities are not applied here. This is a resource/closure benchmark, not
a source of FIN's carrier, energy unit or clock.

[Asymptotic analysis for stationary distributions of multiscaled reaction networks](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/advances-in-applied-probability/article/asymptotic-analysis-for-stationary-distributions-of-multiscaled-reaction-networks/7551C68BD723A93B1D707B7A0F4226D2).

this publication applies to A7.
