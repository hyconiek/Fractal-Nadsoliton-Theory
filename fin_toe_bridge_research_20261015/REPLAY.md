The fresh producer/symbolic/finite-generator checks need numpy, scipy,
mpmath and sympy. Reproduce to a new path with verify_bridges.py --output
/tmp/fin-toe-bridge-checks.json; do not overwrite historical source packages.

The source script exports exact dyadic/rational input enclosures and public
decimal endpoints; the independent checker rebuilds the decisive inequalities
without importing that producer. It does not independently formalize mpmath.iv
or the external electrical-network theorem.

Analytical RP/transfer/domain propositions live in DERIVED_RESULTS.md and
TRANSFER_PROOF_REVIEW.md. The finite matrix fixtures do not prove the growing-N
limit. Their premises point to actual MT/UF/OBS review completions.

The bibliography hashes ignored extracted texts. No original PDF byte or
historical custody identity is claimed. Browser-harness additionally read a
2026 official HTML article after Firecrawl credits ended. Missing local cache
does not invalidate the finite arithmetic replay; source reading must be

authorial metadata writer or external experiment is required by these checks.
