# Frozen negative controls

1. The unrestricted condition 'all right vertices have nine neighbours' does NOT imply Hall for every subset when column marginals are nonuniform; full weighted Hall cannot be replaced by degree. This is a logical countercontrol and was already understood at PC intake, not a new FIN counterexample.
2. The separated-hole proof uses ≤1 removed predecessor per right vertex. If A contains a whole 9-predecessor star, the argument cannot conclude R⊂E. This is a failure of the method, **not** a claimed violation on actual H.
3. Merely showing I'(E)/J→0 does not imply strict Hall for near-full T if O'(A) shrinks even faster; the quantitative condition .173W(A)≥W(E) is essential.
4. The exact phase tie belongs to A; the constructed orbit lies R_N=o(1), far inside A, so no equality decision is needed for the new subclass. No tolerance is used to classify actual phase-boundary states.
5. E states have explicit extra columns I'_E=max(I_E,-s_E); they are bounded by NW_E, NOT incorrectly bounded by the ordinary bulk .35NW_E.
6. The graph at the band endpoints is not the whole generator. A compact Hall subclass does not pay DH-002.
