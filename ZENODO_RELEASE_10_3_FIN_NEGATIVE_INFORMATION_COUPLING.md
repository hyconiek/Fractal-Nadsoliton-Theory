## Abstract

This release presents ten executed mathematical and computational studies of a conditional negative-information-coupling extension of the FIN kernel framework. The legacy kernel is treated as an intended intermediate precursor of the strict kernel, in accordance with the repository's current kernel-split guardrail; no identity theorem between the kernels and no transfer of legacy physical roles are assumed.

The study first audits the historical kernel-transformation narrative and proves that spatial attenuation, oscillatory signs, and path summation do not by themselves define operational information loss. Such a claim additionally requires a state space, temporal law, channel or coarse-graining map, environment, information functional, preparation, instrument, and measurement record.

Programs 31–40 then test: a loss-only legacy-to-strict bridge; Markov admissibility of the signed legacy row; hazard reconstruction of the strict nonlinear envelope; stability bounds for feedback laws; information destruction versus environmental transfer; the observability of a signed system–environment coupling; entropy-production accounting; wave versus diffusion dynamics under the same feedback-modified operator; structural identifiability of coupling sign and scale; and a blinded synthetic four-model challenge.

The main conclusion is a constrained no-go result. Positive attenuation cannot transform the canonical sign-changing legacy row into the positive strict row, so a loss term alone cannot complete the bridge. The raw signed legacy kernel is not a Markov generator, and admissible positivity repairs are nonunique. Reduced-system information loss can be represented exactly as transfer to an explicit environment, while the sign of the coupling is unobservable without a calibrated parity-breaking reference. The same spatial operator continues to admit both unitary wave and Markov diffusion dynamics; negative coupling does not select the temporal category. A synthetic held-out experiment achieves 85.4 percent overall model-classification accuracy, but also exhibits an exact system-only equivalence between genuine negative coupling and a static mimic.

Accordingly, negative information coupling is a coherent possible completion component, but it is neither present in the static legacy formula as an operational object nor sufficient to generate the strict kernel. The smallest surviving bridge requires a phase/frequency transformation, a positive retention or dissipative map, an explicit state space and clock category, an environment or coarse-graining map, an information functional, and an operational sign reference.

All rates, times, actions, and couplings in the release are dimensionless. The work makes no claim of selector closure, dimensional physics, fundamental information destruction, experimental validation, cosmological interpretation, or a Theory of Everything.

## Main scientific contributions

1. **Loss-only phase no-go.** Positive pointwise retention preserves signs and cannot map the canonical legacy row to the strict row.
2. **Geometry-sensitive dissipation test.** A positive-semidefinite operator difference on the twelve-cycle becomes indefinite under interval extrapolation.
3. **Signed-kernel Markov obstruction.** The raw legacy candidate has negative nonconstant spectrum and produces negative entries at arbitrarily small tested positive time.
4. **Hazard reconstruction with nonuniqueness.** The envelope ratio admits an exact positive discrete hazard, but the representation does not supply a microscopic source theorem.
5. **Feedback stability boundary.** Exponential retention remains in the Laplacian cone, whereas linear subtraction is Markov-admissible only up to the exact finite threshold one sixth in the declared model.
6. **Information ledger.** An explicit isometric dilation demonstrates that local information decrease is compatible with globally pure evolution and system–environment correlation growth.
7. **Coupling-sign gauge theorem.** A parity-symmetric environment makes positive and negative couplings system-only equivalent; a calibrated parity-breaking record restores sign sensitivity.
8. **Nonvariational stable feedback.** Stability alone does not imply derivability from a scalar potential or ordinary single-path action.
9. **Wave–diffusion nonselection.** The common spatial operator supports both temporal categories, distinguished by short-time scaling and composition tests.
10. **Blinded synthetic challenge.** The frozen four-model protocol reports 85.4 percent overall accuracy and exposes a causal nonidentifiability that only environment records resolve.
