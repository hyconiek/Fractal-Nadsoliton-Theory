## Executive summary

The campaign completed all 128 planned tasks in the execution sense required by the master plan: every task now has a terminal disposition and evidence. This does **not** mean every conjecture was proved. Several global questions were deliberately closed as bounded partial certificates or explicit unresolved complements rather than promoted beyond the evidence.

The campaign also does not prove full phase-root exhaustion, full-seven-coordinate stationary exhaustion, a unique global minimizing orbit at g=4, or a physical active-gain/source/selector mechanism.
