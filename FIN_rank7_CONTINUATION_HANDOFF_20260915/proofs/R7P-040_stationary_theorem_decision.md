# R7P-040 — stationary-only theorem target decision

## Decision

The unrestricted statement `index(H7) <= 1 at every stationary point` is retired.
R7P-036 gives an interval-certified stationary index-2 point at exact gain g=5.
R7P-038 strengthens the mechanism: in the exact edge-reflection fixed subspace a
simple fold occurs near g=4.35221467865556 with full H7 inertia (1,1,5), and
at exact g=4.36 the two newborn fixed-subspace branches have full indices 1 and 2.
Thus a restricted minimum/saddle classification cannot be promoted to the full
seven-coordinate landscape without checking the odd reflection block.

## Narrow questions retained

1. A gain-restricted stationary statement in a declared neighborhood of the
   certified coexistence event remains viable. The current full-7D multistart
   reconnaissance found only indices 0 and 1 at g=3.72, 3.8, 4.0, 4.2 and 4.3,
   but this is numerical saturation, not exclusion.
2. The exact C4 localized event and barrier classifications remain valid locally.
3. A meaningful next theorem would require a bounded/exhaustive stationary cover
   on an explicit gain interval, not another unrestricted index-one conjecture.

## Non-repetition rule

Do not spend further proof effort on the universal stationary index-one theorem.
Use stationary searches only to delimit a precise gain/domain restriction or to
certify additional bifurcations/orbits.
