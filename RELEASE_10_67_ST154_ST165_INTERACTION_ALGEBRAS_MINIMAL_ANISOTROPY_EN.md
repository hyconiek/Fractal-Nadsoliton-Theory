## Abstract

Release 10.67 executes FIN research programs ST154–ST165. It studies whether observable algebras, symmetry-breaking dynamics, carrier-independent information, robust inference, and thermal resources can be derived without inserting their operational interpretation by hand.

ST154 proves that a finite-dimensional dephasing environment generates the joint commutant of its Hamiltonian and interaction observables as its fixed algebra. Monitoring the strict operator (A) leaves its 22-dimensional commutant. Monitoring a supplied nondegenerate vertex observable leaves a 12-dimensional diagonal pointer algebra. Monitoring both leaves only scalars. Thus an environment may select an algebra without naming that algebra as output, but the typed interaction remains indispensable input. Strict (A) alone does not generate the desired fine-blind algebra.

ST158 obtains a new exact structural theorem. On the normalized first Fourier mode of (C_{12}), every local even monomial below degree twelve is angularly isotropic. Degree twelve is the first local even term producing anisotropy:

\[
\sum_{j=0}^{11}\cos^{12}(\theta+2\pi j/12)
=\frac{693}{256}+\frac{3}{512}\cos(12\theta).
\]

The twelve-site geometry therefore fixes the first allowed anisotropy shape and its projection coefficient, but not the coupling (g), its sign, physical units, or dynamical source.

ST155 increases the proved fold-root lower bound from 29 to 31 locally unique roots and supplies larger root-free shells around them. ST159 covers a nuisance cube of halfwidth (5\times10^{-5}) by 64 strict interval cells. Every cell passes, proving that the earlier single-box failure near (1.46\times10^{-5}) was a certificate-method boundary rather than root loss.

The release also proves optimal recovery for finite Hilbert–Schmidt-orthogonal unitary errors, a sharp finite-time probability-preparation action, functorial coarse-graining of all-odd refinement cohomology, noiseless atomic-net reconstruction from complete labeled instrument tomography, a positive conservative exponent for a fully hidden non-erasure HMM, bounded-generator thermal-reset speed limits, a complete linear extremality criterion for the 66-dimensional minimax spectrahedron, and a relative axiom-deletion map for an eleven-atom operational FIN completion.

## Central conclusion

Two missing structures now have rigorous candidate mechanisms:

1. an observable algebra can arise as the fixed algebra of an environment interaction;
2. twelvefold anisotropy can arise from the first lattice-allowed local nonlinear term.

Neither mechanism is source-complete. The environment coupling and the degree-twelve coefficient are not generated by strict functional calculus. Selection still requires a realized branch, and dimensional physics still requires a scale and calibrated clock.

The best surviving interpretation is therefore a finite spectral-information core with explicitly typed operational completion. No strict environment coupling, QW-2191 discharge, strict anisotropy coefficient, dimensional unit, laboratory result, physical projection theorem, legacy-to-strict completion or role transfer, Standard Model, gravity, (L_{\rm total}), or Theory-of-Everything closure is claimed.
